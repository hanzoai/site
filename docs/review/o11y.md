# /o11y: James-eye review (independent critic, 7 Nov)

Verdict: 7/10
First complaint: "The dark window in the middle is half empty all the way down, and the lines in it don't even meet their labels. It looks unfinished."

Walked moving at 1470×801 (hover-capable pointer) and 375×812 (touch), cookie bar dismissed, with frames about 1 s apart on each screen, wheel scroll with a layout-shift observer, video recorded. `parity.py /o11y`: OK, text 58/58, links 12/12. `taste.py` on O11y.jsx/.css: OK. CLS while scrolling: 0.0005 on desktop and 0.0013 on phone (the suite clock's digits).

## The 11 answers

1. **Sells in 5 s, moves in 1 s?** Yes. The h1 sits at 52px on the left and the lede and "View on GitHub" sit on the right. The dark trace waterfall is the hero object. Its playhead is sweeping by 1.2 s, and span by span it opens the matching log lines, then rests on `db SELECT cart` after about 9 s. Hovering a span previews its logs, clicking holds it, and pause/replay works. This is a real "it does the thing" hero. One weak point: the suite's dark window peeks under it, so the first screen shows two near-identical `o11y.hanzo.ai` dark frames stacked.
2. **One in, one out?** Mostly. The pinned suite changes one pane per chapter while the text on the right swaps (01 to 06), with a progress rail. The OTLP room, the paste room, related cards and the close come one per screen after it. There is a lot of dead air after the paste room and under the close CTA.
3. **A real object in every section?** Yes in name. Waterfall, suite window, 4317/4318 figures with keep chips, console, prompt block, Sigil cards and Sigil close. But inside the suite, the Logs, Exceptions, Alerts and OTLP panes fill only the top third to half of a 400px-plus window, and the rest is empty black (see `img/o11y-empty-pane.jpg`). The console in the OTLP room is a deployment list (gateway / payments-api / checkout-web "Building" / docs-site). It is not an observability view, and the same `LaunchConsole` appears on other cloud pages.
4. **One ask, sticky, nothing floating; scale rhythm?** Yes. On desktop, the header swaps to a solid "View on GitHub" once the hero ask leaves view. The hero ask on the dark room is the one white fill. The close has one solid ask beside a text link. Scale has rhythm: h1 52, chapter titles 32, one 64-84px figure per screen (1,000, 4317/4318), and "Get O11y" grows at the close. On phone, the hero ask is outlined while the header "Try Hanzo" is solid (shared Ask.jsx behaviour, noted below).
5. **Hover and press answer, no jumps?** Yes, everywhere I tried. Hero ask: fill to #e4e4e7, press 0.97. Pause: fill. Span rows: fill plus ink, press 0.97, and the logs card swaps to that span. Suite tabs: fill, and they scroll to their chapter. Keep chips: ring to ink, press 0.97, and the answer object swaps. Nothing only fades. One flaw: a suite tab's hover fill (white 14%) is identical to the active pill, so with the pointer resting on one tab, two tabs look selected.
6. **Overlap, font swap, layout shift?** No overlap at either size. No font swap seen, and the h1 box is stable. CLS is negligible. Scrolling into the pinned section is clean: the hero window clears before the suite pins. The one visible defect is inside the intro pane. The six wire lines are stretched to the pane height (380px) while the six labels sit on a 324px grid, so the lines start 0, 14, 23, 33, 42 and 52px below their labels, and the last one ends under nothing (`img/o11y-wire.jpg`). The OTLP pane's two wires miss "OTel SDK"/"collector" the same way. On phone the bracket is squashed the same way.
7. **Which reference, is its motion there?** Apple product page: a pinned scene where one panel changes, with a sub-rail and a persistent ask. That motion is really there, scrubbed with the chapter text. The anthropic-launch feel of "figures as heroes" shows in 4317/4318 and the 1,000. There is no synchronized-style card-to-page open from the related cards beyond the shared one.
8. **Different from neighbours?** Yes. The signature (a request waterfall pivoting into its logs) belongs to this page alone. /metrics uses a feature-flag field, and the dark-window hero is not the family default. The only shared-looking parts are `PinnedChapters` (9 cloud pages) and the deploy console (3).
9. **18 parts covered?** Largely. Header comment with persona, goal, CTA, metric, signature and beyond; one h1 in server HTML; one `<main>`; skip link to `#main`; `data-cta`/`data-thumb` on the primary; JSON-LD; kit parts (LineMask, SettleStage, PinnedChapters, ClipWipe, SpecDrawer, LaunchClose); pause control on the self-running demo; reduced-motion final states in CSS. Not verified here: budgets, axe and JS-off. The page has no form, no table and no model card.
10. **Banned tells?** None. No hue, no gradient text, no badge above the h1 or h2s. The "RELATED CAPABILITIES" mono label is the h2 itself, not a kicker over one. No cursor label and no shadows on tiles. "02 / 03…" sit below the chapter titles as captions, which is allowed.
11. **Copy untouched?** Yes (parity 58/58, 12/12). The span names, log lines, query and "× 1,000" are illustrative: 1,000 renders Hanzo's own "a thousand".

## Fixes to reach 8

1. **Make the wires meet their labels.** In `O11y.css`, give `.ob-one-wire` the label grid's height (`align-self: start; height: calc(6 * 54px)`; 6 × 28px under 900px) and do the same for the OTLP pane wire against `.ob-src` (3 rows; put the two path starts at the centres of "OTel SDK" and "collector"). Each curve should leave from the vertical centre of its label, with no line under no label.
2. **Fill the suite window, or shorten it.** Logs, Exceptions, Alerts and OTLP use a third to half of the window, with 200-300px of empty black under them. Either size `.ob-panes` to the tallest pane's real content, or let each pane fill its stage. For example: Logs shows all five rows with the non-matching ones dimmed under the span_id filter. Exceptions keeps the collapsed stack and the linked trace bar from the hero. Alerts draws the saved query's line with its threshold above the routes. OTLP sets the endpoint variable line under the destination. All of this reuses words and objects already on the page.
3. **Replace the deployment list in the OTLP room** with an O11y view built from this page's own objects (the trace list, the log tail, the 1,000 row) or drop the console. A "Ready / Building" deploy list is not observability and repeats other cloud pages.
4. **Make suite-tab hover differ from active.** Use an ink colour change or a hairline on hover, and keep the filled pill only for the active tab, so one tab reads as selected.
5. **Separate the two dark frames on the first screen.** Let the suite enter in its intro state without the identical `o11y.hanzo.ai` bar visible under the hero (more peek distance, or the bar draws in on arrival), so the first screen holds one dark window, not two.
6. **Trim the dead air** under the paste room and under the close CTA, about one empty screen each at 1470×801, so the page ends on proof, Band and footer without gaps.

- A red "1 Issue" dev badge appeared on some loads. It is a hydration mismatch in the shared `FooterHTML` (`dangerouslySetInnerHTML` footer markup differs between server and client), not in O11y. It is intermittent and did not reproduce on 6 plain loads.
- On phone the header keeps a solid "Try Hanzo" while the page ask is outlined in the hero, and a bottom "View on GitHub" bar rises. This is Ask.jsx behaviour, so there are two different asks at different weights.
