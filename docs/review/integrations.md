# /integrations — James-eye review
Verdict: 6/10
First complaint: "I click LangChain and stare at a white page with one giant word for two seconds, and on my phone it's just blank and then a blurry smear. And the cards I'm clicking are nineteen empty grey boxes with two letters on them. Where are the logos? It's a list, not a catalogue."

Walked 7 Nov at 1470x801 and 375x812 with Playwright (deviceScaleFactor 1). I took 32 frames at 250 ms with no input, slow-scroll frames through the whole page, hover and press probes on the hero asks, orbit tiles, pause, code tabs, Copy, search, filter chips and cards, and two click-throughs into /integrations/langchain/ per viewport (the second one is judged). I also checked 10 plain loads, JS off and reduced motion. Frames taken during the coordinator's dev-server outage were retaken; the DevPage.jsx parse errors seen on the shared dev server belong to another page and are not counted.

1. **Sells in 5 s, moves within 1 s?** Mostly. The h1 "Works with everything" cuts in by line mask (frame at 250 ms shows it half-masked, settled by 500 ms). The 19-integration orbit is already rotating at 250 ms, and the black "19" disc at its centre is the focal figure, as section 4.3 asks. But the orbit is made of 19 grey 54px tiles reading "Co", "Cu", "Ds", "Ha", "Cr", "AG", "LI". These are initials, not logos, and a visitor cannot tell Cursor from Continue from CrewAI. The h1 sits low: 337-449px, so its baseline is at about 56% of the viewport against the 38% target, and the top 270px are empty. Nothing from the next section peeks above the 801px fold, because the hero runs to 829px.

2. **One in, one out, or a document?** A document. After the hero, the page scrolls past a code block, then a 1,874px grid of 19 cards that all arrive at once, then the black "Paste and ship" room. That room enters as an inset rounded card and opens to full width, the only scroll-authored moment. Nothing pins, scrubs or hands one idea to the next. Between the code block and the "Integrations" heading there is 407px of empty white on desktop (code block bottom 1239, h2 top 1646) and 182px on phone.

3. **A real visual object in every section?** No. The hero has the orbit and the second section has the code block (good: Python/TypeScript/Go tabs that rotate on their own with a pause button). The catalogue, which is the page's whole job, is grey #ededed boxes: title, an "NN / 19" counter, a category label, a hairline and an arrow. Eleven of the 19 cards have no description and are about 60% empty space. There is no logo, image or art in any card. The destination pages draw contour art on their cards, so the index looks poorer than its own children. The phone list shows the same two-letter monograms in white squares.

4. **One ask, in the header, nothing floating; scale rhythm?** Not yet. On phone the header "Try Hanzo" (solid black) and the hero "Get an API key" (solid black, full width) share the first screen permanently: two solid fills with different words. On desktop the header starts solid "Try Hanzo" beside the hero's solid ask, then turns outline after about 1 s, and after scrolling it swaps label to "Get an API key" (good). In the "Paste and ship" room the header's solid black "Get an API key" and the room's solid white "Get an API key" are both on screen. On phone the header never swaps to the page's ask. Nothing floats over content except the shared cookie card. Scale has some rhythm: h1 52px, the 19 figure, 26px h2s, 22px card titles. But the grid is one size repeated 19 times, with nothing that grows or settles.

5. **Every hover and press answers, no jumps?** Partly. Hero ask: hover lifts 1px and lightens, press scales to 0.97. Good. SDK docs: the arrow slides 4px. Orbit tile hover is the best detail on the page: the tile goes ink, the orbit stops, and the centre disc swaps "19" for "OpenAI Python SDK / AI SDKs". Filter chips have a gliding pill (xii-chip-bg). Search filters live ("lang" leaves 5 cards) and has a designed empty state ("Nothing matches "zzzz"." with an All 19 reset). Card hover only darkens the fill slightly and fills the arrow chip black. It tells you nothing: no preview and no description on the 11 bare cards. Card press scales to 0.97 (good). **Copy** and **Copy the agent prompt** give no visible answer: the label, aria-label and fill stay unchanged for 900 ms after the click, though the clipboard does receive exactly the displayed code.

6. **Overlaps, font swap, layout shift?** Clean on load. CLS is 0.0004 on desktop (header tools 0.0002 twice, shared chrome) and 0 on phone. There is no horizontal page scroll at 375 (scrollWidth 375). The filter chips run off-screen as a rail with a peek, which is fine. The search field does not stick under the header, and focused cards stay clear of the 60px header plus the 57px "INTEGRATIONS 19" local bar. Plain loads gave 0 console errors on 5 of 5 at each size. But when hydration is slowed (frame capture during load, the same as a busy phone), the page throws a **hydration attribute mismatch on its own search input** (`label.xii-find > input`) 3 of 3 times and the red "1 Issue" badge appears. On phone the Python code block wraps `base_url="…/v1"` so the trailing comma sits alone on its own line, and the Copy button drops to a second row.

7. **Which reference, and is its motion there?** It wants to be synchronized.studio, with the card opening into its page. On the second click (route already compiled) at 1470 it fails that: the grid vanishes and a white stage holds a 90px "LangChain" at y≈630 from about 1.0 s to about 3.0 s. Only then does the title jump up and cross-fade into "Use Hanzo AI with LangChain", which is roughly 2 s of hold against the 700 ms cap. On phone it is worse: about 2.2 s of fully blank white, then an oversized blurred "LangChain" washes over the destination text (proof 2). This is the **shared Morph/transition engine** (x-veil), but this page is where a visitor meets it. Back restores the scroll position on desktop (1696px) but drops to the top on phone. No apple.com pinning and no osply-style editorial grid.

8. **Different from its neighbours?** Yes, in hero medium. A rotating orbit around a count disc is its own idea, and the black-disc figure is distinctive. The grid, however, is the generic grey-tile catalogue that could sit on any page.

9. **All 18 parts covered?** No:
   - No proof strip before the closing room.
   - The hero next-section peek is missing.
   - Morph misses its timing and its 12px landing on phone.
   - The copy-success state is missing.
   - The hydration error shows under load.
   - The numbering reads out of order on desktop: 16 Python sits under 17 TypeScript.
   - Covered and done well: JS off is legible at both sizes (card descriptions are hover-hidden on desktop by design), reduced motion leaves 0 running animations and freezes the orbit, both moving things have pause controls, and the phone catalogue uses 72px rows.

10. **Banned tells?** No hue, no gradient text, no kicker above the h1 (the "INTEGRATIONS 19" bar is a sticky local nav, not a kicker), no Inter in use. The "3 equal boxes" pattern repeats down the grid: the 8 bare cards in the 3-wide rows are equal grey boxes with nothing in them. That is the "plain boxes, lazy look" James rejects. The destination page carries 12 screen-reader-only emoji spans (🐍 🤖 🤗 🐳 ⚓ 🟦) in its "More integrations" list. They are invisible but read aloud ("snake", "whale"), and they belong to the family page, not this one. Shared chrome: a grey smear band appears behind the header nav on white sections once scrolled (proof 3).

11. **Copy untouched?** Yes: `scripts/parity.py /integrations` reports text 71/71 and links 21/21.

## Fixes
1. **Give every card a real object.** Mirror each integration's real logo or mark into `public/` (greyscale, 28-32px) and use it in the orbit tiles, the grid cards and the phone rows in place of "Co"/"Cu"/"Ds". On the 11 bare cards, show the existing description (or a 2-line code preview from the destination page) on hover and focus, so no card is more than 30% empty. Done when 19 of 19 cards show a mark and hover reveals a detail.
2. **Card-to-page open (shared Morph, request to the owner).** On a compiled route, the old page must never be blank for more than 250 ms, the total hold must be 700 ms or less, the title must land within 12px of the destination h1, and it must never scale or blur over body text on phone. Back must restore scroll on phone as it already does on desktop.
3. **One solid fill per viewport.** On phone, make the header ask quiet (outline) while the hero "Get an API key" is in view, and swap its label to "Get an API key" as on desktop. In "Paste and ship", turn the header ask to outline while the room's white solid ask is visible. Done when a script counting solid #0a0a0a/#fff asks in the viewport reads 1 at every scroll step at both sizes.
4. **Remove the dead gap and make the catalogue arrive.** Cut the 407px gap between the code block and "Integrations" to 116px or less (72px or less on phone). Give the grid a list-level entrance or a "one in, one out" handoff from the code block, so scrolling feels authored rather than like a document.
5. **Copy answers.** Both Copy buttons must show "Copied" (or a tick) within 150 ms, announce it via `role="status"`, revert after about 1.5 s, and play the shared confirm chime.
6. **Fix the hydration mismatch on the search input** (`label.xii-find > input`). Do not let anything set attributes on it before hydration. Done when 0 of 5 loads show an error, including loads with frame capture during hydration.
7. **Hero composition.** Raise the h1 so its baseline sits near 38% of 100svh (about 304px at 801) and let the next section peek 56px above the fold. On phone, fit the orbit fully above or below the fold instead of cutting it at 812, and keep its pause control clear of the cookie card.
8. **Phone code block.** Use `white-space: pre` with a horizontal scroll region (`tabindex="0"`) so `base_url=…,` never wraps a lone comma, and keep Copy on the tab row.
9. **Grid reading order.** Make the "NN / 19" numbers read in order left to right, top to bottom (16 currently sits under 17).
10. **Shared chrome (request):** remove the grey smear band behind the header nav on white sections, and drop the screen-reader-only emoji from the family page's "More integrations" list.
