# /platform — James-eye review
Verdict: 7/10
First complaint: "When I scroll through the planes, the cards crash into each other. Half of one panel sits on top of the next and the heading on the left has gone. That's not premium, it looks broken. And on my phone there are two black buttons fighting at the top."

Walked 7 Nov 2026 with Playwright at 1470x801 and 375x812, deviceScaleFactor 1. I took 32 frames at 250 ms with no input, about 43 desktop and 57 phone frames while wheel-scrolling 120 px every 110 ms, and 12 fine frames through the plane switches. I also probed hover and press, did 10 plain fresh loads (5 normal, 5 reduced motion), loaded with JS off and checked the page at 320 px wide.

Measured: page length 13,933 px = **17.4 screens** at 1470x801, and 19,227 px = **23.7 screens** at 375x812 (JS off: 27.7 / 26.3 screens). CLS 0.0005 desktop and 0.0066 phone. No horizontal overflow at 1470, 375 or 320. Console errors: 0 in 10 plain loads (5 normal, 5 reduced). The 2 walk loads, which screenshot from first commit, each logged one React hydration-mismatch error in the shared `FooterHTML` (`dangerouslySetInnerHTML` footer markup differs server vs client). The red Next "1 Issue" badge showed in every frame of those runs. That is shared chrome, but it is intermittent and a visitor in dev sees it.

## The 11 questions

**1. First screen sells in 5 s; movement within 1 s?** Yes on desktop. The h1, the lede, a filled "Start building", a text-weight "Inspect the stack" and the docker strip all sit in the first screen. The isometric stack is live by 250 ms and starts dealing its layers out one by one (Gateway, AI Gateway, Enso, Zen, Kai, Agents...) from about 1 s, settling at about 4.5 s. That is idle motion with meaning. On the phone the art slot is empty at t0 and t250 (a 140 px white gap between the asks and the support line) and the stack only lands at about 500 ms. The support line "Kubernetes-native..." is pushed below the art, so the hero copy is split in two.

**2. One in, one out, or a document?** For the first half it is genuinely staged. The hero hands off to a black room that zooms into the stack, which explodes and lights 01-11 in groups (API, MIND, ACT, RUN, SILICON) while the left list and the figures (95.6%, 3.02x) change. Then "Explore the platform" and the pinned planes scene (01 Compute ... 06 Pricing) swap one panel at a time. After "Intelligence that connects" it becomes a document: API, Loop, Open and Proofs scroll past as ordinary sections. 17.4 screens on desktop and 23.7 on the phone is long. On the phone the room and the planes are a plain stacked list, so it reads as a document.

**3. A real visual object in every section?** Yes, nearly all: the stack, the directory with its contour preview card, the API Platform window, data tiles, the control-plane tree, the ship timeline, pricing meters, the semantic graph, the endpoint panel that types `api.hanzo.ai/v1/models`, the loop diagram, and terminal strips. "11 native layers" / "4 GPU backends" is figures only, which is acceptable. This is the page's strength.

**4. One ask in the header, nothing floating; scale rhythm?** The ask lives in the header and is page-aware: it reads "Try Hanzo", then "Start building" once you pass the hero, filled over light rooms and inverted white over the black room. But in the first second of a desktop load the header "Try Hanzo" is filled black next to the filled hero "Start building", then it drops to outline: a visible flip. On the phone both stay filled (header "Try Hanzo" black, hero "Start building" black), so there are **two solid fills in one viewport**. The shared cookie card floats over content on every screen until dismissed. It covers the planes tab index (clicking "04 Security" at 500,731 hits the cookie text) and covers the stack room's Protocol/Source bar. Scale has rhythm: an 84 px h1 against a quiet 13 px eyebrow, a 44 px room head, 32 px plane heads, 64 px-class figures, and the 88 px Band.

**5. Every hover and press answers, no jumps?** Mostly. Hero primary: hover changes bg #0a0a0a to #2c2c2e, press scale 0.98. Secondary: hover gives a 10% grey fill, press 0.98. Directory rows: hover fills the row white, slides in an arrow, swaps the right preview card, and press scales 0.97. Stack rows open a real Layer Inspector sheet (01/11, Product/Docs/Source, arrows), which is excellent. Copy button: press 0.92. Dead: "The complete catalog" link (no hover change, only a 3 px press shrink) and the runtime rows ("Functions/Containers/Sandboxes": no hover, no press). Shared chrome: the header ask's hover is **opacity 0.88 only** (a banned fade-on-hover) and its press has no transform.

**6. Overlaps, font swap, layout shift?** No font swap, and CLS is negligible. Two overlaps, though. (a) **Pinned planes switch:** mid-switch, the incoming panel slides over the outgoing one, so "Your cloud account / Your cluster" rows sit on the Datastore tiles. The left column is empty or ghosted at 20% while the right shows two panels at once. It repeats at every switch: Data to Anywhere, Anywhere to Security (Control plane card over the "org it acts for" text), Security to Ship. See proof 1. (b) The shared cookie card sits on the planes tab index and the room's bottom bar. Under reduced motion the hero breaks: the stack drops below the copy, the right half of the first screen is empty, and only the top slab peeks out at y≈610.

**7. Which reference, and is its motion there?** Apple product page: a pinned, scrubbed stack scene where one panel changes, with a persistent ask. That is present and is the best thing on the site so far. There is a touch of synchronized.studio in the inspector sheet rising from a row. The Apple polish is missing in the plane switches: Apple cross-fades whole panels and never lets two collide.

**8. Different from neighbours?** Yes. Its own medium (an isometric layered stack you zoom into, then a directory) is unmistakably /platform and fits the brief's "a system map you zoom into". Its signature (row hover reveals the description) is there.

**9. All 18 parts covered?** Largely. There are states (inspector, tabs, copy confirm), the API panel, proof figures with links, a Band, and the JS-off page is fully legible (0 hidden headings or paragraphs). Gaps: part 11 (the phone hero splits its copy around the art; the phone gets no pinned moments, so it is a 23.7-screen list), part 12 (the reduced-motion hero layout breaks; the Band headline still blurs in under reduced motion, which is shared chrome) and part 17 (the intermittent hydration error in the shared footer).

**10. Banned tells?** None on the page itself: no hue, no gradient text, no eyebrow above a headline (HANZO PLATFORM and THE NATIVE AI STACK sit under their heads), no three equal boxes. Shared chrome has one: header ask hover = opacity fade. The Band reveal uses blur on words, and the brand deck says no blur. That is the shared Band.

**11. Copy untouched?** As far as a visitor can see, yes. Hanzo's labels (Start building, Inspect the stack, Inspect every layer, The complete catalog) and figures (95.6% on GPQA Diamond, 3.02x, 2.0x, 11, 4) read as live copy, and there is no invented sales ask. The header label switching from "Try Hanzo" to "Start building" uses Hanzo's own words.

## Fixes (by impact)

1. **Plane switches must never show two panels.** Through 01 to 06, at every 100 px wheel step, exactly one panel's text may be readable (opacity above 0.15). The outgoing panel must be gone (opacity 0, or clipped) before the incoming one passes its top edge. The left heading must cross-fade with no frame where both left headings are under 0.5 (no empty left column). Re-film at 90 ms steps from scrollY 5,600 to 6,800 and get 0 overlapping text boxes.
2. **One solid fill per viewport on the phone.** At 375x812 the header ask must be outline (or hidden) while the hero "Start building" is on screen, as desktop does. Remove the desktop first-second flip too: the header ask must start in its outline state at t0 and not be filled for the first 750 ms.
3. **Fix the reduced-motion hero.** With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` at 1470x801, the stack must sit in the right column at its final settled frame, fully inside the first screen (top under 200 px, bottom under 760 px), the same as the motion layout at 4.5 s.
4. **Phone hero art in place from t0.** At 375x812 the stack art must be visible in the first frame (no empty 140 px slot at t0 to t250), and the support line must stay directly under the lede (lede, support line, asks, then art). The art must never appear between copy lines.
5. **Keep page controls out from under the cookie card,** or have the shared card avoid them. The planes tab index (y≈731) and the room's Protocol/Source bar must be clickable with the card open: `elementFromPoint` at each tab centre must return the tab. Lift the index to bottom ≥ 110 px, or request a shared change so the card docks bottom-left at 360 px wide on desktop.
6. **Wake the dead controls.** "The complete catalog" and the runtime rows (Functions, Containers, Sandboxes, Machines, GPUs, Kubernetes) need a hover change other than opacity (row fill or arrow nudge 4 px, 150 ms ease-out) and a press of scale 0.98 or translateY(1px).
7. **Shorten the back half.** It runs 17.4 screens on desktop and 23.7 on the phone. The sections after the planes (API, Loop, Open, Proofs) should arrive one at a time, pinned or stacked, so the desktop reads in 14 screens or fewer, and the phone gets the 3 static room panels the brief allows rather than 11 list rows plus thumbnails (under 19 screens).
8. **Shared chrome (request, do not fork):** (a) the header ask hover must change fill (e.g. #0a0a0a to #262626) instead of opacity 0.88, and press must be scale 0.98; (b) the header over the black room renders as a muddy translucent grey band with a light 6-15 px strip above it, so make it solid #000 with a white 10% hairline; (c) fix the `FooterHTML` hydration mismatch so that 0 of 10 loads that screenshot from commit show the "1 Issue" badge; (d) the Band headline must not blur in, and must show its final state under reduced motion.
9. **Directory reading order.** In the masonry, the group heads read 01, 02, 04, 06, then 05 Observe (higher) before 03 Data (lower). Align the second-row group heads to one baseline, or order the columns 01/05, 02/06, 03/07, 04, so that tops in a row differ by 0 px and the numbers read in order. See proof 3.
