# /platform — James-eye review
Verdict: 7/10
First complaint: "When the stack changes layer the words pile on top of each other for a moment. Two headlines, two lists, one on top of the other. That looks broken, not premium."

Walked moving on 7 Nov 2026 with Playwright at 1470x801 and 375x812, deviceScaleFactor 1: 250 ms frames for the first 8 s with no input, then slow wheel scrolling (120 px steps every 110 ms) through the whole page, a second phone pass in 700 px steps, hover and press probes on 28 controls, 22 fresh loads in total (with and without reduced motion), JS off, and a 320 px check. Page length: 13,896 px = **17.3 screens** at 1470x801 and 19,227 px = **23.7 screens** at 375x812. Horizontal overflow: none at 1470, 375 or 320. CLS: 0.0002 on load, 0.0007 after the full desktop scroll and 0.0073 after the phone scroll (the load-time shifts come from the shared header's right cluster).

## The 11 answers

**1. Does the first screen sell in 5 seconds, and does something move within 1 second?** It sells: "The AI-native cloud.", one lede, Start building, Inspect the stack and a docker line with Copy, all clear of the header at both sizes. But the right half of the desktop hero is **empty white until about 1.3 s**: frames at 750 ms and 976 ms are identical and blank, and the isometric stack lands between 1.0 and 1.4 s. After that it is alive: a layer slides out and labels itself (Enso, Sandboxes, ...) on a loop. On the phone the hero leaves a **~300 px blank hole** between the ask and the lede until the art arrives at about 1.5 s. So it narrowly misses "something moves within 1 s".

**2. One thing in, one thing out, or a document?** On desktop, mostly in and out. The architecture map pins for about 4.8 screens and steps the stack through API, Mind, Act, Run and Silicon, one group lit at a time with its figure (95.6%, 3.02x). The planes section pins and swaps Compute, Data, Anywhere, Security, Ship and Pricing. That is the right idea. The **handoffs are not clean**: in both pinned scenes the outgoing and incoming text cross-fade on top of each other ("Hanzo Cloud" over "Hanzo ML", "Push code. Get a production system." over "Pay for what runs."), so for about 600 px of scroll the screen shows two layouts at once. On the phone it becomes a document: the stack turns into a long list with a small striped icon. After the planes, the reach tabs, API demo, loop, open and proofs sections read as a normal scroll document, with big empty gaps (about 250 px of white between the loop and the open section).

**3. Does every section hold a real visual object?** Yes, and this is the page's strength: the isometric stack, a directory with a contour-map preview card, an "API Platform — Hanzo" console window with service states (Ready / Building), a control-plane diagram, meter cards, a connection graph for Semantic + Graph, a self-typing GET api.hanzo.ai/v1/models demo with pause, the recursive-improvement ring with pause, and terminal blocks. The proofs section (11 native layers, 4 GPU backends, figures) is the thinnest: a large figure on the left over about 300 px of empty column.

**4. One ask in the sticky header, nothing floating; scale rhythm?** There is one solid header ask, but it **changes its words while you scroll**: "Try Hanzo" (filled) at the top, then "Try Hanzo" (outlined) once the hero's own Start building is in view, then "Start building" (white) from the stack onward. A System page's ask is the sales ask (MASTER 1 and 4.2); here the hero, header and Band all ask "Start building", and Contact sales lives only in the footer. Nothing of the page's own floats over content. The shared **cookie card sits over the bottom of every screen** and hides the architecture map's ZAP / native-interconnect bar and Protocol / Source buttons during the whole pinned scene, and at 375 it covers about 27% of the screen. Scale has rhythm: the 84 px h1 is the one big thing in the hero; on the stack, 26 px rows sit with a 64 px figure; type then settles to medium. Good.

**5. Does every hover and press answer, with no jumps?** Press answers almost everywhere (scale 0.97 to 0.98 on buttons, header and links). Hover is uneven. The hero primary darkens (#0a0a0a to #2c2c2e), Copy gets a fill, loop steps fill #f5f5f5, inactive reach tabs darken to ink, and directory rows get a white fill, an arrow and a preview in the side card. But in the computed style, the 01 AI / 02 Compute heads, the text links (Compute API, Every capability, Research, Quickstart, Open source) and the "API Platform — Hanzo" window link showed **no hover change** (if they have a pseudo-element underline, it did not register). The directory preview **changes height** with the row: GPUs shows a three-line description and the card is 686 px tall; Containers, IAM and Nodes show a title only and the card shrinks to about 610 px, a 76 px jump on every hover. The "Find in the stack" search is excellent: typing "zen" gives a 2 / 11 count, highlighted matches and lights the matching layers.

**6. Overlaps, font swap or layout shift?** No font swap (the h1 box holds) and CLS is negligible. Overlaps: (a) the cross-fade doubling above; (b) during the pinned stack the top layers slide **under the "Find in the stack" field** and fade there; (c) during wheel scrolling over the black room the sticky header shows a **white 6 to 17 px sliver above it**, and the header itself turns translucent grey over black (the black room's top edge sits under the header); (d) the console window's last chip ("Marketplace") touches the window's right edge at 1470; (e) the shared cookie card covers page controls. A **hydration-mismatch console error** (input `caret-color` attribute and the footer HTML) appeared in 2 of 22 fresh loads: both slow, screenshot-heavy walks at 1470 and 375, 0 of 20 quick loads (10 with reduced motion). In dev it shows the red "1 Issue" badge. Nothing came from the server outage window.

**7. Which reference, and is its motion there?** apple.com product page, clearly: a product-as-hero object, a pinned scrubbed scene where one panel changes, figures as moments, and a sub-ask in the stack ("Inspect every layer"). The motion is genuinely there: scroll-scrubbed layer selection, an idle loop in the hero, typed API demo, a ring that advances by itself with a pause, and search. Apple never shows two states at once, though; its scene cuts are clean. The chatgpt-enterprise calm is missing from the proofs tail.

**8. Different from its neighbours, with its own medium and signature?** Yes. Its medium is the isometric 11-layer stack and its signature is "a map you zoom into", with the directory hover preview as the brief's named signature. Nothing else on the site looks like it.

**9. Are all 18 parts covered?** Visibly covered: strategy (one primary), first impression, trust and proof (figures with source links and honest caveats such as "never blanket speed claims"), the component kit, motion, media (SVG and UI), and states (Ready / Building, pause controls, search empty and count). Weak or missing: the conversion system for a System page (no sales ask in the hero, header or Band); mobile (pinned scenes become a 24-screen document, and the phone directory orphans 04 Dev in a half-empty row); accessibility under reduced motion (the stack becomes a 1,360 px wide list with arrows at the far right edge, and the stack art is gone); and resilience (the intermittent hydration error). JS off: legible, but the hero art is missing, so the right half is blank.

**10. Any banned tells?** No hue, no gradient text, no Inter, no kicker above a headline (HANZO PLATFORM and THE NATIVE AI STACK sit below their headings), no three equal boxes. Borderline: the **white halo glow** around the lit layers in the black room is close to the banned "glow" look. "Everything big at once" is avoided. The shared header's grey translucent band over the black room reads as low-contrast chrome.

**11. Is the copy untouched?** As far as a visitor can tell, yes: Hanzo's own wording throughout, including the cautious caveats. The header ask's label switch (Try Hanzo to Start building) uses two existing labels, but swapping them in one slot is a design choice James would question.

## Fixes (by impact)
1. **Clean the handoffs in both pinned scenes.** Never show two text states at once: the outgoing group fully gone (opacity 0, out by 150 ms) before the incoming one enters (ease-emphasis, 300 ms, 16 px rise), so that 0 frames in a slow wheel walk show doubled text in the architecture map or the planes stage.
2. **Something moves within 1 s, and no holes.** Draw the hero stack's first layers in by about 400 ms, or reserve the stack in server HTML as a static SVG that animates on hydrate. That removes the blank right half on desktop, the blank hero art with JS off, and the ~300 px hole on the phone hero (the 976 ms frame must already show the stack).
3. **Fix the hydration mismatch.** Do not set `caret-color` (or any style) on the search input before hydration. The footer HTML mismatch is shared chrome. Target: 0 console errors in 10 slow (screenshot-every-250-ms) loads at both sizes.
4. **Settle the header over the black room.** No white sliver above the header during wheel scroll (0 px gap in every frame), and an opaque #000 or #171717 header over black instead of translucent grey (shared chrome, triggered by this page's room). Keep one ask label for the whole page; for a System page it should be the sales ask (MASTER 4.2) with Start building as the text secondary, and the Band should use the same ask.
5. **Fixed-height directory preview.** Lock the preview card to one height (the GPUs height, 686 px) and give every row a one-line description or a figure, so hovering any of the 37 rows moves nothing. Re-flow the columns so no column ends 250 px above its neighbours: either 4 even columns of about 9 rows, or AI with Observe below it. On the phone, keep the 01 to 07 order with no half-empty Dev row.
6. **Keep page controls clear of the shared cookie card.** Lift the architecture map's interconnect bar above the card's top edge (at least 16 px clear) while the card is open, or have the card collapse to a slim bar after 4 s (shared chrome, request it). At 375 the card should cover no more than 15% of the screen.
7. **Hover on every link and head.** The 01 AI to 07 Network heads, the API-link rows (Compute API, Function API, ...) and "API Platform — Hanzo" need a visible non-opacity hover (a 1 px underline drawing in over 150 ms, or the arrow moving 4 px) with no layout change.
8. **Shorten and tighten the tail.** Desktop is 17.3 screens and the phone 23.7. Cut empty space between the loop, open and proofs sections (gaps of 250 px or more down to the 72 to 116 px section rhythm), and give proofs one object instead of a lone figure over an empty column. Target: 15 screens or fewer on desktop and 20 or fewer on the phone.
9. **Reduced motion keeps the drawing.** Show the stack art as a static final frame beside the list, with the list at most 768 px wide, not 1,360 px rows with arrows at the far right edge.
10. **Tone down the halo** around lit layers to the deck's floating shadow (black 75% on black, no white bloom), so it cannot read as a glow.
