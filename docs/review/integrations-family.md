## Fixes (ordered by impact)

1. **Fill the pinned stage.** At 1470x801 the active panel should occupy at least 60% of the stage height (now about 46%: 370 of 801), and the empty band under the progress dashes should drop from about 250 px to 72 px or less. Give each chapter a moment of its own: the panel grows from rest to its moment size, or its Hanzo lines highlight in sequence as the scroll scrubs. A crossfade alone is not enough. Target at least 350 px of scroll per chapter with a visible scrubbed change in at least 2 of every 3 frames.
2. **Kill the footer hydration error (shared chrome, `components/Shell.jsx:12` FooterHTML).** 0 console errors and no "1 Issue" badge on 5 of 5 painted loads per member.
3. **One solid fill per viewport on phone (shared header).** On integration pages the phone header ask should be quiet or show the page's "Get API key — Free" only after the hero ask leaves view. Count of solid buttons on the first 375x812 screen: 1. Same at the closing band on desktop: header ask quiet while the band ask is in view.
4. **Cookie card off the hero object (shared chrome).** At 1470x801 the card must not intersect the hero code or diagram rect: dock it at most 360 px wide in a corner, or delay it until the first scroll. At 375x812 it must be 160 px tall or less (now 203) and must not cover the primary or the first code lines.
5. **Phone CLS under 0.02.** Reserve the final wrapped height of the typed lines before typing starts. Docker is now 0.024 and openai-sdk 0.019; both should be under 0.005.
6. **Card-to-page transition (shared engine).** Hold 700 ms or less in total. The flying title should land within 12 px of the destination h1 at its 52 px size, not as a roughly 100 px title on a blank page.
7. **Phone code windows.** Remove the duplicate label (title "Dockerfile" plus a "DOCKERFILE" tag stacked on a second row): one label, one row, 56 px or less. Add 16 px right padding and an edge fade on horizontally scrolling code so lines aren't cut flush at the card edge.
8. **Hover that answers everywhere.** Chapter dots and the inline links ("View source on GitHub →", "platform.hanzo.ai/api-keys") need a visible non-opacity hover (ink, underline offset or fill). The header "Try Hanzo" hover must change fill, not opacity 0.88 (shared).
9. **Idle life after typing on docker.** Something should keep living after 3.5 s (the route chips cycling as on openai-sdk, or a slow Sigil drift), and stop under reduced motion.
10. **Bottom third.** Make the "More integrations" cards differ by each integration's own mark or seed instead of four identical contour tiles, and give the 12-row link list a hover preview (category or one-line description) so it isn't a plain list.
