Verdict: 7.5/10
First complaint: "The demo's good, but I'm reading the same six lines twice: they're tabs under the hero, then they're the chapters again. And I can't see the tabs and the screen together without scrolling."

Walked 2026-11-07 on the dev server at 1470×801 and 375×812, moving. I recorded video while wheel-scrolling in 130px notches with a 1.2s dwell every 6th notch. I sampled frames at 250 to 400ms intervals in the first 1.6s. I hovered and pressed the hero ask, Documentation and the copy button. I clicked 4 hero tabs, the 01/02/03 chapter switcher, all 3 checklist rows and the copy button, and used the pause/play control. I also did a Tab crawl, pressed Esc, emulated reduced motion, and tapped through every phone screen. I checked overflow, target size and text size by script. Proof: `docs/review/computer/desktop.jpg` and `docs/review/computer/phone.jpg`.

## What works
- **The hero object is alive and is the product.** A scripted desktop (the "vendor portal" with its CSV rows, and "the desktop tool from 2009") starts working within 400ms with no input. Rows fill, ticks land, OCR boxes draw, and the step counter goes 01 → 02 → 03 / 06 over about 5s. Set-of-Mark numbers 1 to 6 pop onto the elements. This is "a live demo that runs itself", done properly, and the pause control (44px, `aria-pressed` flips false → true, label Pause → Play) sits first.
- **The h1 cuts in by line mask.** At 0ms it is clipped mid-line, and by 800ms it has settled at 52px/497. Nothing sits above it. COMPUTER.HANZO.AI sits *under* the asks as a 12px mono caption, which is allowed.
- **A pinned chapter stage where one panel changes.** It runs 01 "It reads pixels" (grid of native app / canvas / remote desktop / web page) → 02 "OCR when clicking is hard" (the room shifts to `#f5f5f5`, and the OCR boxes draw around form fields with -with-ocr/-with-som chips) → 03 "Mouse and keyboard" (the permission toggles flip on as you arrive, and the voice waveform moves). The 01/02/03 switcher also works by click. This is the apple.com contribution, and it is really there.
- **The checklist is a real "Beyond" aid.** Clicking each row fills a 48px ink tick, and the big tally goes 0 → 1 → 3 / 3 with a bar drawing across. At 3/3 the caption becomes "Start with: hanzo desktop". Every tap answers.
- **Copy works and copies exactly what is displayed.** The clipboard read back "Read https://hanzo.ai/skill.md and use Hanzo Computer in my project. Start with: hanzo desktop", and the button became "✓ Copied". The caret blinks in the prompt and the terminal cursor blinks.
- **Hero ask press is right.** Hover fills `#f5f5f7` → `#d4d4d4`, and press scales to 0.98 (matrix 0.98) with no layout jump.
- **Mix of motion kinds:** idle (demo, waveform, carets), state (tabs, chapter switch, toggles, checklist), arrival (h1 cut-in), and scroll (pinned chapters, the header progress hairline). Scroll is clearly under half.
- **Reduced motion is handled.** It shows the final state (all 5 CSV rows ticked) and `getAnimations()` is empty after 3.5s.
- **CLS 0.0007.** No horizontal scroll at 375 (scrollWidth 375). No main tap target under 44px. Phone h1 is 40px at y 80 to 127, and the primary sits at y 212 to 256, well above the fold.
- **Parity:** `/computer: OK text 33/33 links 4/4`.

## Fix list (to reach 8+), most important first
1. **Stop showing the same six lines twice.** The hero stage's tab strip ("It reads pixels · Mouse and keyboard · OCR when clicking is hard · Set-of-Mark prompting · Say it out loud · It has whatever you have") repeats the chapter titles below word for word. Let the hero tabs be the *only* index and have each chapter page anchor to it. Or keep the strip as the step counter and drop the visible labels in favour of the 01 / 06 caption, with the labels kept accessible. Either way, the visitor should read each line once.
2. **Fit the hero stage into the first screen at 1470×801.** The stage runs from y 254 to about 1050, so the tab strip and the dock are about 190px below the fold. You can't watch the screen change and see which tab is lit at the same time. Clicking a tab while the strip is in view leaves the top of the scene (where the change happens) under the header. Shorten the stage to about 520px of scene, or crop the mock at true size, so h1, ask, scene and tab strip all sit within 801px.
3. **Close the dead gaps.** These are the ~158px black tail of the hero room plus ~130px of white before chapter 01, and the ~190px gap in the chapter column between the caption and the 01/02/03 switcher. Pull the switcher up under the caption, or let the hero room hand straight into the chapter stage (object overlap of 40px per part 3).
4. **Give "Copy the agent prompt" a hover.** Today the hover is white → white with no transform. Use a fill step to `#f5f5f5` or an icon nudge, and the 0.97 press. Check that the "Documentation" link has a hover that changes more than its arrow.
5. **Fix the heading order and the scale inversion.** Chapter titles are h3 at 32px, coming before any h2, while "It has whatever you have" is an h2 at 26px. Use an h2 (via `as`) for the chapter titles, or give the chapter stage an h2, and keep the h2 visually at least as large as the h3s.
6. **Don't give a zero hero size.** The tally reads "0 / 3" at about 64px before any tick. Start it at its quiet size and let it grow as ticks land, which is the "starts small, grows as you act" rhythm James asked for. Or show the zero at caption scale.
7. **Token drift.** The hero and band asks use `#f5f5f7` / `rgb(10,10,11)`, and the transition uses `cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)`. Move them to the deck's `#f5f5f5`/`#fafafa`, `#0a0a0a` and `ease-out`/`ease-emphasis`.
8. **Confirm the hydration error is gone (see Shared chrome).** On a clean load the page must show no red "1 Issue" badge.
