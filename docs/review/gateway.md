# /gateway — James-eye review
7 Nov, round 2, walked at 1470x801 and 375x812 (isMobile, hasTouch) with Playwright: frames at 0, 1 and 3 s with no input, wheel or smooth scroll a screen at a time with 1.5 s dwell (15 desktop steps, 17 phone), hover and press on every main control on desktop, tap on phone, overflow at 375 and 320, prefers-reduced-motion, plus /dns and /cloud/service-mesh heroes for comparison.

Verdict: 7/10

First complaint: "Six chapters and it's the same white box six times: a big grey word and some grey pills. On the phone it's just six of those boxes stacked one under the other. That's a document with a frame round it. Make each one *do* the thing it's talking about."

## The 11 answers
1. **First screen, motion within 1 s:** Yes. The h1 cuts into its line, the lede and asks rise, and the request card starts on its own: `X-Org-Id: anything` gets stripped, `JWKS ✓` lands and `X-User-Id ← sub / X-Org-Id ← owner / X-Roles ← roles` are written, while the token bucket drains. It sells "trust boundary" in 5 s on desktop. On the phone the stage is pushed below the asks, so at 0–3 s you only see the top of the card, and the shared cookie card covers it by 3 s.
2. **One in, one out:** Partly. On desktop the six-chapter pinned stage (01/06 to 06/06) swaps one panel at a time, which is right. But all six panels are the same composition: browser chrome, a 44px grey phrase (`JWKS ✓`, `sub · owner · roles`, `429 · Retry-After`, `no hop`), then 2–4 grey rows. About 4,000px of scroll passes before anything changes kind. On the phone the pin drops and the chapters stack as text, card, text, card for about 4,500px. That's a document.
3. **Real visual in every section:** Mostly. Hero stage, flow strip, chapter card, agent-prompt block, the black Forge simulator, console mock, and the closing Sigil line art all count. The "Related capabilities" row is four text links with no object.
4. **One ask, nothing floating, scale rhythm:** The header ask turns into "Read the docs" past the hero on desktop. Good. On the phone it stays "Try Hanzo", so the page's own ask never reaches the phone header. Nothing of the page's floats over content (only the shared cookie card does). Scale mostly has rhythm: h1 52, h2 44 at the "moment", h3 32, body 16, mono 12. But the chapter stage puts a 44px grey phrase in the card next to a 32px h3 in every chapter, so each chapter screen has two big things side by side.
5. **Hover and press:** The primary and secondary buttons darken or fill on hover and press to scale 0.97. Good. Copy turns to "Copied". The Forge JWT / API key / None segmented control moves its white thumb and rewrites the received headers. That's the best interaction on the page. Weak spots: the six chapter dots only darken a shade on hover, with no chapter name or preview, so the hover tells you nothing. The related-capability links show only an underline, and their arrows sit in different places. The "Copied" tick icon renders broken (a fragment, not a tick) at 1470.

## Fixes, in order
1. **Make the six chapters six different scenes, not one card re-skinned.** Keep the pin, but let each panel act out its line: 01 a token travels to a JWKS key and gets ticked; 02 the four headers strike through and are rewritten from claims; 03 an empty route map that stays empty; 04 the token bucket drains to a 429; 05 a health ping that touches nothing; 06 the gateway box slides inside the cloud binary. Drop the 44px grey phrase so the 32px h3 is the one big thing per screen.
2. **Phone chapters:** don't stack six cards. Pin a shorter stage (card on top, the h3 and text swapping below) or use a horizontal snap rail with the dots. Either way, one thing in, one out, and remove the empty space at the top and bottom of each card.
3. **Reduced motion:** render the hero's final state (JWKS tick and the three rewritten headers, X-Org-Id struck) and every chapter's final state. No text left at opacity 0.
4. **Agent-prompt block, desktop:** put the `api /v1/gateway` row above the block as on the phone, so it stops sitting on the header bar. Wrap the prompt or fade its end with a "scroll →" hint so it is not cut at "confi". Fix the broken "Copied" tick icon.
5. **Phone flow strip:** fit the rail inside 375 and 320 (shorten the line or stack "client" / "Hanzo Cloud /v1" end-labels) so the end-label is visible and the pill keeps the gutter.
6. **Phone header ask:** swap "Try Hanzo" for the page's "Read the docs" past the hero, as desktop does.
7. **Chapter dots:** on hover show the chapter's h3 as a small mono preview, and on click scroll to that chapter. Give them a press state.
8. **Related capabilities:** make all four the same (arrow in the same place, one-line descriptor for all or none) and give each a small object or a hover preview, not an underline alone.
9. **Console section:** bottom-align the caption with the console's footer on the 8/5 grid at 14–16px, or move it under the window, so the left column isn't an empty field.
10. **Phone hero stage:** give the request card a clear gap from the GATEWAY rule, and move the pause button off the rule.
