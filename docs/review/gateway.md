## Fixes, in order
1. Hero stage: make the request card actually travel. Have it cross the Gateway line, get its headers rewritten at the line, and land on the right half as a trusted row in the "Hanzo Cloud /v1" lane. Then the right 50% of the stage is the payoff, not a grey field. Keep the ledger top right, but feed it from the card arriving.
2. Hero stage: move the "Token-bucket" pill clear of the card's path, or raise its z-index above the card, so it is never covered at 1470x801. Check 1280 and 1024 too.
3. Make the server-rendered first frame equal the final state (JWKS ✓, three rewritten headers, bucket at its resting level). This matters especially under prefers-reduced-motion, so hydration never visibly jumps the card.
4. Chapter stage: drop the duplicate counter. Keep "0N / 06" in one place (the window bar or under the heading, not both). Drop the three-dot fake browser chrome from the six panels and the console mock, or keep it on one object only.
5. Chapter 04: give both buckets their top rim, or crop them on purpose, so they don't read as clipped. Chapter 06: separate the "Gateway" chip from the "Hanzo Cloud" pill, or nest it visibly inside, so it doesn't look like a collision.
6. Phone (375, 320): don't stack six identical window cards. Use one sticky panel that swaps as the six texts scroll past (the same pinned idea as desktop), or a horizontal snap rail with the six panels.
7. Dark room on phone: put the "every service behind it" result directly under the switches, or make it sticky, so tapping a switch shows its answer on the same screen. Replace the lone "02 / 06" opener with the room's real label from the copy, or remove it.
8. One docs ask. Keep "Read the docs" in the header and in the hero. At Get Gateway, lead with "Deploy to Cloud" and keep "Self-host" as the quiet link, and drop or re-target the "Gateway docs →" related tile so the same URL isn't offered five times.
9. Hover that tells you something: the six chapter buttons should preview their panel (the diagram's mini-state or the heading) on hover, not only fill grey. Give Service Mesh and Nodes their one-line description like DNS has, so the related row is consistent. Put the "Gateway docs →" arrow in the same right-aligned slot as the others.
10. Add one pointer-aware moment (e.g. the hero lane lines respond to the pointer, or tilt on the console mock within 2.5°) so not all motion is self-run or scroll.
11. "Example project" block: give the left column something (the selected row's detail, or let the mock span the full container) so half the screen isn't empty beside one sentence.
