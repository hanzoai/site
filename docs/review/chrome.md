## Fixes (ordered, most important first)
- **Phone side panel layout shift (CLS 0.085).** At 375x812, the step list in the window's side panel reflows each time a step's result opens and the previous one closes: rows move 40–48px, and the "Ask Hanzo…" field moves about 22px down and back. Give each step row a fixed height and let the result appear in a reserved slot (or animate it with transform/clip-path only), and pin the ask field to the bottom of the 516px panel so it never moves. Target CLS under 0.02 with the window in view for 15 s.
- **Give the "Real-time browser intelligence under enterprise control" screen an object, or close it up.** At 1470x801 it is a 32px heading, a lede and the "WHAT IT IS" caption floating in about 600px of white. Either join it to chapter 01, so the heading sits over the pinned stage as its title (the first panel arrives in the same viewport), or give it a small live object from text already on the page. Also cut the empty bands above "Paste and ship" and above "Built for engineering…" (about 200px of white each) so the next object peeks in.
- **Black room fit.** At 1470x801 the room's heading sits at y≈530 with about 300px of empty black above. The tester's "Allowed" verdict sits below the fold while you use the chips or the field, so you act and can't see the answer. Top-align the copy with the tester card, and fit the card (toggle, chips, field, chips, verdict) inside 801 − 60px, for example by putting the verdict beside the field or tightening the chip rows.
- **Dead control in the hero window.** The "Allowlist / Allow all sites" pill in the window's Organization Site Policy card looks pressable but does nothing. Either make it work like the black-room toggle (switching the "Allowed:" line), or render it as plain text so it doesn't promise a press.
- **Tidy the tail before the close.** The rail is followed by two hairline blocks ("Looking for Firefox, Safari…" with four pills, then "Get Hanzo in Chrome" with one "Deploy to Cloud" link), and then "Put Hanzo in every tab". That is three endings in a row. Keep the copy but make them one block: put "Get Hanzo in Chrome · Deploy to Cloud" as a single line under the pills, not a second h3 row with its own hairlines. This also lets the close arrive one screen sooner.
- Smaller: text links ("Configure Org Policies", "Browser docs", "Deploy to Cloud", "Manage Chrome Policies") should show something on hover: an underline drawing in, or the arrow sliding about 4px. On the phone, let the h1 break into 3 lines at 40px, as the brief asks (balance or a tighter measure). Inside the window render, consider moving "ACTIVE TAB · STAGING.ACME.INC" and the "Agent overlay active" pill below the "Acme Pro Enterprise Checkout" title, so the mock doesn't model a kicker over a heading.
