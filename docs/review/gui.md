# /gui — James-eye review
7 Nov, round 3, walked at 1470x801 and 375x812 (plus 320 and a reduced-motion load) with Playwright from node: frames at 0/1/3 s with no input, wheel-scrolled a screen at a time with 1.5 s dwells through the pinned stage and its release, every main control hovered and pressed on desktop and tapped on phone, a platform card opened and closed with Esc and with Back, console collected, h1 box and CLS tracked from first paint.

Verdict: 6/10

First complaint: "It's the same little 'Hello, Hanzo / Get started' card in a phone and a browser over and over. The hero shows it, then the next section shows it again, and on my phone it's the same pair of devices five times down the page. Where's the new thing each time I scroll?"

## The 11 answers
1. **Sells in 5 seconds, moves within 1 second?** Mostly. "Hanzo GUI / One UI kit. Every platform." and the four device frames say what it is. The black prop strip cycles `p` / `bg` / `rounded` on its own, and the four device cards change padding, fill and radius with it. That runs within the first second with no input and has a pause button. The weak part is the bottom of the screen. At 1470x801 the device labels sit on the fold, and then about 250px of blank white comes before the next section (desk s01). The first screen ends on dead air rather than on a hand-off.
2. **One in, one out, or a document?** Desktop: yes. The device stage pins and steps 01 to 05 with a scrubbed progress hairline, and each copy block fades out as the next comes in. That is the page's best moment. At release, though, the stage parks on 05 with an empty right column (desk s06), and the "06 / 06" step turns up in a different object in the black room. The counter and the stage disagree. Phone: no. The pinned stage unrolls into the same iPhone-plus-browser pair repeated once per step, with a heading and paragraph under each and hairlines between them. That is a document.
3. **A real visual in every section?** Yes, but it is nearly always the same visual. The hero, all five stage steps, the shorthand preview, the four platform cards and the opened card all show the same "Hello, Hanzo / Get started" mock. "Drop it in. Start shipping." has a real terminal, but its left column is one small heading floating in a half-empty field. In "Get GUI" the left half is two chips and empty space.
4. **One ask, nothing floating, scale rhythm?** The header ask becomes "View on GitHub" once you pass the hero, which is the right ask in Hanzo's words. Phone keeps "Try Hanzo", so desktop and phone carry different asks. Nothing of the page's own floats over content (the cookie card is shared chrome). Scale is reasonable: 52px h1 with a grey 52px second line, 26px section heads, a 44px closing head and 12-13px mono labels. Nothing grows or shrinks as the moment, though. The stage devices sit at one size from step 01 to 05.
5. **Every hover and press answers, no jumps?** Mostly. Press is scale 0.97 to 0.98 everywhere with no layout jump. Token chips brighten their border and text, and clicking one restyles the live preview (`$accent` flips the card to ink): a real answer. Platform-card hover is the best detail. The `p` / `bg` / `rounded` props appear and the + inverts, so hover tells you something. Weak spots: the platform card's own background shift (#f2f2f2 to #ededed) is invisible. "Deploy to Cloud" has no visible hover, only a 1px press. Copy hover is just a grey wash. Nothing is pointer-aware: no tilt on the cards and no magnetic ask.
6. **Overlap, font swap, layout shift?** The h1 box holds from first paint (89/110/580 px, CLS 0.0006). No font swap was seen, and there was no horizontal page scroll at 375 or 320. Overlaps found: on phone, the opened platform sheet slides up under the sticky header, which stays at full contrast above the scrim and covers the sheet's top edge and corners (phone-card-open). In the stage at step 03, the "Atomic CSS" and "macOS · Win · Linux" tags sit on top of the Get started card's border (intended annotation, but it reads as a collision). The platform cards' titles are misaligned: iOS and Android sit about 22px higher than Web and Desktop. The `$accent` chip wraps onto its own line on desktop. On phone the code sample soft-wraps mid-tag (`Hello,` / `Hanzo</Text>`) and loses its indentation.
7. **Which reference, and is its motion there?** Apple product page: a pinned scene where one panel changes. It is present on desktop and genuinely good. synchronized.studio card-to-page: the card does grow into a full-bleed sheet in about 500 ms, but what opens is a modal with one paragraph reused from step 01 and a large empty right side. It is not a page, and the URL does not change. Esc and Back both close it and return to the same scroll position with focus on the card, which is correct. There are no osply/hamol pointer details.
8. **Different from its neighbours?** The hero medium (a self-running prop strip driving four live device frames) is its own idea. Its signature, though, is the device quartet, and the page reuses that quartet as the hero, the stage and the card row. So the page differs from its neighbours but does not differ from itself. I did not compare against neighbouring pages in this walk.
9. **All 18 parts covered?** Not fully. Proof is thin: the only trust signal is a "4 stars" GitHub chip plus the licence. Small numbers like that do more harm than good placed prominently. The platform-card row has no head of its own. States are fine: there is a pause control, reduced motion shows the final state with the stage still stepping, and the Esc/Back/focus return all work. There were no console errors and no hydration diffs, on a normal load or a reduced-motion load.
10. **Banned tells?** No hue, gradient text, Inter, emoji or eyebrow above a headline. The mono line sits under the h1, and the 01 to 05 numbers sit under their heads. Borderline: the near-invisible card hover and the grey-wash Copy hover lean toward "buttons that fade on hover". The empty half-columns ("Drop it in", "Get GUI") read as the "lazy" look.
11. **Copy untouched?** It appears so. I did not diff it. The terminal shows `pnpm add @hanzo/gui` but imports from `'@hanzo/ui'`. If that is Hanzo's own copy, leave it. If not, flag it to the owner.

## Fixes, in order
1. **Stop repeating the mock.** Give each stage step its own state of the object rather than the same card again. For example: 01 the four frames, 02 one token change rippling across them, 03 an atomic-CSS readout, 04 the component tree, 05 a real spring transition. Make the hero and the stage different objects. Either the hero shows only the prop strip with one device and the stage introduces all four, or the hero quartet itself becomes the pinned stage, with no second copy below it.
2. **Phone: keep the stage as a stage.** At 375, pin the device pair (or a single device) and swap only the copy and annotation per step. Do not stack five copies of the same devices with hairlines between them.
3. **Close the stage properly.** Run the stage counter to 06/06, or renumber it 01 to 05, so it agrees with the shorthand panel. Do not release with an empty right column. Hold the last copy block until the stage leaves.
4. **Cut the dead air.** Close the roughly 250px gap under the hero labels so "Build it once. Ship it everywhere." hands off from the first screen. Fill or tighten the empty left columns of "Drop it in. Start shipping." and "Get GUI" with the 8/5 split, bottom-aligned. Remove the empty bottom of the platform cards.
5. **Make the opened card a page.** Give each platform its own content in the opened state: that platform's own screen, a live prop change and the relevant step copy. Do not reuse step 01's sentence. Set the sheet below the sticky header on phone, or dim the header under the scrim, so the header does not overlap the sheet.
6. **Hover that shows.** Give the platform cards a visible lift or tilt of up to 2.5° on fine pointers, beyond the props reveal. Make "Deploy to Cloud" slide its arrow. Make Copy change to a tick and "Copied" instead of a grey wash.
7. **Fix the alignments.** Align the platform-card title baselines. Keep the `$accent` chip on the same row. Make the phone code block scroll horizontally instead of soft-wrapping mid-tag.
8. **Proof.** Replace or de-emphasise the "4 stars" chip. Lead with something Hanzo can stand behind, such as "Runs every Hanzo app in production", already in the closing copy.
9. **Add one non-scroll pointer moment.** Add a cursor-aware sheen on the black room's panel, or a magnetic primary ask, so motion is not only idle plus scroll.
