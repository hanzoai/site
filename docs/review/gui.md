# /gui — James-eye review
7 Nov, round 4, walked at 1470x801 and 375x812 (isMobile, hasTouch) with Playwright in Chromium: frames at 0, 1 and 3 s with no input, wheel scroll one screen at a time with a 1.5 s dwell, hover and press on the main controls, a platform card opened and closed with Esc and Back, horizontal overflow checked at 375 and 320, and prefers-reduced-motion loads at both sizes.

Verdict: 6/10

First complaint: "On my phone the little phone drawing is sitting on top of the words. 'iOS', 'Nativ'… it's cut off. That's the one thing I said never to do."

## The 11 answers
1. **First screen sells, and something moves in under 1 s, but the demo starts late.** "Hanzo GUI / One UI kit. Every platform." sits over four live device frames (iOS, Android, Web, Desktop), with a black token pill driving them, so it sells in under 5 s. The h1 lines cut in within about 0.9 s: the transform settles from 56px to 0 with the box fixed at top 89 and height 110, so it is a cut-in, not a shift. The self-running token demo only changes for the first time at about 3.4 s. Until then the devices are frozen.
2. **Mostly one in, one out.** The pinned stage swaps one panel at a time: the tokens step turns the devices graphite, then wireframe outlines and chips for Composable Primitives, then fills for Animated by Default, with a 01/06 counter. After that come the graphite playground, the terminal, the platform rail, Get GUI and the close. The steps are too short, though. One screen of wheel jumps from 01 to 03, so "Design tokens, not CSS" is never seen when scrolling a screen at a time. On the phone, step 05 is left alone on a blank white screen after the devices have scrolled away.
3. **Real objects almost everywhere.** There are device mocks, the token playground with a live browser preview, a terminal with Hanzo's code, four platform cards with their own mini UIs, and the agent-prompt card. The closing section ("The design system that ships your product") is type and two buttons with no object, which is acceptable for a close. The playground's status row is a 400px box holding a lone ✓ and nothing else. It reads as a missing message.
4. **Ask: right on desktop, split on the phone. Scale: flat in the middle.** On desktop the header ask swaps from "Try Hanzo" to "View on GitHub" once the hero has passed, with nothing floating. On the phone the header keeps "Try Hanzo" while a slim bottom bar says "View on GitHub", so there are two different asks. Scale has rhythm at the hero (52 / 52 grey / mono 13) and the close (44). The whole middle runs at 26 / 21 / 16, three small text columns with no medium-to-big moment. On the phone the stage h2 "Build it once. Ship it everywhere." is 17px, smaller than the 21px h3 under it, so the hierarchy is upside down.
5. **Press answers; hover mostly only recolours.** The chips press to scale 0.97 and their hover brightens the text and border. The platform cards press at 0.97, but their hover is only #f2f2f2 to #e4e4e7. There is no tilt and no preview, and the hover tells you nothing. Copy inverts to ink. The hero "View on GitHub" hover only lightens to #2c2c2e: an off-ramp grey and a near-fade. I measured no change on the "Deploy to Cloud" and "hanzoai/gui" links themselves; there may be a child arrow change. The card opens into its sheet (synchronized), but mid-morph the card content is visibly stretched sideways ("Android / Native" squashed and smeared) because the layout scale is not counter-corrected.
6. **Overlap, yes: at 375.** In the platform rail the device mock overlaps the card title and caption. "iOS" crowds the phone frame, "Nativ" is cut by it, and "And… / Nativ" is cut on the next card. The terminal code is cut at the right edge on the phone ("…from", "Hello, Hanzo</T"). There is no horizontal page overflow at 375 or 320. I saw no font swap and no layout shift. The console was clean on every load, including reduced motion: no hydration diff and no errors.
7. **References: apple.com and synchronized.studio.** The pinned stage with one changing panel and the sticky ask come from apple.com, and the card that opens into its sheet comes from synchronized.studio. Both are actually present. The apple stage is too quick per step, and the synchronized morph distorts mid-flight.
8. **Different from its neighbours, I believe.** Its hero medium (four live device frames driven by one token pill) and its signature (one prop line re-rendering every platform) are its own. I did not walk the neighbouring pages this round.
9. **18 parts: the visible ones are covered.** Hero, narrative stage, proof (licence and stars), install, agent prompt, close, states (copy, pause, focus ring) and reduced motion are all present. Reduced motion shows the final, settled state and the stage still steps by scroll. The pause control dims correctly.
10. **Banned tells: one, overlap at 375×812.** No hue, gradient text, Inter, glow or pill above the h1. The mono line sits under the h1, not above it.
11. **Copy untouched.** Every visible sentence and label traces to `content/live/gui.json` or `content/pages/gui.html`, or comes from Hanzo's own code block ("Hello, Hanzo", "Get started", "Welcome"). Copy and Pause are in `ui-strings.json`. I found no invented copy.

## Fixes, in order
1. **Platform rail at 375.** Size the device mock to sit fully above the card's title block, or put the title and caption below the art in normal flow. No art may cross "iOS / Native" or the + button. Check at 375 and 320.
2. **Hero demo.** Start the token cycle within 1 s of load, with the first change at about 800 ms, so the devices move without input. Keep the cut-in.
3. **Stage pacing.** Give each pinned step at least about 0.8 screen of scroll, so a one-screen wheel lands on every step from 01 to 06. On the phone, keep the devices pinned through step 05 so its text is never alone on white.
4. **Card-to-sheet morph.** Counter-scale the card's inner content during the layout animation, or crossfade it, so text and art never stretch mid-flight.
5. **Card hover.** Make it tell something: a 2.5° pointer tilt plus the device mock waking up (the token pill applying), not just #e4e4e7.
6. **Phone ask.** Use one ask. Either the header carries "View on GitHub" after the hero, as on desktop, or drop the bottom bar.
7. **Phone heading scale.** Make the stage h2 at least the h3 size (21 to 26px), so the section head outranks the step title.
8. **Middle scale.** Give the stage one moment: let the active step title or the counter grow while it is current, then settle. The middle should not be three equal small columns.
9. **Playground status row.** Fill it with the state the ✓ stands for, using Hanzo's own words, or collapse it to a tick beside "/>". Drop the empty 400px box. Remove the duplicate value fields under each chip row unless they do something.
10. **Phone terminal.** Wrap or visibly scroll the code (a fade at the edge, with scroll) so lines do not look cut.
11. **Hero button hover.** Use a palette step, #262626 or #171717, not #2c2c2e, and add the press translateY(1px) so the hover is not just a fade.
