# /hsm — James-eye review
7 Nov, round 2, walked at 1470x801 and 375x812 (isMobile, hasTouch) with Playwright: idle frames at 0/1/3 s, wheel-scrolled a screen at a time with 1.5 s dwell, hovered/pressed every main control on desktop and tapped on phone, frame-diffed the hero idle, measured CLS, checked overflow at 375 and 320, and reloaded under prefers-reduced-motion.

Verdict: 6/10

First complaint: "On my phone it's a document again. The black section is six screens of text, then a picture, then a big black gap, then more text. On the laptop it's one stage where the panel changes; on the phone that idea is gone. And the hero just sits there for three seconds before anything happens."

## The 11 answers
1. **First screen sells / moves in 1 s?** Half. The 52px h1 cuts in and the lede rises, so something moves in the first second. The slab object, though, is frozen: frame diffs read 0 changed bytes from load to about 2.8 s at both sizes. After that the only visible change is the module name under it ("One interface → AWS KMS" becomes "Azure Key Vault"). The "key id + bytes" and "signature ✓" chips don't visibly travel through the slab, and pointer hover on the object does nothing. It does sell the idea in 5 seconds: clear h1, one lede, one black ask, one object.
2. **One in, one out, or a document?** Desktop: yes. The black room pins, and its six panels (01/06 to 06/06) swap one at a time while the slab diagram changes layer emphasis and labels. That part is good. Phone: no. The stage unpins into a 4,842px stack of text, diagram, then 250–300px of empty black, repeated six times. That's a document with dead air.
3. **A real visual in every section?** Mostly. Hero slab, stage slab (six states), dark agent-prompt card, module slab inside the picker, and a contour Sigil that drifts in the close. "Paste and ship" has only a code card. It's the same isometric slab three times (hero, stage, picker), so the page has one object in three costumes. There's no Hanzo media.
4. **One ask, nothing floating, scale rhythm?** The header ask swaps to "View on GitHub" once you scroll (desktop), which matches the hero ask. That's shared chrome, not counted. Floating over content: the stage dot hover tooltip ("Post-quantum, and honest about where it runs") lands on top of the "04 / 06" caption. Scale: the hero is fine (52 / 16). Section heads drift with no logic: "Paste and ship" 26px, "Pick your module" 32px, stage h3s 32px, "Get HSM" 44px. "Paste and ship" sits with its lede jammed directly under it at 0 gap, and "GET STARTED WITH HSM" is jammed under "Get HSM" at 0 gap, so the close reads as three stacked lines of different sizes with no room.
5. **Every hover/press answers?** Partly. The hero/closing asks and the picker tabs change fill (tabs also move a white pill). Copy flips to "✓ Copied". The six stage dots change nothing in computed style on hover; only a dark tooltip appears, and it covers content (see 4). Picking a module swaps the slab and the left text, but the right column barely changes (ML-DSA-65 text is identical across modules), so the change reads as small. Hover on the hero object gives nothing (no tilt, no light).

## Fixes, in order
1. Fix the reduced-motion hydration mismatch in `Settle` (`Hsm.jsx`): render the same `style` on server and first client paint (e.g. always emit `transform-origin:50% 30%` and set the reduced-motion transform after mount, or drive it with a class). Re-check that the red badge is gone with `reducedMotion:'reduce'`.
2. Phone stage: keep the one-panel-changes idea at 375. Pin a short diagram stage (about 45vh) at the top and swap the text below it, or use a horizontal snap rail with the 01/06 counter. Kill the 250–300px black gaps between panels. Give the diagram labels a fixed width so they don't wrap into the right edge.
3. Hero object alive in under 1 s with no input: start the cycle immediately (pulse travels from "key id + bytes" through the slab to "signature ✓", then the module name swaps) instead of after about 3 s. Add the allowed card tilt (≤2.5°) or a pointer-aware sheen on fine pointers.
4. Stage dots: give hover a real state change (dot grows or fills, ring at white 30%) and place the label tooltip above the dot row with clearance, or drop it. It must not sit on "0N / 06".
5. Spacing and scale: put a 10.5–17px step between "Paste and ship" and its lede, and between "Get HSM" / "GET STARTED WITH HSM" / "Hardware security module". Pick one section-head size (26px resting per MASTER) for "Paste and ship" and "Pick your module", and let only the stage or the close grow past it. Bottom-align the "Pick your module" lede to its heading per the 8/5 split.
6. Module picker: make the switch feel like a state change. Morph the slab (layer count or glow position per module), cross-fade the whole card, and make the right column vary per module where Hanzo's copy allows. On phone, add an edge fade to the tab rail so the cut pill reads as scrollable.
7. Vary the slab across its three uses (hero lit-white, stage line-art, picker), or replace the picker thumbnail with something that tells you about the module, so the page isn't one object three times.
