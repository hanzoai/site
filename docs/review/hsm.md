# /hsm — James-eye review
7 Nov, round 3, walked at 1470x801 and 375x812 (isMobile, hasTouch) with Playwright Chromium on the shared dev server: frames at 0, 1, 3 and 5.5 s untouched; wheel-scrolled one screen at a time with ~1.5 s dwell; hovered and pressed the ask, chapter dots, module tabs, Copy and the hero pause on desktop, tapped on phone; reduced-motion load at both sizes; overflow at 375 and 320; a short look at /kms, /iam and /guard for neighbour comparison.

Verdict: 5/10

First complaint: "Turn the animations off and the black section is six paragraphs printed on top of each other. You can't read a word of it. And with them on, it's the same stack drawing three times, then the same heading twice. It feels like one trick on repeat, not a product."

## The 11 answers
1. **5 seconds / 1 second.** Partly. Something does move inside a second: a dot travels through the stack, "a key id and some bytes" → "a signature ✓" pills appear, and "One interface → AWS KMS" cycles through the vendors. But the first screen is a small two-line "Hanzo HSM" floating in white on the left beside a big flat grey box. It reads as a template, not a launch. The h1 sits at about 50% of the height, not 38%. The hero ends flush at 801 with no peek of the next section, so nothing pulls you down.
2. **One in, one out?** On desktop, yes for the chapters: a pinned black stage where the stack stays and one plate lights while the right-hand text swaps 01→06. That is the best thing on the page. After that it turns back into a document: Paste and ship, then "Bring the module you already have" a second time, then Get HSM, with large dead white bands between them (about 140 px above Paste and ship, about 150 px under the module panel, about 250 px above Get HSM, about 180 px of empty white with a faint contour fragment above the footer). On phone the chapters are about 2,500 px of black: a sticky drawing on top and six paragraphs scrolling up under a hairline that cuts them off mid-line.
3. **A real object in every section?** Yes, but it is the same object every time. The isometric module stack is the hero (light), all six chapters (dark), and the module panel (small, black). Paste and ship has the code card, and Get HSM has a faint contour Sigil. It is one drawing used three times, never a second idea.
4. **One ask, nothing floating, scale rhythm.** On desktop the header ask swaps to "View on GitHub" once the hero ask leaves, which is right. On phone a full-width "View on GitHub" bar rises over the black chapter room just after the hero, then disappears a screen later. It pops in and out, and while it is up it sits over the room. The header there still says "Try Hanzo" (shared chrome, not counted). Scale is mostly sane: h1 52, chapter heads 32, section heads 26, close 44. But the h1 is the smallest thing in its own first screen next to a 820 px grey box, so the hero has no "big" at all.
5. **Hover and press.** The primary darkens on hover (#0a0a0a → #262626) and presses to scale .98. Good. Module tabs get a grey fill and a cross-dim of the panel on change. Copy turns into "✓ Copied". Chapter dots show a dark tip with the chapter title and a ring. The hero pause turns into Play. "Deploy to Cloud" shows no computed change on hover except its arrow. No hover anywhere tells you something new (a number, a preview). There is no pointer-aware detail at all: the stack ignores the cursor.
6. **Overlap, font swap, layout shift.** **Fails.** With prefers-reduced-motion on desktop, all six chapter headings and paragraphs render in one grid cell on top of each other, an illegible pile for the whole length of the black room (dr-s01…s05). In the same mode the hero pill reads "a key id and some" with "bytes" hidden behind the plate. Normal motion shows no overlap, CLS is 0.0002, the h1 box does not move while fonts load, and the console is clean. No Next.js issue was raised for this page.
7. **Reference.** It wants to be apple.com: a pinned stage where one panel changes. That motion is really there on desktop, which is the page's strongest point. There is nothing of osply/hamol pointer detail and no card-to-page open. On phone the pin collapses into a long scrolling column with the art stuck on top, so the Apple feel is gone at 375.
8. **Different from its neighbours?** The hero is a half-match. /iam has the same composition: h1 and lede on the left, a #f5f5f5 rounded box on the right with a round pause button at its top-right corner. /kms at least centres its h1 over a full-width console. The exploded-stack chapters are HSM's own signature, but the hero frame is the shared template.
9. **18 parts.** Gaps: heading order skips (h1 → six h3 chapters before any h2). Reduced motion does not show a usable final state (part 9 and part 12). The black room enters with a hard full-bleed edge, not as a rounded card opening out (3.1 rooms). The hero has no 56 px peek (part 3). The two-part "Bring the module" head has its lede top-aligned while the heading sits low, not bottom-aligned (3.1 space). The phone ask bar appears and vanishes (part 4). The rest that I could see holds: one solid fill, pause control on the loop, 44 px dots, no horizontal scroll at 375 or 320 (the module tab rail scrolls inside itself), clean console.
10. **Banned tells.** None of the hard ones: no hue, no gradient text, no Inter, no eyebrow above a headline, no cursor glow. The page does have overlap in reduced motion (banned: overlap), and a near-boxed hero (a framed grey card; it animates, so it is not a static boxed hero, but it reads boxed).
11. **Copy untouched?** No. "Bring the module you already have" and "One Go interface behind all of them, so which module you use is a line of configuration rather than a rewrite" each appear twice, as chapter 06 and again as the module section. The module panel adds a "THE KEY" label that is not in Hanzo's copy. It re-casts a clause into a new sentence, "An in-memory signer for development." It also files Hanzo's sentences under individual vendors (for example "Hanzo holds a handle, never the key." under AWS KMS, and "ML-DSA-65 is available as a signing algorithm today." under every vendor), which makes per-vendor claims Hanzo never made. The diagram labels ("a handle", "the key", "the password", "decrypts it at boot", "Shares are attested", "a signature ✓", "One interface → …") are fragments cut from Hanzo's sentences, re-cased, and none of them is in ui-strings. They are tolerable as diagram call-outs, but the panel label and the re-cased sentence are invented copy.

## Fixes, in order
1. **Reduced motion, desktop chapters:** under `prefers-reduced-motion`, render the six chapters as a stacked list, one heading and paragraph each, each beside its own still drawing, or keep the pin but show only the active chapter with opacity 0 and `visibility:hidden` on the rest. Never leave all six in one grid cell at full opacity. Check at 1470x801 that every chapter reads.
2. **Reduced motion, hero pill:** the left pill must show its full text "a key id and some bytes" in its final state, not clipped by the plate.
3. **Kill the duplicate section.** Chapter 06 already says "Bring the module you already have" and its paragraph. Fold the vendor tabs into chapter 06's stage, where the lit bottom plate swaps vendor names, or into the hero's "One interface → …" cycler. Delete the second h2 and its lede. If the panel stays, drop the invented "THE KEY" label and the per-vendor sentence mapping, and show only the vendor names Hanzo lists. Restore "an in-memory signer for development" exactly as written, inside its sentence.
4. **Give the hero a big thing.** Let the stack break out of the grey box (no frame, or a box that dissolves as the pills fire) and grow the module to be the one big object. Put the h1 baseline near 38% and leave a 56 px peek of the black room. Right now /hsm and /iam share the same left-text/right-grey-box frame.
5. **Phone chapters:** make each chapter one screen: drawing at about 45svh with its own lit plate, then heading and paragraph. Stop text sliding under a sticky drawing and being cut at the hairline. Keep the phone ask bar either up for the whole page or off. Do not let it pop over the black room and then vanish.
6. **Close the dead white.** Cut the empty bands between Paste and ship, the module section and Get HSM, and above the footer, to the section-padding token. Make the closing contour Sigil readable (its lines are near-invisible greys) or remove it.
7. **Add one pointer moment** on desktop: tilt the stack to 2.5° with the cursor, or part the plates up to 12 px on hover, so the object answers the hand and not only the clock and the scroll.
8. **Heading order:** make the chapter titles h2 (or add an h2 for the chapter room) so h1 → h3 does not skip.
9. **Black room entry:** enter the chapter room as a rounded card inside the gutters that opens to full width as it is reached, per the room rule, rather than a hard full-bleed edge.
