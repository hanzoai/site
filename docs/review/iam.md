# /iam — James-eye review
7 Nov, round 3, walked at 1470x801 and 375x812 (isMobile, hasTouch) with Playwright: frames at 0/1/3 s untouched, wheel scroll a screen at a time with 1.5 s dwell (plus a 200px fine scroll through the strip), hover and press on every main control on desktop, taps on phone, overflow at 375 and 320, a prefers-reduced-motion load, layout-shift observer, console capture, and /kms opened for comparison.

Verdict: 6/10

First complaint: "It's the sign-in page and I never see anyone sign in. Dots on a line, grey boxes with one word in them. Show me hanzo.id actually doing it. And on the phone it's eight grey boxes stacked one after another: that's a document again."

## The 11 answers
1. **First screen sells in 5 s, moves within 1 s?** Half. Moves: yes, the hero stepper (hanzo.id, Authorization code, PKCE · S256, RS256, JWKS) starts advancing by 1 s and the caption swaps. Sells: weakly. The h1 and line are clear, but the object is an abstract five-dot line in a grey panel. It reads as a diagram, not as a product. On the phone the stepper is vertical and falls below the fold, so the first screen is the h1 and two pills. On desktop there is about 200px of empty white under the hero before "hanzo.id".
2. **One in, one out, or a document?** On desktop, mostly one in, one out. The "hanzo.id" strip pins and scrolls sideways, and the centred card lifts, turns ink and opens its sentence while the others go grey. That is the best moment on the page. It ends badly, though: the last card stops at x≈905 with the right 40% of the screen empty, and the strip lets go into a tall dead gap. After the strip it is a document: code block, three link rows, a black room, Get IAM. On the phone the strip collapses into eight stacked cards about 600px each, which is a pure document.
3. **Real visual object in every section?** No. The cards hold line glyphs (key, arrow-into-box, brackets) and a word. The "Refresh tokens" black room holds a row of dots. Paste and ship is a code block. Get IAM is the shared contour SVG. There is no real product UI: no hanzo.id sign-in screen, no token, no JWKS document, no org switcher. Every section has an object, but none of them is real.
4. **One ask, nothing floating, scale rhythm?** The ask is right: "API reference" moves into the sticky header after the hero, and nothing floats over content (the cookie card was dismissed). The rhythm is right on desktop: h1 52, section heads 26, one big figure per card at a time with grey neighbours, Zen Mono counters small. On the phone every card has a 40px+ figure, so one big thing follows another for eight screens.
5. **Every hover and press answers, no jumps?** Mostly. Buttons press to scale ~0.975. The link rows fill #f5f5f5. Cards darken on hover. Gaps: "Source →" and "Deploy to Cloud →" show no measurable hover change. The prompt text row and the Get IAM primary show no hover change either. After pressing Pause, the play control fades to near-invisible grey on the panel, which reads as disabled, not as "press me to resume". The "01 / 08 – 08 / 08" counter never changes while the strip moves, so it is a dead label.
6. **Overlap, font swap, layout shift?** No layout shift in the page; the only CLS entries are in the shared header's right cluster. No font swap seen. Overflow at 375: the "tenancy boundary" figure runs past the right edge of card 07 (the text ends ~2px outside the card, against the card's padding). At 320 the overflow comes from the shared header button (shared chrome, not counted). No hydration diff in the page. Console was clean at both sizes and under reduced motion; the Next dev portal had no issue listed.
7. **Which reference, and is its motion there?** Apple product page: the pinned scrubbed strip with one panel lit is genuinely there on desktop. Little else is. There is no card-to-page open (synchronized.studio): the related rows are plain links. There is no editorial asymmetry or pointer detail (osply, hamol). There is no WebGL or 3D moment, although a fixed canvas exists. The page's motion is the stepper (idle), the strip (scroll) and a refresh-token replay (state); scroll is not more than half of it. Pointer motion is absent: no tilt, no magnetic ask, no hover preview.
8. **Different from its neighbour?** Above the fold, yes. /kms opens on a centred h1 with a big black secret-and-versions UI, while /iam opens with h1 left and the stepper right. Below the fold, no: the closing run ("Paste and ship" code block, "Related capabilities" rows, "Get IAM" with the same contour line art) is the same template on both. The black "Refresh tokens" room is also the same black-room-with-a-demo move /kms uses.
9. **All 18 parts covered?** Not in spirit. The structure is there (hero, proof strip, developer block, related, closing, footer, skip link, progress bar, reduced motion). Media (part 10) is empty. Trust (part 5) is only "3 stars" and a licence pill. Mobile (part 11) is unadapted: the strip just becomes a list. States: under reduced motion the refresh-token demo rests on its first dot instead of the final revoked-family state, so it says nothing without a press.
10. **Banned tells?** No hue, no gradient text, no green, no Inter, and no eyebrow above the h1 (live's "Identity" kicker is correctly dropped). Close calls: the hero is a boxed grey panel to the right of the h1. It is not static, but it is framed. The 8-card stack on the phone reads as clutter, with an explaining sentence under every tile. "RELATED CAPABILITIES" sits as a left eyebrow beside a list, which is allowed. "api" is a black chip label over the code block.
11. **Copy untouched?** Yes. Every visible sentence traces to content/live/iam.json. The figure words (S256, argon2id, single-use, passkey, SCIM 2.0, RS256, tenancy boundary, Authz) are lifted from Hanzo's own sentences. Two small composites: "PKCE · S256" joins two Hanzo words, and "01 / 08 – 08 / 08" is built from the counters. The page repeats itself, though: card 03 and the black room both say the full "Refresh tokens are single-use…" sentence. The hero caption cycles through the same Federation and Signing keys sentences the cards then repeat, so the visitor reads the same lines two or three times.

## Fixes, in order
1. **Put the real product in the hero.** Make the stage a hanzo.id sign-in that runs itself: Google, GitHub or OIDC buttons, a passkey prompt, then the issued token decoding into its header, RS256 and JWKS kid. Drive the existing five steps from it, so the stepper becomes the progress line under a real UI instead of being the whole object. Remove the grey frame so it sits on the page. Close the dead 200px under it.
2. **Rebuild the phone strip.** Do not stack 8 cards. Make it a horizontal snap rail with inertia (one card in view, a peek of the next, the counter live). Or keep the pin and drive it by vertical scroll as on desktop. Each card should show only the figure and title until it is centred.
3. **Make the strip end land.** Let the last card finish centred, or bring the next section in as the strip leaves, so the right 40% is never empty white. Make the "01 / 08 – 08 / 08" counter track the active card ("06 / 08").
4. **Stop saying it twice.** Either drop the "Refresh tokens" black room or make it the place card 03 opens into, the card-to-page move. The card would keep only its figure and title, and the room would hold the sentence and the revoke-the-family demo. The hero caption should not cycle the same sentences the cards hold.
5. **Make the refresh-token demo speak.** Label the dots with Hanzo's words (token, spent, family revoked). Play it once on arrival without a press. Under reduced motion, show the final revoked state.
6. **Give this page its own closing.** The Paste-and-ship, related and Get IAM run with the contour art is identical to /kms. Give /iam its own Get IAM object (the discovery document or a JWKS key appearing), and make the related rows open into their pages.
7. **Fix the controls.** Add a hover answer to "Source →", "Deploy to Cloud →", the Get IAM primary and the prompt row (an arrow nudge or an underline draw, not opacity). Keep the Play control after Pause at full ink, so it does not read as disabled.
8. **Fix the 375 overflow.** Let "tenancy boundary" wrap or step down a size at phone width so it stays inside card 07's padding.
9. **Add one pointer moment.** Card tilt to 2.5° on the strip cards, or a magnetic "API reference", on fine pointers only.
