# /iam — James-eye review
7 Nov, round 4, walked at 1470x801, 375x812 and 320 (isMobile, hasTouch) with Playwright Chromium: frames at 0, 1, 3, 6, 10, 14 and 20 s untouched; wheel scrolled one screen at a time with a 1.5 s dwell, plus 200px steps through the pinned rail; hovered and pressed every control in main on desktop; tapped the controls and swiped the rail on phone; checked console, horizontal overflow and a prefers-reduced-motion load at desktop and phone; compared against /kms.

Verdict: 7/10

First complaint: "When it loads, the sign-in card is already finished, then it wipes itself grey and starts again, and the buttons slide up under my eye. Then I scroll into the cards and the first one, the one it says I'm on, is chopped off the left edge. It's nearly there, but it doesn't feel finished."

## The 11 answers
1. **First screen sells; something moves within 1 s?** Mostly yes. "Hanzo IAM / The sign-in behind every Hanzo service." sits left and the hanzo.id sign-in card runs itself on the right: GitHub is picked, then Authorization code, PKCE · S256, the RS256 bars fill, JWKS ticks, and the five-step timeline below fills in. It plays once in about 10 s, then settles and only swaps the chosen provider, which is right. But frame 0 (server paint) shows the finished card and a grey API reference button. By 1 s, after hydration, the card has reset to washed-out grey and the hero buttons have risen 16px (y 477 to 461) after they were already painted. So the first thing you see is the end state being undone. Under the ask, the left half of the hero is empty, and there is a ~140px band of nothing under the timeline before the next section.
2. **One thing in, one thing out, or a document?** On desktop, the 8-card hanzo.id rail is a real pinned horizontal scrub: the active card turns its figure ink and shows its sentence, and the counter top right ticks 01 / 08 to 08 / 08. That is the page's best passage. After it, the page turns into a document: Paste and ship, a Related capabilities list, then the black Refresh tokens room, then Get IAM, each just stacked. The related-links list sits mid-story, between the code block and the black room, where it interrupts instead of closing. On phone the rail is a native swipe rail with snap and a working counter (swiped to 02 / 08), but the next-card peek is about 12px at 375, so nothing invites the swipe.
3. **A real visual object in every section?** Yes. Hero: the sign-in card with its flow line. Rail: per-card line art (PKCE plug, arrow-into-slot, token chain ending in X, SCIM merge, twin keys in brackets, tenancy boxes, Authz gate). Code block with Copy. Black room: the token-family chain with Replay and a legend. Close: the sign-in card again. Only the Related capabilities list has no object, and that is acceptable for a list. The close **reuses the hero card** (now tilted), so the page ends on an object you have already seen instead of a new one.
4. **One ask in the sticky header, nothing floating, scale rhythm?** On desktop, the header's solid ask becomes "API reference" (Hanzo's label) once past the hero and inverts correctly over the black room. Nothing floats over content apart from the shared cookie card. Scale is mostly right: h1 52, h2 26, h3 21, figure words at about 52-64. But the rail shows five display-size words across one screen at once (S256, argon2id, single-use, passkey, SCIM…). Only one is ink and the rest are grey 40%, which saves it from "everything big at once", but only just. Under reduced motion they all go ink in a 4x2 grid, and that grid *is* eight big words at once.
5. **Every hover and press answers, no jumps?** Mostly. API reference darkens to #262626 and lifts 1px in the close. Discovery document gets a border and an inset fill. Related rows fill #f5f5f5. Rail card 03 fills #ededed. Copy lifts 2px, presses at scale 0.976 and confirms "Copied". Replay and the token buttons press at 0.96-0.98 and change state. The hero card tilts toward the pointer. No layout jumps on hover.

There is no page-level horizontal overflow at 1470, 375 or 320, and no console errors or hydration errors on a normal or a reduced-motion load (the Next issue badge stayed clear). ## Fixes, in order
1. **Stop the load flash.** Server-render the hero card in its *first* demo frame, not the finished one, or hold the finished frame and start the run from it. Don't paint the hero buttons at +16px and then animate them up after hydration; render them at rest, or keep them hidden until the rise starts. The primary must never show as grey at frame 0.
2. **Fix the rail's start.** When the stage pins, card 01 must sit fully inside the left gutter (x = 24) while it is active. Start the horizontal translate only after a short hold, so S256 / PKCE are fully readable at 01 / 08. Check the same for 08 / 08 at the end.
3. **Give the close its own object, or none.** Don't repeat the hero sign-in card at Get IAM. Use the finished JWKS/token state (the one thing the visitor has now "seen through") or keep the close typographic. If it stays, keep its tilt inside the 1446px gutter.
4. **Reorder the tail so it isn't a document:** rail → black Refresh tokens room (the "↓" on card 03 leads straight into it) → Paste and ship → Get IAM, with Related capabilities as the last quiet row inside the close. Make card 03 → room an opening (the card expands into the room) rather than an anchor jump, or drop the arrow.
5. **Close the dead bands:** trim the empty ~140px under the hero timeline, the ~200px between the rail and Paste and ship, and the black space above the room's heading, so each screen has one object and nothing empty.
6. **Related rows:** put the arrows on one line (they sit at 1010 / 997 / 1010) and at the row's right edge, not in the middle of a row that runs to 1446.
7. **Hover on the text links:** "Source →" and "Deploy to Cloud →" must answer (the arrow moves 3-4px, the underline draws in). Give Pause more than a grey wash.
8. **Phone rail affordance:** widen the next-card peek to about 40px at 375 and 320 so the swipe reads, and let the counter tick with the snap (it already does).
9. **Reduced-motion rail:** keep only the current card's figure in ink and the rest in grey, so the static grid isn't eight display words at full weight.
