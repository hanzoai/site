# /captable — James-eye review

Reviewed 2026-11-07, walked moving at 1470x801 and 375x812 (Playwright, wheel 400px every 700ms, rest pairs on screens 1, 6 and 12, hover and press on every main control, both sliders dragged).

Verdict: 7.5/10

First complaint: "The top half is great, I can play with the round and it moves on its own. Then I drag the exit price and the big number crashes into its own label, and after pricing the page just turns into a text document with big empty gaps."

## The 11 answers
1. **Sells in 5 seconds, moves within 1 second?** Yes. The h1 (52px), the founders' share as the one big figure, a short lede and the live cap table make the page clear immediately. The pre-money price drifts on its own ($12M to $13M, 72.3% to 73.1%), but the first change lands at about 1.3s, not within 1s. The pause button (44px, `aria-pressed`) stops it.
2. **One in, one out?** On desktop, mostly yes. "The instruments, modelled" is a real pinned stage: one table stays put while 01/06 to 06/06 dim and highlight rows, change the footer figure and fill the progress rule. Then the black waterfall room, then the term sheet. On phone, no: the stage turns into six near-identical table cards stacked one after another (about 570px each), which reads as a repeated document. After the waterfall, desktop also turns into a document: pricing, a text-only open-source block, three link rows, then the close, with blank bands of about 300px between them (above "The instruments, modelled", above the term sheet, and between pricing and "Built on Open Source").
3. **A real object in every section?** Hero: the table (yes). Chapters: the table (yes). Term sheet: an outlined sheet (yes). Waterfall: the stepped payout cards (yes, the best moment). Pricing: offset cards (yes). "Built on Open Source", "Get Captable" and the three Documentation / For Developers / Custom Solution rows have no object, only text and links. The close is one sentence and the ask, which is fine for a Band.
4. **One ask, sticky after the hero?** Yes. The header's "Try Hanzo" becomes the solid fill once the hero is passed, and the hero and close asks are quiet. Scale has rhythm: figure big, h1 52, chapter heads 26, captions small. One extra heavy shape: the inverted black "FOUNDERS, FULLY DILUTED" chip under the figure competes with the ask on the first screen (the card says the inverted chip was deleted, but this one is still there).
5. **Every hover and press answers?** Mostly. Hero Cta: border darkens, inset fill, press scale .98. Pause turns into a solid play button. Both sliders respond live, with focus rings. "Read the term sheet" opens a side sheet, focus moves in and Esc returns it. Plan cards open centred panels. The close row fills grey on hover. The "Documentation →" link beside the hero ask changed nothing measurable on hover (no colour, border or transform change on the anchor).
6. **Overlap, font swap, layout shift?** One overlap, which I could reproduce. While you drag the exit-price slider, "$43M" grows from 64 to 84px and its top runs into the "EXIT PRICE" Zen Mono label above it (desktop-walk.jpg). It settles back on release. CLS was 0.0002 on desktop (header chrome) and 0 on phone. No horizontal overflow, no console or page errors, and no red error badge (the `nextjs-portal` holds only the dev overlay's styles). No font swap seen.
7. **Which reference, and is its motion there?** apple.com product page: the product as hero, a pinned scene where one panel changes, one idea per screen. The pinned stage is there on desktop, and the drift, the slider and the sheet give idle, pointer and state motion as well as scroll. The editorial asymmetry of osply.co and hamol.studio shows only in the waterfall staircase. The lower third has no reference motion at all.
8. **Different from its neighbours?** Yes. The hero medium is a live figure plus an interactive table (not a phone or a rail), and the signature is a round that re-runs itself.
9. **All 18 parts covered (claims true on the page)?** 17 of 18 parts are filled; part 18's review line is open, as expected. The claims I checked hold: parity 103/103 text and 7/7 links (I re-ran it), sliders have 44px hit areas, the pause has `aria-pressed`, no console errors, the ask is at about 428px on phone. Two are not quite true: part 3's "One idea per 100svh; no 3 same-height sections in a row" (the phone chapters are six equal-height cards in a row) and part 11's "375×812 pass with no overlap" (the close row wraps badly; see Fixes). The first change is at 1.3s, not "within 1 s".
10. **Banned tells?** None from the list: no hue, no gradient text, no pill above a headline, no buttons that fade on hover. The "MOST POPULAR" pill sits beside a mono label inside a card, which is allowed.
11. **Copy untouched?** Yes. parity.py reports OK, 103/103 text and 7/7 links.

## Fixes (ordered, most important first)
- **Exit-price figure overlaps its label while dragging.** In the waterfall room, the figure grows 64→84px on drag but grows upward into the 11-12px "EXIT PRICE" label. Anchor the scale at the bottom-left (`transform-origin: left bottom`), or give the label a reserved gap of about 20px, so the label never touches the digits at 84px.
- **Phone chapters are six identical stacked tables.** At 375 the pinned stage becomes 6 × about 570px cards with the same table each time. Show one compact table and let the chapters move it: sticky table at the top and the chapter text scrolling under it, or a horizontal snap rail of the six states with the `01 / 06` caption. Either way one object changes instead of being repeated six times.
- **The lower third is a document.** After pricing: (a) cut the blank bands of about 300px above "The instruments, modelled", above the term sheet and between pricing and "Built on Open Source" down to the section padding (188px max). (b) Give "Built on Open Source / Get Captable" an object: the existing `License: Apache-2.0` and `hanzoai/captable 3 stars` chips as the focus, or a small repo / fork drawing (hanzoai/captable → upstream captableinc/captable) built from the text already there. (c) The three Documentation / For Developers / Custom Solution rows need a row hover fill like the close row.
- **Phone close row wraps into four broken lines.** "Get started with Cap Table / Read the docs →" splits into "Get started with Cap / Table" and "Read the / docs". At under 600px, stack the row: label on one line, "Read the docs →" right-aligned below, or drop the left label to 14px with `white-space: nowrap` on the right half.
- **First movement at 1.3s and a heavy chip beside the ask.** Start the first price step within about 600ms of load so the page is visibly alive inside 1s. Make the "FOUNDERS, FULLY DILUTED" caption ink-on-white mono like the other labels instead of a solid black chip, so the first screen has one heavy fill (the header ask) and not two black shapes.
- Minor: the "Documentation →" link in the hero needs a hover answer (the arrow shifts 4px, or an underline draws in). On the term sheet, the stray "×" signature line to the right of "Read the term sheet" reads as a close icon; give it its mono caption or drop it.
