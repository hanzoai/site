## Fixes (ordered, most important first)
- **Stop showing the same table twice in a row.** The hero card (4 rows: holder, class, shares, fully diluted) and the chapter card "A round, run first" are the same object about 400px apart. Either hand the hero table into the pinned stage (the hero card becomes the pinned card as you scroll, one object travelling), or give the chapters a different view of the round (for example the bar alone at full width, with one class lit per chapter). Today it reads as repetition, not progression.
- **Give the first desktop screen one solid ask.** When the hero ask (`data-cta="captable.hero"`, outline, 124x48 at y 662) is in view, the header ask drops to outline and nothing on the screen is filled. Make the hero "Try Hanzo" the solid fill while it is visible (MASTER part 4: "the solid fill sits in one place at a time: the hero ask, then the bar"), or stop marking it `thumb` so the header keeps its fill.
- **Close the dead space in the chapter scene and the open-source section.** Desktop: the first chapter text ("Share classes") sits about 400px below the section heading with an empty right column above it. Bring step 01 up to align with the top of the pinned card (y about 160). Open source: the right column ends at "Contact Sales →" and leaves about 280px of white with a stray hairline under it. Align the columns' ends, or let the repo card and the link rows share one baseline.
- **Tighten the pricing sheet.** The four plan cards (325x300 and 325x372, staggered) carry about 200px of empty fill between the mono label and the price, and the left column under "Simple, Transparent Pricing" is empty for about 600px. Shorten the cards to their content (about 180px, as on phone) and keep the staggered rhythm with smaller offsets, or put one real detail in each card's empty top (its first feature line from the panel, in Hanzo's words).
- **Phone chapters: hide the outgoing text under the pin.** At 375 the previous chapter's paragraph shows half-faded directly under the pinned table ("classes, with authorized shares, par value and voting rights."). Clip it at the card's bottom edge, or fade it fully before it reaches the card. Also bring CLS from `ct-pch-list` (0.019) down by reserving its height.
- Smaller: give the "Captable, Inc." inline link a hover answer (underline thickness or offset step, like the other links). Make the fork drawing read at rest (more of the branch drawn, or ink rather than grey). Avoid reading the chapter 06 sentence twice by letting the waterfall h2 take over as chapter 06 ends.
