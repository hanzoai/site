## What works
- The hero is a real product doing its job, by itself. Within 0.3 s the next message drops into the EVENTS stream, its cell goes white, and the wires draw down to analytics, audit and the queue group; one delivery goes dashed "↻ redelivered", then acks. It never stopped in my 6 s with no input. Nothing sits above the h1.
- Pointer detail that tells you something: hovering sequence 40 lights 40 to 43 and the bus's toolbar names "replay · from #40 → #43". That is exactly James's "the hover tells you a number".
- Pinned chapters do one in, one out: six chapters swap over one stage, each with its own small working demo (retention by age/count/bytes, ack every/all below/none with "duplicate collapsed", bucket history dots, request-reply landing on one instance, chunks filling, account walls). Progress dashes track the chapter.
- Colour: deck greys only, one black room, states by fill, dash and weight. No banned tells found.
- Desktop header: "Try Hanzo" is outlined over the hero, then the header ask becomes a solid "GitHub" after it. One ask, in Hanzo's live words.
- Press answers: the hero ask scales to .978 on press, lightens on hover (no opacity fade). Reduced motion: 0 running animations, settled fan-out shown. JS off: 0 hidden headings or paragraphs. CLS 0.006 desktop, 0.010 phone. No horizontal scroll at either width.
- Parity 82/82 text, 2/2 links; taste OK; unique OK.

## The 11 answers
1. **Sells in 5 s, moves within 1 s?** Yes. "Hanzo PubSub / The bus everything else rides / GitHub" plus a bus that publishes and fans out before you've finished reading. Composition note: the h1 sits low-left (top at ~490px) under a large empty black quadrant; the bus is the one big thing, which is right, but the left half feels unanchored.
2. **One in, one out, or a document?** One in, one out through the hero and the six pinned chapters. After that it becomes a document: a pale "Publish and Subscribe" room (small h2 stranded bottom-left beside the prompt), a row of four equal spec words, a code split, the close. There is also a nearly blank white screen between the code split and "Get PubSub" on desktop.

But several chapter panels are half empty (Request-Reply, Object Store and Accounts use the top half of a 420px outlined box), so the object reads small in a big frame.

The header keeps a solid black "Try Hanzo" for the whole page while the hero has a solid white "GitHub" (two solid fills on the first screen), and during the chapters the bottom bar says "GitHub" while the header says "Try Hanzo" — two different asks on screen at once. Scale: hero big, close grows "Get PubSub" to 64px; everything between is medium (32px chapter titles, 44px spec words in Air). No big moment in the middle third.

## To reach 8
- **Make the chapters this page's own, not the cloud template.** Keep the bus from the hero as the stage: pin the black bus and let each chapter change what the bus does (the stream cells shrink to a retention window, the ack switch makes redelivery appear on the wires, a bucket collapses a key's history into its last value, the queue group picks one instance, a big payload splits into chunks along the wire, an account wall drops between subscribers). One object carried down the page is what separates it from /realtime and /datastore, and gives the middle a big moment.
- **Fill the chapter frames or shrink them.** Request-Reply, Object Store and Accounts leave the lower half of a 420px outlined box empty. Size each panel to its content, or let the demo scale up while it is the moment and settle back.
- **Phone: one ask, one solid.** While the hero's white "GitHub" is on screen the header button must be outlined (as it already is on desktop), and once the bar says "GitHub" the header should not say a different thing solid. If the header label is shared-owned, at least outline the header on phone page-locally, and correct the card to say "desktop only" until then.
- **Phone hero: let the fan-out be seen.** At 375 the wires are a single 30px stub between the stream and the subscriber rows; the signature disappears. Give the wires room (a three-branch drawing above the three rows, or the rows side by side as on desktop).
- **Anchor the quiet room.** "Publish and Subscribe" at 26px sits alone bottom-left beside the dark prompt; lift it to the top of the room with the prompt, and remove the near-empty white screen between the code split and the close.
- **Desktop hero composition.** Top-align the h1 block with the bus's toolbar (or centre both) so the left half isn't 400px of empty black above a small title.
