# /extension — James-eye review
7 Nov, round 2, walked at 1470x801 and 375x812 (plus 320 and reduced motion) with Playwright Chromium: frames at 0, 1 and 3 s with no input, scrolled one screen at a time with ~1.5 s dwell, hovered and pressed on desktop, tapped on phone, console collected. A walk, not a source read.

Verdict: 6/10

First complaint: "It's the same browser window over and over. Hero, the read-a-page box, the browser list, the use cases: it's that little Chrome frame with the mountain film every time. And on my phone the hero text is sitting on top of the step numbers. Show me one thing, then a different thing."

## The 11 answers
1. **First screen in 5 s, motion in 1 s?** Mostly yes. At 0 s the h1 is still masked and only the lede, ask and card show. By 1 s the h1 has cut in and the card has fanned into a three-window stack. The card runs on its own (01/05 progress bar, film loop, Chrome/Firefox/Safari chips, pause button). It sells "your browser, as a tool" quickly. At 375 the card starts at y≈330 and only its tab strip and film show above the fold.
2. **One in, one out, or a document?** Half and half. "How it works" (black room, a rail that draws through six stops) and the pinned three-panel stage (connectors star, read-as-text, installs-from-a-file) each replace one panel. After that the page turns back into a document: browser list, then tabs, then a downloads card, then the closing CTA, with dead white gaps of ~250 px between the browser list and the use cases and again before "Get started" (desktop s06, s07).
3. **A real visual in every section?** Yes, but the same one. The browser frame with the Hanzo film card shows up in the hero, the "Read a page as text" panel, the "Built for every major browser" aside and the use-case tab panel. The use-case mocks are grey skeleton bars ("NETWORK REQUESTS" with five grey lines, "CONSOLE MESSAGES" with three), which looks lazy. The zip list shows up twice ("Installs from a file" and "Get started").
4. **One ask in the header, nothing floating, scale rhythm?** Desktop: the header ask changes from "Try Hanzo" to "Download Extension" once you scroll past the hero. Good. Phone: the header still says "Try Hanzo" all the way down. The page's ask never reaches the phone header. Nothing page-owned floats over content. Scale mostly has rhythm (h1 52, sections 21–26, mono captions 12). But "How it works" and the stage headings rest at 32 px, which is off the 26 px resting size, and the browser list puts **four equal outline buttons** in one column (Download for Chrome / Firefox / Edge / All Extension Builds). That breaks "never 3 or more equal buttons".
5. **Hover and press answer, no jumps?** Mostly. The hero ask darkens and presses at about 0.98, then shows a spinner, but on an external GitHub link the spinner stays on and the label is gone. Browser rows get a quiet room fill and the button fills on hover. Rail stops take a ring on hover and swap the caption on click. The tabs move a white pill. "See the spec" opens a right sheet with a scrim, and Esc closes it. The hover details never *tell* you anything (no size, no preview). The browser-row button hover is just a fill.
6. **Overlap, font swap, layout shift?** **Overlap at 375 and 320.** In the hero card, panel 02 "No port to open" runs its last line ("machine can dial in.") straight through the 01–05 step labels (m-hero-card, m-tap-firefox). At 320 the same happens on panel 01. Zip names break mid-word at 375 ("hanzo-ai-chrome-" / "v1.9.31.zip"). CLS on load was 0.0002, and there was no visible font swap. There is no horizontal page scroll at 375 or 320 (scrollWidth equals clientWidth). Elements that run off the edge are clipped stack windows and shared header chrome. One hydration-attribute mismatch appeared on the very first cold load (root layout → Smooth → MotionConfig → OpenerProvider…, diff truncated) and did not come back in five further fresh loads. I cannot place it, so it is noted, not counted. The cookie bar and the dev "Issue" badge are shared chrome. On phone the cookie bar covers a third of the screen until it is dismissed.
7. **Which reference, and is its motion there?** Apple product page: a pinned stage where one panel changes, the product as hero, a persistent header ask on desktop. That part is there. The synchronized.studio card-to-page open is not there (the spec opens as a side sheet). Nothing from osply or hamol (asymmetry, pointer-aware detail). There is no pointer tilt on the window stack.
8. **Different from neighbours?** The hero medium (a self-running browser stack with browser chips) is its own. The page body after the stage is the generic product-page kit: a two-column list, tabs, a downloads card, a centred CTA.
9. **18 parts?** Not verified from a card (not read). From the walk: there is a sound toggle in the header. Reduced motion shows the final state (h1, stack and rail all settled). The sheet uses Esc. The tabs and rail are buttons. States: the hero ask's loading state never ends on an external link. The mobile ask (part 4) is missing in the header.
10. **Banned tells?** Four equal buttons in one block. Skeleton-bar placeholder "UI" reads as a lazy look. I found no hue, gradient text, Inter or eyebrow above a headline: "WHAT YOU WOULD USE IT FOR" sits under its h2, and CONNECTORS/READ/BUILD sit under the body.
11. **Copy untouched?** It reads as Hanzo's. One flag: "How it works" stops 03 and 04 show the identical caption ("The extension talks to the local Hanzo daemon through a native messaging host over a Unix socket…"). Either the copy is mapped to the wrong stop or a stop has no caption of its own. Check against `content/pages`.

## Fixes, in order
1. **Kill the phone overlap in the hero card.** Let the panel body grow, or clamp the text area to the longest panel at 375 and 320, so the 01–05 labels never sit under text. Re-check all five panels at 320, 375 and 1470.
2. **Stop repeating the browser frame.** Keep it in the hero. Give "Read a page as text", the browser list aside and the use-case tabs their own objects: the actual returned page-text, console and network output as typed or streaming lines (real Hanzo strings), a browser-logo row that answers the "You are on Chrome" detection, and so on. Replace every grey skeleton bar with real content.
3. **Break up the four equal outline buttons** in the browser list. Make the detected browser's row the one filled ask. Turn the others into quiet text links with the file size as the hover detail (e.g. "956 KB").
4. **Put the page's ask in the phone header.** "Download Extension" should replace "Try Hanzo" after the hero at 375 too, as it does on desktop.
5. **Close the dead gaps** after the browser list and after the use-case tabs (~250 px of empty white each on desktop). Let one section hand over to the next.
6. **Remove the duplicate zip list.** Show the three files once (the pinned "Installs from a file" panel already does it well), or make the "Get started" card open the list.
7. **Fix the hero ask's endless spinner.** On an external download link, return to the label after the click (or after a short timeout).
8. **Bring 32 px stage and "How it works" headings back to 26 px** at rest. Let only the moment grow.
9. **Fix the rail caption mapping** so stops 03 and 04 each show their own sentence, and make the rail's dot states consistent (filled = passed, ring = current, hollow = ahead). Right now it mixes filled, large and ringed out of order.
10. **Add one pointer moment on desktop:** a 2.5° tilt on the hero window stack. Let the Firefox and Safari chips visibly change the front window's chrome. Today Firefox looks like Chrome. Only Safari changes the toolbar.
11. **Don't break filenames mid-token on phone.** Shrink the mono size or let the size column wrap below the name.
