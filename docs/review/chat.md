Evidence: own Playwright walks (/tmp/claude-0/critic-chat/j/*.png, deviceScaleFactor 1), timing and hover probes, plus the motion.mjs film strips in shots/chat/ (d-sheet-01..18, p-sheet-01..12, filmed 16:13). All timings are from the dev server, so load-time numbers come out slow.

## The 11 answers
1. **Sells in 5 s / moves within 1 s?** Partly. The h1 "Ask anything. Enso picks the model." plus the live app is the right pitch. On a warm reload, the screen stayed byte-identical (header only, rest blank) until 3.1 s at desktop and 2.2 s on phone. The h1 is already in the DOM (elementFromPoint hits `span.v2a-line-in` at 945 ms) but isn't painted until JS runs. With JS off, the full hero paints at once, so the blank is caused by the JS-gated reveal. After the cut-in, the 711px chat window stays an empty white pane for about 2 to 3 s (film d-sheet-01, 6 frames) while "How do black holes form?" types into the small composer at its bottom edge. The run then streams two exchanges and settles on a replay icon at about 12 to 15 s, longer than the 6 to 10 s in MASTER 4.1.
2. **One in, one out?** Mostly yes. The "A chat that does the work" h2 scrubs from 84px down to 32px. A pinned stage swaps six chapters with a tab rail and an "0N / 06" counter. The Enso section has a sticky media card that swaps to the active model's film as you scroll the list. The end goes flat: list, Band, footer. On phone the six chapters stack as plain cards.
3. **Real visual in every section?** Yes, on the four screens that exist: the live app, the pinned stage (Enso orbit film, code block, files, voice, memory, Chat/Dev toggle), model films, and the Band's large type. The "Chat, then Dev" panel is mostly an empty dark box with one bubble (d-scroll-07).
4. **One ask, sticky, nothing floating; scale rhythm?** Header ask is right: outlined "Try Hanzo" at the hero, then "Open Hanzo Chat" as you scroll. Weight is right too: the hero primary is the only solid fill, "Read the docs" is a text link. Two problems. The cookie banner (760x69 at y 716) sits over the hero app's composer at 1470. On phone it is 343x203 and covers the whole demo on first visit. Also, the header ask turns grey (looks disabled) while the Band ask is on screen (d-scroll-11). Scale rhythm is good: h1 52, h2 grows to 84 and settles to 32, chapter h3 32, Band 88.
5. **Every hover and press answers?** Most do. Hero primary: background #0a0a0a to #2c2c2e, press scale 0.98. "Read the docs": underline darkens, press +1px. Demo model rows, Copy and the chapter tabs change colour and scale on press. Enso list rows nudge, show an arrow and swap the card. Two failures. The header "Try Hanzo" hover changes only opacity (1 to 0.88), which is the banned "button fades on hover". "Open Hanzo Chat" has no hand-off state: it navigates to hanzo.ai/login in under 250 ms with no visible change, though MASTER asks for one.
6. **Overlaps, font swap, layout shift?** CLS 0.0012 desktop, 0.0005 phone. No horizontal scroll at 375. No font swap seen. Overlaps: the cookie banner over the demo, as in 4. On phone the chat recents row cuts mid-word ("Black holes and a…"). Chapter-2 code lines are cut with ellipses ("retry(fn, …", "} c…"). The first tab label is cut at 1470 ("The right model, every ti…"). On every load the console logs a hydration-mismatch error, and the dev "1 Issue" badge shows it.
7. **Which reference, and is its motion there?** Apple product page: pinned scenes, a scrubbed headline, a sub-nav with a persistent ask. Hanzo model cards: films in the Enso list. That motion really is there. synchronized.studio's card-open-into-page is not, and nothing here opens.
8. **Different from neighbours?** Yes. The hero is a split layout (copy left, live app right) where home has the app centred low. The signature is the six-chapter pinned stage.
9. **18 parts covered?** No. MASTER 4.1 for /chat asks for "the existing plan cards, Download, Band". Plan cards and Download are missing. Of the Launch screens, proof and the objection strip are also missing. The page is hero, chapters, model list, Band, footer.
10. **Banned tells?** The header ask fades on hover (opacity 0.88). Nothing else seen: no gradient text, no green, no eyebrow over the h1, monochrome palette.
11. **Copy untouched?** No invented claims seen. The headline, sub and model lines match Hanzo's own phrasing. The demo scripts ("Code that works, somehow", "Sourdough starter" and others) could not be traced to Hanzo's source from the browser alone. The owner should confirm they are real Hanzo content.

## Fixes (ranked)
1. **Page structure, both widths:** add the existing plan cards and the Download block between the Enso list and the Band (MASTER 4.1 /chat), then proof and the objection strip.
2. **Hero first paint, both widths:** the h1 and app stay unpainted for 2 to 3 s until JS runs. Paint them from the server and let the line cut-in run without hiding content before hydration. Something must move within 1 s.
3. **Hero demo, 1470:** the chat pane is empty for about 2 to 3 s while the question types in a small composer at the bottom. Start with the first user bubble, or type within 300 ms. Shorten the run to 6 to 10 s (one exchange, or a faster second one).
4. **Cookie banner, first visit:** at 1470 it covers the app composer (y 716 to 785). At 375 it covers the whole demo (203px tall). Make it a compact corner card that clears the hero object, or delay it until after the demo settles.
5. **Header "Try Hanzo" hover, 1470:** opacity-only fade (0.88), a banned tell. Change background or border instead, and keep the scale-0.97 press. Stop the scrolled header ask going grey and disabled-looking while the Band ask is visible.
6. **"Open Hanzo Chat", both widths:** add the hand-off state MASTER asks for. Today it navigates instantly with no feedback.
7. **Hydration-mismatch console error, both widths:** fix the server/client attribute mismatch logged on every load.
8. **Phone clipping, 375:** chapter-2 code lines end in ellipses (wrap or scale the code). The recents chips row cuts mid-word with no fade edge.
9. **Tab rail, 1470:** "The right model, every ti…" is truncated. Widen that tab or let tabs size to their labels.
10. **"Chat, then Dev" panel, 1470:** it is a near-empty dark box with one bubble. Let the toggle actually flip to Dev with a diff landing, so the panel has an object.
11. **Reduced motion, 1470:** the static final frame is scrolled so the first question bubble is cut off at the top. Show the conversation from its start.
