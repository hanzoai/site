# /flow — James-eye review
7 Nov, round 2, walked at 1470x801 and 375x812 (isMobile, hasTouch) with Playwright Chromium: frames at 0/1/3 s untouched, wheel-scrolled a screen at a time with 1.5 s dwell, hovered/pressed every main control on desktop, tapped on phone, Copy tested with clipboard permission granted, overflow at 375 and 320, prefers-reduced-motion pass.

Verdict: 7/10

First complaint: "It's the same six boxes four times. Hero, then the same pipeline again, then again on black, then again as little numbered dots at the bottom. One thing in, one thing out, not the same thing over and over. And on my phone the good bit turns into a list of paragraphs."

## The 11 answers
1. **First screen sells, and moves in under 1 s: yes.** The hero is a live node canvas that runs itself: 01 Document loader lights, the wire draws to 02, "in a file" turns to "out documents", and the black playground card counts 01/06 to 02/06 with a pause button. At t0 the h1 has not cut in yet; by 1 s "Hanzo Flow", the lede, Open Flow and Documentation are there. Clear and premium. The hero headline is 52px and the nodes are medium, so the rhythm is right.
2. **One in, one out: mostly on desktop, no on phone.** Desktop "The canvas is not the ceiling" is a proper pinned stage: the graph builds one node per step (01 to 06) while the right column swaps chapters (The library, Open any node, Step through it, Multi-agent, Keys stay out of the flow), with a 01–06 scrub bar. That is the best moment on the page. But (a) about 250px of blank white sits between the hero and the pinned stage, and the right column sits empty while the left one is already pinned (desk-s01). (b) On the phone the stage collapses into a sticky 01–06 strip over a stack of paragraphs, which reads like a document. The strip works, but the text scrolls under it and stays half-visible ("02 / 06" peeks out above the chips).
3. **A real object in every section: yes, but it is the same object.** Hero canvas, pinned canvas, black "Step through it" playground canvas (same six nodes, same received/returned) and the close band's 01–06 chain are all one pipeline. The facts (Python, REST, :7860, MIT) are wired cards that open into a sheet with a node preview, which is good. Get Flow and Run it locally have the agent prompt and the typing terminal, which is fine. There is no Hanzo media anywhere (JAMES 6), and no block of 4+ real visuals. If Flow has product UI screenshots or film, the page needs it.
4. **One ask; scale rhythm: mostly.** The sticky header ask becomes "Open Flow" (ink) once you pass the hero on desktop, which is right. Nothing floats over content apart from the shared cookie card. But on phone the header ask stays "Try Hanzo" for the whole page, while desktop says "Open Flow". In the body, the asks repeat: Open Flow + Documentation in the hero and again in the close band; Deploy to Cloud twice; View on GitHub twice; the hanzoai chip twice; Documentation three times. Scale: section heads are 26px, but "Run it locally first" (44px) and the close band headline (44px) land back to back, so the last two screens are both "big". The facts row shows four big numerals at once (Python, REST, :7860, MIT), and stepping their sizes and offsets helps a little.
5. **Every hover and press answers: desktop yes, phone partly.** Desktop: the hero nodes brighten their border on hover and press to scale 0.98. Open Flow darkens. Documentation underlines (the computed style showed no change because the underline is on a child, but it does answer). Play/Pause and the step chips invert, and press scales to 0.97. Fact cards open into a sheet. Copy shows "✓ Copied" and the clipboard held the exact prompt. No layout jumps seen. Phone: tapping the hero nodes and the playground prev/next/Play changed nothing visible in my frames, while the step chips invert. Either the hero nodes are not tappable on phone, or the answer is too quiet. Either way it reads as dead.
6. **Overlaps, font swap, layout shift: one edge issue.** No horizontal scroll at 375 or 320. The only elements past the edge are the shared header and footer. At 320 the hero graph nodes run to x≈3px, past the 16px gutter, so the nodes touch the screen edge. On desktop the "Step through it" graph clips 06 Output parser against the right edge of its frame (desk-s08). The h1 font is Zen and nothing swapped. The red "Issue" badge appeared only when a screenshot fired before hydration. A clean load with no early screenshot shows no badge and no console errors, so I am not counting it against the page.
7. **Reference: apple.com product page (pinned scene, one panel changes) and motion.dev (staged loading, the typing terminal).** Both are actually there on desktop. The synchronized.studio card-opens-into-page shows up a little in the fact sheet. Nothing from osply/hamol (asymmetry, pointer-aware detail) beyond the hover borders.
8. **Different from neighbours: yes, in medium.** A self-running node graph as the hero is its own idea. It is undercut by using the graph four times.
9. **18 parts:** not verified from the coverage card (not read). Visibly: there is sub-nav/ask behaviour, a hero, a narrative, proof (the facts), conversion (Get Flow, prompt, terminal), a close and states (Copied). Missing: any trust/proof beyond the four facts, and any media.
10. **Banned tells: none fatal.** Monochrome throughout, with no hued colours in main (a scan of every element's colour found 0 with chroma). No eyebrow above the h1 or h2. Not Inter. No gradient text. Things to watch: the facts row is 4 equal-ish cards in a row (staggered, so not the banned "3 equal boxes"). Small mono labels ("playground", "received/returned") are grey and allowed.
11. **Copy: untouched as far as I can tell, but it contradicts itself.** Get Flow shows "License: Apache-2.0" while the facts card says "MIT · Self-hosted". One of them is wrong. Check it against Hanzo's own copy (an unverifiable claim fails the page).

## Fixes, in order
1. **Kill the repeats.** Keep the self-running graph in the hero and the pinned build-up stage, and make them visibly different (hero = finished graph running; stage = graph being built). Turn the black "Step through it" section into something other than the same six nodes: the playground inspector alone, big, with one node's received/returned streaming, no canvas. Drop the 01–06 dot chain from the close band.
2. **Phone pinned stage:** give the phone a real one-in-one-out scene. Pin the mini graph (not just number chips) and swap one chapter at a time in a fixed box under it, instead of stacking all six paragraphs. Hide the text that scrolls behind the sticky strip.
3. **Close the dead gap** between the hero and "The canvas is not the ceiling" (desk-s01). Bring the first chapter text in with the pinned canvas so the right column is never empty.
4. **Phone header ask:** swap to "Open Flow" past the hero, the same as desktop.
5. **De-duplicate asks:** one Deploy to Cloud, one View on GitHub, one hanzoai chip. The close band keeps Open Flow only, plus Documentation as a link.
6. **Scale at the end:** settle "Run it locally first" to 26px (section rest size) so only the close band headline is big. Or keep it big and drop the close band headline to 32px. Not both at 44.
7. **Phone taps:** hero nodes and the playground prev/next/Play must visibly answer on tap (invert the node or step the playground).
8. **Edges:** keep the hero graph inside the 16px gutter at 320. Stop 06 Output parser clipping at the right edge of the "Step through it" canvas at 1470.
9. **Licence contradiction:** confirm Apache-2.0 vs MIT against Hanzo's source copy and show only the true one.
10. **Media:** if Hanzo has Flow product UI shots or film, add one block of 4+ real visuals (for example, the real canvas UI) in place of the third graph.
