7 Nov. Walked at 1470x801 and 375x812 (phone: isMobile, hasTouch), plus 320 and reduced motion, with Playwright: frames at 0, 1 and 3 s untouched, wheel-scrolled a screen at a time with 1.5 s dwell, hovered and pressed every main control, tapped on phone. Frames are in /tmp/claude-0/critic-flow/.

First complaint: "It's the same diagram three times. Six white boxes in the hero, then the same six boxes again on the left while I scroll, then the same six boxes again in a black box. And there's a red error thing in the corner. Where's the moment?"

But the h1 sits at about 67% of the screen (baseline about 560px of 801), under 330px of canvas, and the dark playground card is pushed into the middle of the graph between Model and Output parser. You read the boxes before the name. On phone the h1 is at the top but 110px of empty dot grid sits between the ask and the graph.

But then the dark "Step through it" room plays the *same* six nodes again with the same received/returned readout. ## Fixes, in order
1. **Kill the red "1 Issue" badge.** The console shows a hydration mismatch ("A tree hydrated but some attributes of the server rendered HTML didn't match"). Something in the hero graph or playground renders different attributes on server and client, probably a random value, time-based value or `window` branch in the initial state. Render the initial state deterministically and start the run in an effect.
2. **Stop showing the same graph three times.** Keep the hero graph and the dark playground, and replace the chapter section's left-hand graph with one that *grows*. Option A: chapter 01 shows only the Document loader node at large size, and each chapter adds the next node and wire until the full flow stands at 06. Option B: drop the dark-room repeat and move its controls (prev/play/next, the 01–06 tabs, the big received → returned readout) into the pinned chapter stage. Either way, one graph should become the next, not reprint.
3. **Phone chapters:** do not print the 6-node graph six times. Use one sticky mini-graph (about 200px tall, top under the header) whose lit node follows the chapter in view, or show a single node card per chapter at true size.
4. **Re-compose the desktop hero.** Lift the h1 so its baseline sits near 38% of 100svh (about 300px), with h1, lede and asks on the left 5 of 13 columns. Let the graph live on the right 8 columns and behind, not as a band over the headline. Take the black playground card out of the middle of the graph: dock it under the graph's last node or drop it from the hero, since the dark room already does this job.
5. **One solid fill on phone.** While the hero "Open Flow" is in view, make the header ask quiet (outlined), then solid once the hero ask leaves, as desktop already does. Use the page's ask ("Open Flow") in the phone header too, not "Try Hanzo". Fix the 320 header so the ask is not clipped.
6. **Fact row rhythm.** Python / REST / :7860 / MIT are four equal 64px words. Make one big at a time: the hovered or in-view card grows to 64px and the rest rest at 32px, or step them 64 / 44 / 32 / 26 along the wire as it draws. Remove the dead 130px of white under the row.
7. **Node cards: drop the icon-in-a-rounded-box.** Use a bare 16px glyph or the mono index ("01") in ink. Widen nodes (or allow 2 lines) so "Document loader" and "question and chunks" never truncate at 1470 or 375.
8. **Dark room entrance.** Remove the 120px empty black band between the "flow.hanzo.ai / playground" bar and the canvas. Never show dark nodes on a light #f5f5f5 canvas mid-scroll: switch the canvas and nodes together, or fade the canvas to black before the nodes darken.
9. **Closing.** After "Run it locally first" add the page's closing `Band` (its own words with the one "Open Flow" ask), and remove the empty white after "View on GitHub" so the footer follows with no gap.
10. **Python sheet.** The opened Python card is a 1040px modal with the right half empty. Give it the same right-hand object as REST (a small node card showing a Python component with its in/out ports), or make the sheet 640px wide.
11. **Hover colour.** The primary's hover must use a deck value, #262626 or #171717, not #2c2c2e. Add a visible "Copied" state (tick and word, `role="status"`, about 1.5 s) to both Copy buttons.
12. **Chapter captions.** "04 RETRIEVER" under "Multi-agent" reads as a mislabel. Keep the step number but set the node name as the graph's highlight only, or drop the caption under the heading.
13. **Reduced motion.** Under reduced motion the chapter headings below the active one stay ghosted at low opacity (rm-s4.png). Show every chapter at full ink.
