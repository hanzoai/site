# /idv — James-eye review
7 Nov, round 3, walked at 1470x801 and 375x812 (isMobile, hasTouch) with Playwright: frames at 0/1/3 s untouched, wheel-scrolled a screen at a time with 1.5 s dwell through the stacked cards and the pinned forge room, hovered and pressed every control in main on desktop, tapped on phone, a prefers-reduced-motion pass, and overflow checks at 375 and 320.

Verdict: 6/10

First complaint: "Why is the next card sliding over the one I'm still reading? The words get cut in half and I never even see the little picture on the card underneath. And then it's six grey boxes that all look the same. That's a document with a shadow on it."

## The 11 answers
1. **First screen sells in 5 s, moves within 1 s?** Half. Something moves straight away: the vendor demo runs by itself (the pill glides Jumio, Onfido, Plaid, the rest, and the call line draws) and the lede reveals word by word. But the left half of the first screen is a nine-line paragraph that is still half grey at 3 s, so it reads as a wall of text that hasn't loaded. The lede also breaks after "written," onto a new line (that break isn't in Hanzo's copy). On the phone the whole first screen is that paragraph. The demo starts at the fold, and its tab pill wraps, leaving "the rest" alone on a second line.
2. **One in, one out, or a document?** Mostly a document. The six feature cards stack as sticky sheets, which is the right idea, but each card is the same grey box with text on the left and a small white tile on the right. Six identical beats in a row. The forge room after them is the one real scene.
3. **A real visual object in every section?** Yes, but they are small and quiet: the "4" figure with the call list, approved struck through to refuses, a hash, a three-dot polling line, a KMS tree, an IDV/Risk two-column list. On the phone the next card covers cards 1, 2 and 5 before their visual is ever fully on screen.
4. **One ask in the sticky header, nothing floating, scale rhythm?** Mostly. The header ask is Try Hanzo at the top, then becomes View on GitHub on desktop, but stays Try Hanzo on the phone, so the two widths ask for different things. Nothing floats over content. Scale has some rhythm (52px h1, 26px card heads, the 64px+ "4" and "refuses", a 44px closing head), but every card's head is the same size, so the middle third is flat.
5. **Every hover and press answers, no jumps?** Mostly. Buttons darken and press to 0.98, tabs fill, Copy confirms. Problems: the Jumio tab's box changes from 80 to 78px on hover (a small jump). Deploy to Cloud has no hover change on its own box. In the forge room's segmented control, two segments are lit at once after a press: approved and An empty signing secret on the phone, approved and a forgery on desktop 900 ms after the click.
6. **Overlaps, font swap, layout shift?** Yes, overlap. The incoming sticky card slices the one under it mid-line and mid-glyph. On desktop "refuses" is cut through its middle, the 02 body stops at "until the parsing", and in the reduced-motion pass the heading "A verdict, or nothing" is cut in half. The header turns black before the black room arrives, so a dark band sits over the white "Paste and ship" heading. View on GitHub pops into the hero between 0 s and 1 s. No font swap. No hydration diff or console error from this page, including on the reduced-motion load.
7. **Which reference, and is its motion there?** Apple: a pinned scene where one panel changes (the forge room runs approved, a forgery, then the empty signing secret, with progress ticks) and a self-running product demo. That part is real. Nothing from synchronized.studio (no card opens into its page), and nothing from osply or hamol (no pointer-aware detail, no confident asymmetry). The stacked cards feel generic, not referenced.
8. **Different from its neighbours?** Partly. The forge room, where you forge a webhook, is this page's own idea. The six stacked grey cards with numbered `01 / 06` captions are a shared template look.
9. **All 18 parts covered?** Not fully. The conversion path is clear (GitHub, Deploy to Cloud, agent prompt), and states and the reduced-motion end state are there. Missing: media (no real Hanzo visual), pointer motion, and the phone layout of the forge room. That room is taller than the screen, so on the phone you can't see the choice and its result together while it changes.
10. **Banned tells?** Overlap or cut text (above), which fails on its own. No hue. Two off-ramp greys with a blue cast: `rgb(44,44,46)` on the button hover and `rgb(210,210,215)` on the Copy label. Neither is a token. No eyebrow above a headline: the `01 / 06` and GET STARTED WITH IDV captions sit under their heads. No Inter, no gradient text.
11. **Copy untouched?** Mostly. Every label traces to idv.json or idv.html, though many are fragments lifted from sentences. Two problems. The forge-room headline "…an HMAC under a key everybody knows is one anybody can compute." is the tail of a body sentence promoted to a heading, with an ellipsis Hanzo didn't write. The lede has a line break after "written," that isn't in the source. The forge room also pairs fragments wrongly: the approved state shows a cross with "anything else is polled from the provider directly…", and the reduced-motion end state shows a tick with "A webhook whose signature we can check is accepted" next to a forged body. The story doesn't read.

## Fixes, in order
1. **Stop the stacked cards from cutting text.** Either give each card a solid top edge so the incoming card fully hides the previous one (with a clean 16px peek strip only, no sliced glyphs), or drop the stacking. On the phone, let each card's visual come fully into view before the next card arrives. Under reduced motion, unstack them completely.
2. **Break the six identical cards.** Keep the stack, but give each beat its own scale and layout. Make one card's object the big thing ("refuses" at 84px, or the HMAC hash typing in), and keep the others medium and small. Alternate the sides on the 8/5 split. Six grey boxes in one rhythm is the document James hates.
3. **Fix the forge room's segmented control:** one lit segment only, with the pill gliding at 300 ms ease-emphasis. Make the state and the result agree: the forged body gets a refusal, the signed body gets the tick, and an empty secret gets "a refusal, never a skip". On the phone, fit the room in one screen: put the two panels side by side compactly or show the callback panel only, with the choices above the result.
4. **Hero lede:** remove the stray break after "written,". Finish the word reveal within about 1.2 s of load, or tie it to the first 30% of the scroll, so the resting first screen isn't grey. On the phone, keep the vendor tabs on one line (shrink the padding or let them scroll sideways) and bring the demo above the fold under the h1.
5. **Header colour:** switch to the dark header only when the black room's top edge reaches it, not while white content is under it.
6. **One ask across widths:** the phone's header ask should become View on GitHub after the hero, the same as on desktop.
7. **Forge-room heading:** use the full sentence or an unaltered fragment from Hanzo's copy, with no ellipsis.
8. **Snap the off-token greys** (`#2c2c2e` hover, `#d2d2d7` Copy text) to the ramp: `#262626` and white 78%.
9. **Fix the hover jumps and dead hovers:** the tab width change from 80 to 78px on hover, and give Deploy to Cloud an arrow nudge or underline draw. Render the hero View on GitHub on the first paint so it doesn't pop in.
10. **Add one pointer-aware detail,** for example the vendor demo's call line following the hovered step, or the KMS tree lighting up on hover, so the motion isn't only time and scroll.

Not counted (shared chrome): the header's Try Hanzo is clipped at 320px; the cookie card was dismissed before judging.
