## The 11 answers
1. **First screen and early motion.** Partly yes. On desktop the vendor stage runs by itself within 1 s (Jumio, Onfido, Plaid, the rest, each with its own result card) and the lede inks in word by word, so the page is alive straight away. But the lede is a 10-line paragraph at 21px beside the stage, and at rest its last words ("cannot tell.") stay grey on desktop and at 375. On the phone the first screen shows only the h1, the GitHub button and the stage. The sentence that sells the product sits below the fold.
2. **One in, one out, or a document?** Halfway. The six sheets do stack and pin, and each new sheet slides over the last one. But an outgoing sheet empties its content and stays visible as a blank slab, 150 to 300px of plain graphite or grey (desktop at scroll positions 1978, 2338 and 2878). That reads as a dead panel, not a handover. All six sheets also use one template: a 26px title, "0N / 06", a 4-line grey paragraph and a small object, mirrored left and right, in four greys. On the phone the sheets are paragraph walls of 8 to 9 lines each.
3. **A real visual object in every section?** Thin. The objects are typographic and drawn from the copy: a big "4" with the four calls, "approved" struck through with "refuses", a hash, three polling dots, a KMS tree, and two columns of IDV against Risk. The four-call list in sheet 1 repeats the hero's list one screen later, and sheet 6 repeats it a third time. The forge is the real object and it is good. The closing contour art is quiet and fine.
4. **One ask, nothing floating, scale rhythm.** Nothing floats over content, and there is no pill bar on desktop. The header ask is shared chrome and not counted. Scale has some rhythm: the 52px h1, the "4" and "refuses" figures as the large moments, 26px sheet titles, and 11px mono labels. But the forge opens with a 32px, three-line headline beside a heavy two-panel console, so that screen is all big and heavy.
5. **Does every hover and press answer?** Mostly. The vendor tabs show a grey hover fill. That fill is rgba(128,128,128,.14), which is not a deck step. The hero GitHub button changes from ink to #262626 on hover and scales to 0.98 on press. Forge options: approved, a forgery, An empty signing secret and Reset each set aria-pressed correctly, and the result matches each one. "approved" (tampered body) refuses. "a forgery" (signature last char changed, struck through) refuses. An empty secret blanks both HMACs and shows "An empty signing secret is a refusal, never a skip". The untouched default shows a tick with "A webhook whose signature we can check is accepted". On the phone the tabs work on tap. The hover detail never tells you anything you didn't already know (no number, no preview).
6. **Overlaps, font swap, layout shift.** No overlap at 1470 or 375, and no horizontal overflow at 1470, 375 or 320. No font swap seen. One phone defect: at 375 the vendor rail's edge fade plus the pause button hide "the rest" ("the r…"). When "the rest" is selected, the fade washes out the active black pill. At 320, "Plaid" fades as well and "the rest" is gone. Console: 0 errors in any run, and no Next.js issue badge in normal or reduced-motion loads. Reduced motion shows the final state: the lede is fully inked, the dots are solid, and the forge is unpinned. Shared, not counted: under reduced motion the wheel is swallowed over the "Paste and ship" code card (the pre has overflow:auto with overscroll-behavior:contain), so the page stops scrolling at about y=2724 while the pointer is over it. This belongs in docs/requests/cloud2.md.
7. **Reference and its motion.** The pinned stacked sheets aim at apple.com product pages, and the forge is a scripted, state-driven demo. The Apple idea of one scene where the panel changes is undercut by the empty outgoing slabs. No synchronized.studio card-open, no count-up on the "4", and no headline line-mask cut-in on the sheet titles. Motion mix is good: idle (the vendor autoplay and the polling dots), time (the word-by-word lede), state (the tabs and the forge), and pointer (slight tilt on the hero stage). Scroll is not the only kind.
8. **Different from neighbours?** The hero medium (a self-running vendor switcher) and the forge console are this page's own. The six-sheet stack's single layout feels like a reusable cloud template. Neighbours were not walked this round.
9. **18 parts.** Hero, explanation, proof-in-object, developer path (Paste and ship), closing ask, footer: covered. Trust and proof are only the licence chip and GitHub. No states beyond the forge are shown, which is fine for this page. Nothing missing that the content offers.
10. **Banned tells.** No hue, no gradient text, no Inter, no glow, no eyebrow over an h1 or h2 ("GET STARTED WITH IDV" sits under "Get IDV", and "THE REST" labels a figure, not a headline). The tab hover fill is an invented grey step. "Clutter" is borderline: six explaining paragraphs, one per tile, plus a list repeated three times.
11. **Copy untouched?** Every visible label traces to idv.json or ui-strings ("Reset", "Pause", "Copy"). But the copy is re-used in ways that change its reading. The forge headline repeats sheet 3's sentence word for word, so it appears twice. "the rest" shows the true statement "refuse until their response parsing is written" struck through, which reads as if it were false. The forge result for approved and forgery is a mid-sentence fragment starting lower-case: "anything else is polled from the provider directly…".

## Fixes, in order
1. **Kill the empty slabs.** An outgoing sheet should scale back (to about 0.94) and dim, keeping its content, or tuck away to a 12 to 16px peeking edge. It must never be a 150 to 300px blank card above the incoming one. Check at scroll 1978, 2338 and 2878 at 1470x801.
2. **Break the six-sheet template.** Give the sheets at least three distinct shapes. For example: sheet 1 as a big "4" with a count-up and no repeated list; sheet 3 as a full-width hash that types in and then ticks; sheet 5 as the KMS tree drawn as line art in 800 to 1,400 ms. Drop the four-call list from sheet 1 and sheet 6, because the hero already shows it. On the phone, cut each sheet to the title plus the object first, with the paragraph after it.
3. **Finish the hero lede reveal.** At rest the last words must be ink, not grey, on desktop and phone. On the phone, move the lede's first sentence above the stage, or put the stage after the lede, so the first screen says what IDV is.
4. **Fix the phone vendor rail.** All four vendors must be visible at 375 and 320. Wrap them to two rows, or shrink the padding and put the pause button on its own line. Never let the fade sit over the selected pill.
5. **Forge copy placement.** Don't repeat sheet 3's sentence as the forge headline. Use "A webhook has to prove it is one" (Hanzo's own title) and leave the sentence as the empty-secret result only. For approved and forgery, show the result as a short state word from the copy ("refuses") with the polling sentence as a secondary line, not a lower-case fragment as the headline result.
6. **Don't strike through a true statement.** In the hero's "the rest" card, keep "refuse until their response parsing is written" in ink. Strike only the thing refused ("approved"), as sheet 2 already does.
7. **Phone forge order.** At 375 and 320, show "The provider you chose" and the callback data first, then the three options, then the verdict. Right now the verdict comes before you see what is being checked.
8. **Calm the forge's first screen.** Drop the forge headline to the 26px section step, and let the black room enter as a rounded card inside the gutters that opens to full width as it is reached (MASTER 3.1).
9. **Make hover say something.** On vendor tabs, show the vendor's mode (webhook, polling or refuse) as a hint on hover. Swap the rgba(128,128,128,.14) fill for a deck step (black 5 or 6% on light).
10. **Add the missing authored moves.** A line-mask cut-in on the sheet titles, and a count-up on the "4".
11. Shared, for docs/requests/cloud2.md, not counted here: the CodeBlock pre (overscroll-behavior: contain) traps the wheel under reduced motion.
