## The 11 answers

The first app lands by 1.0 s, the second by 2.0 s, and the clock bar fills. Phone: h1 40px, lede and ask, with the canvas starting at y 529. At 300 ms the page is still blank white under the header, but it is filled by 1 s.
2. **One in, one out?** No. Three rooms: hero (734px), catalogue (2,438px on desktop), dark "Not here?" band (590px). The catalogue is the problem: nine category heads and 81 same-size grey tiles in a 4-column grid, scrolled like a document for about three screens. Only the side tray stays sticky. Nothing enters or leaves as you scroll; only the progress hairline moves.
3. **A real object in every section?** Partly. Hero: the dock plus a live three-place canvas (good). Catalogue: the sticky "Hanzo Auto" tray mirrors the flow, but it is small (270x403) beside a large emoji grid. The emoji grid is the visual, and greyscale emoji read as clip art. Band: the HTTP to credential node pair is static (identical shots 1.2 s apart).
4. **One ask, sticky after the hero? Scale rhythm?** There is one solid in the hero ("All Integrations", ink). It jumps to `#all` (scrollY 794), so it is an in-page scroll rather than a destination. The band's "Request Integration" is the only solid there. The header carries "Try Hanzo" (shared chrome) and nothing floats over content. Scale: the h1 is the only big thing on the page. The catalogue is all medium-small tiles (209x63) and 15px heads, and the band head is 26px. Nothing grows into a moment after the hero.
5. **Every hover and press answers?** Mostly. A catalogue tile hover lifts, turns white, brings back colour and shows a "+". A click drops the app into the tray with a place number (1, 2, 3). ## Fixes (ordered, most important first)
- **Break the catalogue wall (the 2,438px 4-column grid of 81 tiles).** Make it one thing in, one thing out. One option: show one category at a time in a pinned stage, where scrolling advances the category, the chips act as the index, the tray stays the object, and the tiles of the outgoing category leave as the next arrive. Another option: collapse the default view to the 12 "Popular" plus chips and open the rest on demand. Either way, a visitor should never scroll past nine uniform grids.
- **Stop the demo stomping on the visitor.** The first time someone taps a dock or catalogue tile, stop the auto-drop loop and keep their flow in both the canvas and the tray. Show their three places filled, run the packet once, and end at rest. Resume the loop only after a clear reset. Right now a tap is appended into the loop's half-built flow and is wiped on the next cycle.
- **Phone "Most reached for": put the names back.** The 12 tiles are 80px emoji-only squares with no label. Show the name under each tile at 13-14px (or use 3 across) so a tile is not an icon alone.
- **Give the page one scale moment after the hero.** Everything below the h1 is medium or small: tiles of 209x63, 15px category heads, and a 26px band head. Let the flow you build grow when its third place fills, for example by enlarging the tray into a full-width run with a 64px count or a "3 / 3" figure, then settle back. Also make the band's HTTP to credential pair draw itself in (a hairline in 900 ms) as it is reached, since it is currently static. The dark band should enter as a rounded card inside the gutters that opens to full width (MASTER 3.1), not as a hard full-bleed edge.
- **Separate it from /auto.** Both heroes are node boxes on a horizontal line inside a framed card with a pause button. Give /automations its own medium: drop the outer grey frame (box in box) and let apps travel from the dock into the flow along a visible path, or lay the canvas out as a non-linear board, so it does not read as /auto's run list again. Also fix the coverage card claims above (phone dock names, header label, rail snap set to `proximity`).
