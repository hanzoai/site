# /functions — James-eye review
7 Nov, round 2, walked at 1470x801 and 375x812 (and 320) with Playwright from node. Frames at 0, 1 and 3 s with no input; wheel-scrolled one screen at a time with a 1.5 s dwell; hovered and pressed all 28 controls in main on desktop; tapped the composer on phone; checked overflow, layout shift, reduced motion and a clean-load console.

Verdict: 7/10

First complaint: "The top half is lovely: the code types itself, the pinned chapters change one at a time. Then it goes back to being a document. The use cases are four cards of text stacked on top of each other with an empty column next to them, and on my phone the whole story is just six lots of words and diagrams, one after another."

## The 11 answers
1. **Sells in 5 s, moves within 1 s?** Yes. The handler.js stage types itself within the first second, the badge flips from editing to deployed, a trigger lights up and replicas fill the node grid. "Hanzo Functions / Functions that run on Kubernetes" with one black ask reads at once. There is a pause control. On phone the stage starts just under the ask, which is fine at 375; at 320 the first screen has no object.
2. **One in, one out, or a document?** On desktop it is mixed. The pinned 6-chapter stage is properly one thing in and one out (01–06, the panel morphs, the rail fills). The composer is a real state change. After that it slides back into a document: "Common Use Cases" is four stacked sheets of heading, explaining sentence and three bullet rows, and the left column sits empty for about 2,000 px. On phone the pin is undone, so all six chapters become text-then-diagram blocks in a row, which is a document.
3. **A real visual in every section?** Mostly. The hero has the live demo, the chapters each have their own diagram, and the composer has a live handler card. The use cases have no object at all: just big 01–04 numerals and text. The closing black room is a list and a prompt box, with about 250 px of dead black under "Related capabilities".
4. **One ask in the header, nothing floating, scale rhythm?** There is one ask, "Try Hanzo", in the sticky header and in the hero. Nothing of the page's own floats over content; the cookie bar and the shared header are chrome. Scale is mostly medium with no rhythm. The chapter h3s are 32px, bigger than the section h2s at 26px, so the hierarchy is upside down. "Get Functions" jumps to 44px. Nothing grows or shrinks as you act, except the 01–04 numerals in the sheets.
5. **Every hover and press answers, no jumps?** Yes, technically. 28 of 28 controls press at 0.96–0.99 scale with no size jump. Hovers change ink, fill or border; already-active pills don't change, which is fine. But no hover tells you anything (a number, a preview): chapter numbers don't preview their panel, and the related rows only get a 6% wash. On phone, tapping a composer pill updates a handler card that sits below the fold, so the answer isn't on screen.
6. **Overlap, font swap, layout shift?** No horizontal overflow at 375 or 320. CLS 0.0002 and the h1 box stays still. Under reduced motion the hero shows its final deployed state and the chapters render whole. There is a near-overlap at the hero's right: the stage bleeds off the viewport, and the "RabbitMQ queue" pill sits about 5 px from the window edge at 1470. In the use-case sheets, the bullet rows sit about 10 px left of the description's inset and their hover fill runs past it, so the spacing is inconsistent.
7. **Which reference, and is its motion there?** Apple's pinned scrubbed stage with a sub-rail, and it is really there on desktop. The typing hero is a motion.dev "time" pattern. There is no synchronized.studio card-open, and none of the osply or hamol pointer-aware detail. The stacked sheets are a stock pattern.
8. **Different from its neighbours?** Yes in its hero medium: a self-running code-to-cluster demo. The composer is its own signature. The stacked use-case sheets and the closing black room with "Related capabilities" read as the shared template.
9. **All 18 parts?** Not every part could be verified from a walk. Narrative, conversion, motion, mobile, states (pause) and reduced motion are present. Trust and proof is thin: there is no proof beyond the Apache-2.0 and GitHub chips, and the use cases carry no evidence.
10. **Banned tells?** No hue, no gradient text, no Inter (Zen and Zen Mono), no eyebrow above an h1 or h2: "WHAT YOU GET" and "COMPOSE YOURS" sit below their headings. But "an explaining sentence under every tile" is exactly the use-case sheets: a sentence plus three bullet lines under each. The 4 equal cards are stacked rather than in a row, so it is not the strict three-boxes ban, but it is clutter.
11. **Copy untouched?** Section copy reads as Hanzo's. In-diagram labels echo the copy ("Scale to zero is still the default…", "A traffic decision rather than another build"). One line I can't trace: "Choose at least one trigger." in the composer card, after untoggling every trigger. Confirm it against Hanzo's source or drop it.

Console: on a clean load, with no screenshot before hydration, there were 0 errors on desktop and phone and no Issue badge. The badge, the 404s and the `<rect> width "undefined"` error seen in the first walk came from screenshotting before hydration, so they are not counted. The 320px "Try Hanzo" clip and the blurred patch behind the header's links are shared chrome, so they are not counted either.

## Fixes, in order
1. **Use cases: give the section an object and cut the document.** Show one sheet at a time in a pinned stage, with the left column carrying a live object for the active case: an API request/response for API Backends, an event stream for Real-time, a GPU node lighting for AI/ML, a cron tick for Scheduled. Put the three bullet lines behind an open/expand instead of always showing them. Fill or remove the empty left column.
2. **Phone: keep "one in, one out" for the six chapters.** Pin a compact stage with a horizontal swipe or a scrubbed rail (01–06) instead of six stacked text-plus-diagram blocks. At minimum, show the diagram first and the copy collapsed under it.
3. **Phone composer: show the answer where the tap is.** Keep the handler card on screen while you tap: a sticky mini summary, or put the card above the pills.
4. **Fix the type hierarchy.** Chapter h3s should rest at 26px or smaller, below the section h2s, and grow only while active if needed. Bring "Get Functions" back to the 26/52 system unless it is the one big moment of that screen.
5. **Make hovers say something.** Hovering a chapter number should preview that chapter's title or diagram. Related rows should show their one-line detail or a mini preview. A hovered composer pill should briefly show its effect on the handler card before you commit.
6. **Hero stage right edge.** Either let it bleed deliberately, with the pill row running off the edge, or inset it so "RabbitMQ queue" keeps the stage's own padding.
7. **Use-case sheet spacing.** Align the bullet rows and their hover fill to the same inset as the description (697 px, not 687 px).
8. **Closing black room.** Remove the roughly 250 px of dead black under "Related capabilities", or give the left column the prompt box so the two columns end together. Give "Containers" its one-liner like the other rows, or drop the column for all three.
9. **Add one authored reference move.** Make the use-case sheets, or the related rows, open into their page (synchronized.studio) instead of a stock stack.
10. **Trace "Choose at least one trigger."** Confirm it against Hanzo's source or remove it.
