2. **Give the first screen one solid ask.** Make the hero "Try Hanzo" the solid ink fill (48px), with "View Documentation" kept as the text link. The header ask stays quiet until the hero ask leaves view, then takes the fill. Right now neither is solid on arrival.
3. **Recompose the hero so it sells in 5 s.** Drop the ~270px of empty white above the h1: set the h1 baseline near 38% of 100svh, with the lede, ask and SERVERLESS COMPUTING caption stacked directly under or beside it on the 8/5 split, aligned to the bottom. Pull the demo up so its full height (triggers, handler.js, replicas filling) fits within 801px, or shorten it, so the replicas filling happens on the first screen and not below the fold.
4. **Give scale a rhythm.** Add one big thing that grows and settles. For example, the replica count or the "01 / 06" numeral grows to 64–84px while it is the moment, then shrinks back. Or let the h1 start larger and settle to 52px as the demo deploys. Make the stage panel titles 32px while active and the rest of the page's h3s 17–21px, so not every heading is 26px.
5. **Fill the dead intro screen.** "A handler file, a trigger, and the cluster you already run" sits alone with an empty right column and about 250px of void above. Either cut that gap to one section step and dock the heading straight onto the pinned stage, or put a small live object on the right (e.g. the three words handler / trigger / cluster drawing their connecting line in 900 ms).
6. **Fix the phone card clipping in the stage.** At 375, panel 01's top tiles (Python, Go) and panel 03's chips (quotas, node pools) are cut by the card's top edge. Give the inner drawing top padding equal to the card padding, or let the card grow to its content. Recheck every panel 01–06 at 375 and 320.
