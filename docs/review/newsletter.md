## Fixes to reach 8

1. **Fix the closing ask.** "Subscribe ↑" must scroll to `#subscribe` in the same tab and focus `#newsletter-email`, with no `target="_blank"` and no `rel`. The `Cta`/`A` helper is treating the hash as external: use an in-page handler or the helper's internal form. Check that a tap at 375 moves the page to the form and the field takes focus.
2. **Phone hero paper: no crop.** At 375x812 the paper must sit fully inside the 16px gutters at rest, as it already does on desktop. It is offset about 100px right today: check what `.xnl-tilt`/`.xnl-stage` resolve to under 900px. Also pull it up so it peeks within the first screen instead of after about 100px of blank.
3. **Give the middle scene's first screen something to hold.** When the pinned section starts, the top half is empty and the statement sits grey near the bottom (`d-s01`). Bring the first statement in with the card, or start it inked, so no screen in the scene reads as blank. Consider shortening `.xnl-inside` from 300svh. Three statements do not need three full screens of scroll.
4. **Give the dark room an object.** Right now it is only type and an empty lower half. Use the envelope from the hero, travelling through the four steps (addressed, link out, issue in, unsubscribe), or the paper itself as the stage. That way the room shows the opt-in instead of only labelling it, and it ties back to the signature.
5. **Remove the 0.027 shift on send.** Keep the envelope in the same box as the paper (fixed-size `.xnl-tilt`, or position the envelope absolutely) so the swap moves nothing.
6. **Note the copy question.** Put the cut fragments (dark-room h2 and step labels) on the coverage card as a copy question, or show the full sentence once and let the steps be numbered marks with the sentence parts as small captions.
