## What works
- **The hero object is real and alive.** The monitor plus 4-track timeline (Koma/Oto/Koe/Uta) plays by itself; within 1 s the shot, rig progress bar and playhead move with no input. Grabbing the playhead pauses it (button flips to Play), clicking a rig jumps the shot, the shot title, `Actor Lock` and active clip change as the playhead passes. This is the page's own idea and nobody else has it.
- **Pinned 2.39:1 reel** after the hero performs Dolly In / Crane Rise / 360° Orbit on the still as you scroll, with `01 / 03` counter. Genuine "one panel changes" moment.
- **Film strip** shows 4+ real Hanzo model films at once; hovering a frame bulges it through the WebGL opener and clicking opens /koma. That is the synchronized.studio move, and it works.
- **Header ask swaps** to "Launch studio.hanzo.ai" on desktop once the hero ask leaves view.
- Motion mixes kinds: idle (timeline, Studio/Design chips hopping, travelling active node in the graph), pointer (opener warp, scrub), state (rig tabs), scroll (reel). Not scroll-only.
- 0 console errors in default motion at both sizes. No horizontal scroll at 375 (`scrollWidth` 375). `taste.py` passes; everything is greyscale. Parity 87/87 text, 16/16 links. Copy button confirms "Copied".

## What fails (ranked, each with a concrete fix the owner can make in the page's own files)
1. **Hero composition wastes the first screen.** At 1470x801 the top-left 280px above the h1 is empty black (the visually hidden `.xst-kick` still leaves the grid bottom-aligned against the lede), the h1 bottom sits at 454px (57% down, target ~38%), and only the top ~290px of the monitor shows; the timeline itself is below the fold. At 375 the first screen is h1 + lede + two stacked buttons and no product at all (`.xst-mon` top at 610px; less than 80px of product inside 660px).
   Fix (`Studio.css`): `.xst-top { align-items: start; }`, remove the dead space from `.xst-kick`'s slot (or set `.xst-top-l` to `align-self:start`), cut hero top padding so the h1 baseline lands near 38% of 100svh and the monitor plus the first timeline row are in view at 801px. On phone (`@media (max-width:899px)`) put the ask row inline (`.a2-acts{flex-direction:row}`) and drop "Open Studio" to a text link so the monitor starts by ~460px.
2. **Six equal explainer boxes ("Built for cinema, VFX…").** A 3x2 grid of identical cards, each with a paragraph — exactly the "3 equal boxes in a row / explaining sentence under every tile" James bans, and it is where the page turns into a document. The hero also ends with three equal text columns (`.xst-specs` 01/02/03).
   Fix (`Studio.jsx` `Graph`, `Studio.css`): make it a real node graph — six small nodes (title + mono tag only) wired on one canvas, with the travelling active node opening that node's paragraph in one detail pane beside it (one in, one out). For `.xst-specs`, set them as one hairline-divided list in a single column under the monitor, or fold them into the inspector, not three equal columns.
3. **Label above a headline in the close.** `.xst-mark` "● REC" sits directly above the `Get Studio` h2. Banned tell.
   Fix (`Studio.jsx` `Chair`): move the `xst-mark` span below the h2 (after `Visual builder studio`) or delete it; keep the blink on the hero only.
4. **Scale has no rhythm, and it is inverted.** h1 is 32px; section h2s are 32px; the closing "Get Studio" h2 is 44px, bigger than the page's h1. Nothing grows or settles.
   Fix (`Studio.css`): `.xst-h1` to the 52px resting size (phone 40px, 3 lines), section h2s to 26px, `.xst-get h2` to 26–32px. Let the reel still be the one thing that grows past rest while pinned.
5. **The closing solid ask goes nowhere.** `Open Studio` (`data-cta="studio.close"`, solid) links to `/studio` — the page you are on; the hero secondary does the same. MASTER: a button with no destination is hidden, never looped.
   Fix (`Studio.jsx`): keep the words but make both "Open Studio" quiet text links that scroll to the cinema (`#xst-cinema` on the monitor), and give the close's solid fill to the existing "Launch studio.hanzo.ai" (move `data-cta="studio.close"` onto it).
6. **Dead white gaps between sections.** ~250px empty above "Production-grade…" and ~350px after the reel's third pillar before "Built for cinema"; the A2Paste strip floats between the black hero and that gap with nothing to look at.
   Fix (`Studio.css`): trim `.xst-what` and `.xst-graph` top padding to the section clamp, and overlap the reel exit with the next heading by 40px (`.xst-reel { margin-bottom: -40px }` or shorten the pinned stick height by the trailing blank).
7. **Hydration error under reduced motion** — red "1 issue" badge. The strip renders `<video>` on the server but `on === i && !calm` drops it on the client.
   Fix (`Studio.jsx` line ~390): gate the video on a mounted flag (`const [ready,setReady]=useState(false); useEffect(()=>setReady(true),[])` and render `ready && on===i && !calm`), so server and first client render match.
8. **Hover that says nothing.** The solid hero ask has no hover change; the Studio/Design chips and timeline clips have none (press only); the secondary hover is a faint grey inset tint.
   Fix (`Studio.css`): inside `@media (hover:hover)` give the chips a border to ink and a fill shift, the clips a border plus a timecode reveal (`00:04–00:08`), and the hero ask a magnetic nudge or arrow slide — something that tells you a thing, not a tint.
9. **Phone header ask stays "Try Hanzo"** for the whole walk at 375; the page's ask never reaches the header on phone. Write `docs/requests/studio.md` asking the header owner to honour `data-thumb` at mobile widths too.
10. **Small targets / off-palette greys.** Inline links `/design`, `studio.hanzo.ai`, "Explore model pricing", "View full model catalog", `hanzoai` are 19–22px tall at 375 (need 44px hit area: pad them). Studio.css uses Apple greys (`#f5f5f7`, `#a1a1a6`, `#6e6e73`, `#0d0d0f`, `#19191c`, `#1c1c20`) instead of the deck ramp — swap to `#fafafa`, `#a3a3a3`, `#737373`, `#0a0a0a`/`#0f0f0f`, `#171717`, `#262626`.
