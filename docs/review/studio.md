## What works
- **The first screen sells in under 5 s and moves within 1 s.** The h1 cuts in at 52px (40px on phone), and the lede and solid ask sit to the right. The monitor plus the first timeline row are on the first screen at 1470x801. The film starts by itself: the rig name rolls, the progress bar fills and the playhead travels, all with no input.
- **The hero object is a real, playable product.** Rig tabs jump shots, model chips light their track and retitle the prompt, clips show their timecode on hover, and dragging the playhead takes over ("Playhead is yours: press Play to roll again"). The keyboard drives the slider too. Every control answers with something other than opacity. This is the page's own idea.
- **The six explainer boxes are gone.** The capabilities are now one wired node graph. A layoutId ring travels from node to node, the wires light behind it, and a single pane beside the graph holds the active sentence. The idea is right: one in, one out.
- **The card opens into its page.** A film-strip frame hover plays its film, with one video at a time and 4+ real model pictures on screen together. A click runs the opener into /oto and Back returns. That is the synchronized.studio move.
- **Pinned 2.39:1 reel** with a `01 / 03` counter, and each stretch of scroll performs a camera move on the still. Scale has rhythm: h1 52, section heads 26, node titles 21, pillar titles 17, mono labels 12.
- **The motion mix is right:** idle (film, graph pass, chip hop), pointer (scrub, opener, hovers), state (rig tabs, ring), time (caret), scroll (reel). Scroll is under half of it.
- **Fixes from last round are in:** "● REC" now sits below "Get Studio". Both "Open Studio" links scroll to the monitor instead of reloading the page. The solid close goes to studio.hanzo.ai, the greys are the deck's, and the reduced-motion hydration error is fixed.
- **Clean checks:** no horizontal scroll at 375 (scrollWidth 375), no main target under 44px on phone, no visible eyebrow above any heading (the kickers are visually hidden), 0 page console errors over 7 fresh loads, and a reduced-motion state that is complete and still.

## What fails (ranked, each with a concrete fix the owner can make in the page's own files)
1. **Dead white holes between sections (desktop).** The gap from the last line of content to the next section's first line is 231px from What to Reel and 355px from Graph to Strip. At 1470x801 a whole screen goes by with only the end of the graph canvas and white below it (frame d-s060). There is another 182px from Paste to What. This is the "dead sort of page" feeling between the good parts.
   Fix (`Studio.css`): give `.xst-graph` a `padding-bottom` of about 44–72px and `.xst-strip` a `padding-top` of about 44px, so the strip heading arrives within 120px of the graph. Trim `.xst-what`'s bottom padding (`clamp(44px,5vw,72px)` → 27px). Pull the reel stage up so the frame starts about 40px under the Studio/Design card (lower `.xst-reel-stick` top offset, or use a `margin-top: -72px` on `.xst-reel`). Keep the 40px overlap rule: old and new objects should share the screen.
2. **Text-on-text crossfades.** In the reel subtitle, pillars 02 and 03 cross on the same grid cell with the same 0.4 s opacity, so both titles and paragraphs read over each other mid-change (frame d-s040). The graph pane does the same: "Dedicated Cloud GPU Render Farm" printed over "Koma 2.0 Camera Motion Control" while the clip-path wipes (frame d-s056).
   Fix (`Studio.css` lines ~185 and ~223): take the outgoing item out first and bring the incoming one in after it. Give `.xst-subs li:not(.on)` and `.xst-pane-c:not(.on)` `transition-duration: 150ms` with no delay. Give `.on` a `transition-delay: 150ms`. Better still, use the line mask for the incoming title (translateY 105%→0 inside an overflow-hidden line) so the change reads as a cut, not a blend.
3. **The pinned reel is long for what it does.** `.xst-reel` is 360svh (about 2,880px, 3.6 screens) for three beats. The stills are dark-left compositions, so the 2.39:1 frame is half black and the slow scale or translate is barely visible at notch speed. Most of the dwell, nothing seems to change.
   Fix (`Studio.css`/`Studio.jsx` `Reel`): cut the height to about 240svh. Give each move more amplitude (Dolly 1.0→1.35, Crane ±12%, Orbit ±12% with rotate ±2.5°). Set `object-position` so the still's subject sits in the frame's centre third. Let the frame grow past rest while pinned (for example, scale it from 0.92 to 1 over the first 15% of the pin), so the moment has a size change too.
4. **On phone the signature turns into a wall of text.** Under 900px the reel stacks into one static frame frozen on "Dolly In 01 / 03" followed by three full paragraphs, about 750px of grey copy (frames q-030, q-035). Stacking is allowed, but the frame should still do something.
   Fix (`Studio.css` `@media (max-width:899px)`, `Studio.jsx`): give each pillar its own small 2.39:1 still above its title, so you get 3 static panels, which is MASTER's "pinned scene becomes 3 static panels". Alternatively, keep the single frame `position: sticky; top: 72px` and swap its still and rig label as each pillar's title crosses it (IntersectionObserver, no scroll scrub).
5. **Phone hero: 5-line h1, and only about 100px of product inside 660px.** At 375 the h1 runs 5 lines at 40px (132–340px), the lede takes 5 more, and the monitor starts at 563px. MASTER asks for 3 lines at 40px with 80px+ of product, so this passes the product line only just and the first screen is mostly type.
   Fix (`Studio.css`): set the phone h1 to 34–36px with `text-wrap: balance` (it then sets in 4 lines). Cut the hero top padding on phone by about 24px. Tighten the lede to 16px/1.45, so the monitor and its rig label start by about 470px.
6. **Too many asks at the close.** In one viewport there are a solid "Launch studio.hanzo.ai", an outlined "Open Hanzo Design", a text "Open Studio ↓" that sends you back up to the top, and an outlined "Deploy to Cloud" in the black card. That is four, and two of them are outlined pills of the same weight. James: "never 3 or more equal buttons".
   Fix (`Studio.jsx` `Chair`): render "Open Hanzo Design" as a text link (`Go` without the outline, or `Cta kind="link"`). Move "Open Studio" out of the close's button row into the related list or below it as a plain link. Keep "Deploy to Cloud" as a text link with an arrow inside the card.
7. **Minor polish.** At 375 the empty voice-track gap clip gets the active "now" outline, an empty box with a border (frame a-jump). Skip `.now` on `.gap` clips. The "Studio or Design?" card is shorter than the copy column's line, which leaves its lower half white. Align the card to the bottom of the copy (8/5 split, bottom aligned per MASTER 3).
