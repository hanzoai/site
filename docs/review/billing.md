Put the count in the vertical middle of the visible part (or shorten the machine so its bottom edge sits near 801px) so the 64px figure is always on the first screen; never let the slab read as empty black.
- **Hero CLS:** the arriving events push the LI rows down (0.002 to 0.0097 per event, 0.047 total in 5 s and rising). Reserve the list height and animate new rows with transform only (translateY into a fixed-height, overflow-hidden list), so layout-shift entries stop.
- **Phone ISSUED stamp overlap (375x812):** the stamp (about 120x50px, rotated) lands on "Preview one before it is issued." Give it its own row or place it in the head next to the status chip; and consider replacing the rotated rubber stamp with the status chip flipping to a filled "Issued" (already done in the head on phone), which is quieter and on-deck.

- **Dead hovers:** hero "GitHub ->" and the light code-pane "Copy" show no change on hover. Give them an underline/arrow nudge (text link) and a fill to #f5f5f5 (Copy). Give Publish a new price a visible disabled state (40%) once Price 2 exists, so the press is not swallowed silently.

Let the price stack from chapter 01 stay on the left and settle into its "invoice issued" state in 03; tighten the hero so the h1 rises towards the 38% line.
