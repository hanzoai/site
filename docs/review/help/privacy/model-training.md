# /help/privacy/model-training — James-eye review
7 Nov, round 6, walked at 1470x801, 375x812 and 320 (phone: isMobile, hasTouch) with Playwright from node: frames at 0, 0.5, 1 and 3 s with no input, wheel-scrolled a screen at a time with 1.5 s dwell, hovered and pressed every main control on desktop, tapped every TOC row and demo control on phone, cookie card dismissed, reduced-motion loads at 1470 and 375, console and overflow collected.

Verdict: 7/10

First complaint: "Why am I reading the same thing twice? The panel on the right says word for word what the paragraph on the left says, and that switch shows up four times before I've scrolled two screens. Show it once and let it do the talking."

## The 11 answers
1. **Sells in 5 s, moves in 1 s?** Yes on desktop. The h1 cuts into its line, and beside it a small app window plays the real steps on its own: a cursor clicks the avatar, Settings slides in, Privacy is picked, the switch flips. A 01/04 step counter and progress dashes keep time, and a Pause button sits on the panel. On 375 the h1 and lede fill the screen and the phone mock is only half in view at 1 s. It is moving, but you have to scroll to see what it shows.
2. **One in, one out?** On desktop, yes. The demo panel sticks, and its scene changes as each section comes up: the toggle for 01, Settings > Privacy, a timeline of filled and hollow dots for 03, and a checklist that ticks Safety review, Feedback, Business and API, Analytics for 04. Each row in 04 lights as it passes. That is a real pinned stage. On phone nothing pins. Each section has its own figure inline, so it reads as a long document with pictures, 7,354 px or about nine screens.
3. **A real visual in every section?** 01 to 04 yes (the toggle, the step demo, the timeline SVG, the checklist). 05 "More" is a large quiet grey room with "05 More" alone on the left and a paragraph plus a Help Center row on the right. That is acceptable for a close, but it is the one dead room.
4. **One ask, nothing floating, scale rhythm?** "Try Hanzo" sits in the sticky header and nothing floats over content. The scale is right: h1 52, section heads 26, lede 21, body 16, Zen Mono 11–12 labels, so there is one big thing per screen. At 320 the shared header clips "Try Han…" at the right edge. That is shared chrome, noted but not counted.
5. **Every hover and press answers, no jumps?** TOC rows hover to #f5f5f5 and press translateY(1px). The demo tabs and the Pause button press to scale 0.98, and Pause also hovers to #ededed. The demo's big switch has no hover state at all, only the press scale. The breadcrumb and inline links only darken their underline on hover, which is colour only but fine for prose links. On phone, the TOC anchors land with the heading clear of the header. I could not confirm the tap press state from synthetic events.
6. **Overlap, font swap, layout shift?** No horizontal overflow at any width, no console errors, and no hydration warning in any load, including reduced motion. The Next.js badge reads "Issue" with a 0 count and no log behind it, so it is not counted. One overlap: on 375, mid-transition, the sliding Settings sheet in the phone mock paints over the phone's right bezel. The pane measures 414 px against 375, so it is clipped by the card but not by the device frame. The computed heading stack leads with "Noto Kufi Arabic". The glyphs render as Zen, but check that face is limited by unicode-range, or it is a third face waiting to happen.
7. **Which reference, and is its motion there?** Apple's pinned stage where one panel changes: present on desktop, absent on phone. The motion.dev line-mask cut-in on the h1 is there. The self-running demo is the "idle" kind of motion JAMES.md asks for. Scroll is not the only motion. That is good.
8. **Different from neighbours?** The hero medium (a scripted settings demo of this exact switch) belongs to this article. I did not walk sibling help articles this round, so the signature looks specific to this page but is not compared against them.
9. **18 parts?** Breadcrumb, TOC with numbers, section anchors, a pause control on the auto-play, a reduced-motion final state (the demo rests on step 04/04 with the switch shown), phone tabs that default to "On mobile", and contact and legal links at the close. Missing: no "was this helpful" or next-article path beyond the single Help Center row.
10. **Banned tells?** None found. No hue. The "HELP · PRIVACY" mono label sits under the lede, not above the h1. The step-card labels sit above lists, not headlines. No three equal boxes and no glow. Clutter is the near miss (see 11 and the fixes).
11. **Copy untouched?** Every visible sentence traces to content/live/help/privacy/model-training.json. "Help Center" is in support.json and "Pause" is in ui-strings. Nothing is invented. But the panel repeats copy. Section 01's paragraph ("When it is on… does not use them for that.") sits in the panel right beside the identical paragraph in the body. "The change saves to your Hanzo account…" appears twice on one screen. On phone, the 04 checklist lists the four labels directly above the same four labels in the body. "Help improve Hanzo's models" is printed about six times. Nothing is rewritten, but it is overload.

## Fixes, in order
1. **Stop the panel repeating the body.** Keep the toggle and its one-line label in the panel's footer. Drop the "When it is on…" paragraph and the "The change saves…" tick line from the panel, because the body already says them. The panel should show, and the text should say.
2. **One switch per screen.** On desktop the first two screens show the switch in the demo screen, the panel footer and the section 01 panel scene. On phone, section 01 also gets a toggle card right under the hero demo's toggle. Phone: remove the section 01 toggle card (the hero demo just showed it). Desktop: when the panel moves to the 01 scene, hide the footer toggle so only one is visible.
3. **Phone 04: pick the checklist or the rows, not both.** Make the four rows the checklist (the tick and highlight on the row itself) instead of a card listing the same four labels above them.
4. **One counter meaning in the panel slot.** The hero counter reads "01 / 04 Click your avatar" (steps). After scrolling, the same slot reads "01 / 05 What the setting does" (sections). Give sections their own small label or reuse the TOC numbers, and keep x / 04 for steps only.
5. **Clip the phone mock to its device frame.** The Settings sheet must slide in under the bezel. Put overflow:hidden and the radius on the screen inside the bezel, not on the outer card.
6. **Give the phone a pinned moment.** At 375 the page is nine screens of document. Pin the demo card while section 02's steps scroll past and light the matching step, as desktop does with the panel. Or at least make the 02 step list drive the hero demo, so the demo runs once instead of twice.
7. **Kill the dead band in the desktop panel.** About 70–80 px of empty grey sits under the panel text at every scroll position. Size the panel to its content, or let the screen take that height.
8. **Hover on the demo switch.** It is the one control that gives no hover answer. Darken the track or nudge the thumb 2 px toward its other state. Add the same to the Help Center row in "More" (arrow moves 4 px).
9. **Close the "More" room properly.** "05 More" sits alone on the left with about 300 px of air. Bottom-align it with the paragraph on the 8/5 split, per MASTER 3.1, so the close reads as one unit.
10. Check the "Noto Kufi Arabic"-first heading stack is unicode-range limited, so Latin can never render in it.
