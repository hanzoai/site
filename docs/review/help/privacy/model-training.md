# /help/privacy/model-training — James-eye review
7 Nov, round 5, walked at 1470x801, 375x812 and 320x700 (phone: isMobile, hasTouch) with Playwright Chromium: frames at 0, 1 and 3 s with no input, a screen at a time by wheel (desktop) or scroll (phone) with 1.5 s dwell, hover and press on every main control on desktop, a tap on every TOC row, tab, switch and step on phone, a prefers-reduced-motion load at 1470 and 375, console and overflow collected. Cookie card dismissed before judging.

Verdict: 7/10

First complaint: "It loads finished and then rewinds. The panel says 04 of 04, then jumps back to step one and the switch flicks on by itself. On my phone the tab jumps from desktop to mobile right in front of me. Start it at step one and let it play."

## The 11 answers
1. **Sells in 5 s, moves within 1 s?** Mostly. The question as the h1 (52px) with the lede on the left and a self-running settings demo on the right make the job obvious in a glance. The demo moves before 1 s (a cursor goes to the avatar, `01 / 04 Click your avatar.`). But the first frame is wrong. Desktop t0 shows `04 / 04`, the end state, with the switch off and a stray dot where the tick has not drawn yet. At 1 s it snaps back to `01 / 04` and the lower switch is suddenly on. On phone t0 the segmented control has "On the web and the desktop app" selected while the mock is already a phone. It flips to "On mobile" after hydration (it ends on mobile at both 375 and 320). The first impression is a snap, not an arrival.
2. **One in, one out, or a document?** Better than a document. On desktop the right panel is pinned and changes per section: the settings view for 01, the steps, a before/after timeline (filled dots, a cut line, hollow dots) for 03, and the settings view again with a lit row for 04. The caption tracks `0N / 05` and a hairline progress bar runs under the header. The left column is still a long article, and the pinned panel shows the same Settings > Privacy mock for 3 of its 5 states. On phone the pinned panel becomes inline objects. The same "Privacy / Help improve Hanzo's models" card appears three times (hero demo, section 01, section 04), so it reads as repetition, not a new thing coming in.
3. **A real visual in every section?** Desktop: yes, through the pinned panel. Phone: 01, 03 and 04 have an object, 02 has the step stepper, and 05 "More" has only links and a Help Center pill. Acceptable for a support page.
4. **One ask, nothing floating, scale rhythm?** Yes. "Try Hanzo" sits in the sticky header and nothing floats over content (the cookie card is shared chrome). Scale has rhythm: h1 52 (32 on phone), section heads 26, body 16, mono labels 11-12. Only the h1 is big. "Help · Privacy" sits below the lede, not above the h1. The breadcrumb above the h1 is navigation at 14px, not an eyebrow. At 320 the shared header's "Try Hanzo" is cut off at the right edge (shared chrome, not counted).
5. **Hover and press answer, no jumps?** Yes, with small faults. TOC rows fill to #f5f5f5 and press down 0.88px. The tabs, Pause, switch, step rows and Help Center pill change fill and scale to **0.971** on press, past the 0.98 limit. Hover on the steps lights the matching demo frame. Pause swaps to a play icon and the demo stops, but its accessible name stays "Pause". On phone every tap lights its row or tab, and TOC taps land on the right anchor. Tapping a desktop step on phone scrolls the page by about 21px. The "usage policy" link (`/aup`) returns 404 on this server and lands on `/`.
6. **Overlap, font swap or layout shift?** No horizontal overflow at any width (scrollWidth = clientWidth; the 320 pane overflow is clipped). The h1 box is identical at 0, 1 and 3 s. No hydration diff or console error at any width, including the reduced-motion load, and the Next.js dev badge showed no issue. Faults: the state snaps in answer 1 (demo rewinds, switch flips, phone tab flips). The demo's crossfade at about 3 s double-exposes two frames: "Settings" ghosts over menu skeletons with a cursor in the middle, which looks muddy, not authored. At 320 the platform tabs stack into two uneven full-width rows.
7. **Which reference, and is its motion there?** chatgpt.com/business/enterprise calm trust, plus apple's pinned panel that changes per section. The pinned stage and the self-running demo are really there. Nothing from synchronized or the osply/hamol pointer detail, which is fine for this page type.
8. **Different from its neighbours?** It is the only help article. It differs from /support and /privacy, which do not use a self-running settings demo. Its signature (a staged UI walkthrough with a pinned panel) is its own.
9. **18 parts covered?** Mostly. Navigation (breadcrumb, TOC with active fill, progress bar), conversion (header ask, Help Center exit), states (pause and play, reduced motion shows the final state with nothing hidden), accessibility (radios, a real switch, a skip link) are all there. Gaps: one broken in-body link, a stale Pause label, and the press scale over spec.
10. **Banned tells?** None found. No hue (no chroma above the threshold in main). Fonts are Zen, Zen Mono and an Arabic fallback face listed first in the stack. Nothing is boxed in the hero. No eyebrow above the h1. No fade-only buttons.
11. **Copy untouched?** Yes. Every visible sentence and label traces to `content/live/help/privacy/model-training.json`, `support.json` ("Help Center") or `ui-strings.json` ("Pause"). Two small liberties:
    - The demo's lower card shows only the first half of the "When it is on…" paragraph and drops "When it is off, Hanzo does not use them for that."
    - On phone, Terms of Service and Privacy Policy appear twice in "More": as link rows built from the sentence, then again in the sentence.

## Fixes, in order
1. **Server-render the demo at step 01.** Its first frame should be `01 / 04`, the lower switch in its starting state, the tick drawn or absent, and no visible rewind at hydration. Under reduced motion it may still render the final state.
2. **Choose the platform tab before paint on phones**, using a CSS media query or by rendering both tabs and hiding one with CSS, so "On mobile" is selected at t0 with no visible flip.
3. **Replace the crossfade between demo frames** with a clean cut or a short slide using ease-in-out at 300 ms, so two frames are never visible at once.
4. **Stop repeating the Settings > Privacy mock.** On phone, keep it only in the hero demo. Section 04 needs a different object, for example the four "does not change" rows as a small ledger with the switch shown off. On desktop, give 01 and 04 different panel states, not the same settings screen twice.
5. **Fix the `/aup` "usage policy" link** so it resolves (the route is missing or 404s on this server). If the page belongs to another chat, point to the working usage-policy URL the footer uses.
6. **Bring press scale to 0.98** (or translateY(1px)) on the tabs, Pause, switch, steps and Help Center pill.
7. **Make Pause a real toggle**: aria-label Pause/Play, or aria-pressed.
8. **At 320, keep the platform tabs on one row** (smaller type or tighter padding), and stop a step tap from scrolling the page.
9. **On phone "More", drop the duplicate Terms and Privacy link rows** or the duplicate sentence links, not both kept. Show the full "When it is on… When it is off…" text in the demo's lower card.
