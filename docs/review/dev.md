# /dev — James-eye review
Verdict: 7/10
First complaint: "The code box flashes up grey with the answer already in it, then rewinds, and my button turns up two seconds after everything else. Then on my phone the race just sits there at 0.00. It's nearly there, but the first five seconds feel unfinished."

Walked moving on 7 Nov with Playwright at 1470x801 and 375x812 (plus 320 and 1470 reduced-motion / JS-off): 250 ms frames for 9 s untouched, a slow wheel scroll through the whole page with the race board held in view for 10 s, hover and press probes on 12 controls, and click-through of the rail, model rows, Kai steps, install tabs, Copy, FAQ and Replay. Loads during the coordinator's server outage were retaken. Proofs are in `docs/review/dev/`.

## The 11 answers

**1. Sells in 5 s, moves within 1 s?** Mostly. "Describe the change. Dev ships it." with a terminal that types `curl … | sh`, `dev`, the prompt and two ticks, then lands the `src/retry.ts` diff (−/+ on line 3) by about 8 s and settles. That is the brief's "terminal with a diff landing". The arrival is the problem. Nothing paints until about 0.9 s. From 1.0 to 1.75 s the demo fades in as a grey wash, and its diff pane already shows the finished −/+ change (proof 1) while the terminal is still typing "cu". The diff then rewinds to the old code, so the ending is given away. The lede, Open Dev and Read the docs only arrive at about 2.0 s, after the demo, so the read order is h1 → grey box → ask instead of h1 → ask → object. The header ask also flips from solid "Try Hanzo" to solid "Open Dev" (1.25–1.5 s) and then to outlined "Try Hanzo" (2.0 s).

**2. One thing in, one out, or a document?** Better than most pages. The black race room arrives with one big figure (3.02×). The "How" chapters are a pinned card stack where each card slides over the last. The places rail moves on arrows. From Setup onwards it turns into a document: install tabs, then open source, then FAQ, then paste, each a static two-column block with nothing coming in or going out. While the stack moves, the outgoing card's text is cut mid-line ("One candidate, its preconditions hold…" sliced in half at 5210).

**3. A real visual object in every section?** No. Hero terminal, race clocks, the four place mock-ups, the bash model picker, the Kai step list, the fence sandbox and the Gen diagram all work. "Built to be checked" is a heading plus three Lucide check-circle rows with a sentence under each, so it has no object. The open-source "hanzoai/dev" card shows grey skeleton bars that never fill, which looks lazy or broken. Paste and FAQ are plain text.

**4. One ask in the header, nothing floating; scale rhythm?** Partly. The hero ask is "Open Dev", but the header says "Try Hanzo" on desktop while the hero is in view, and on the phone it always says "Try Hanzo" (it never changes). On the phone the header pill and the hero Open Dev are both solid black in the same viewport (proof 3). JS-off desktop shows the same two solid fills. The cookie card covers the bottom 220 px of the phone hero, hiding most of the demo, and the dev "1 Issue" badge sits on top of it. Scale has good rhythm: h1 52 px, the race's huge Air "3.02×" as the one moment, sections at 26 px, mono captions small. The closing Band's 88 px headline breaks as "…the change. Dev / ships it." and splits the product name across lines.

**5. Does every hover and press answer, with no jumps?** Mostly. Open Dev darkens and presses to 0.98. Replay inverts. Model rows light up, and a click swaps the `enso-auto` chip to `enso-ultra`. Kai steps fill and change the explanation. Install tabs switch. Copy says "Copied" and copies `curl -fsSL https://hanzo.sh | sh` without the `$`. There are no rect jumps. Failures: FAQ rows change nothing on hover (no fill, no colour, no icon move). The header ask only fades (opacity 1 → 0.88), which is a banned "button that fades on hover". Copy's hover is a barely visible 10% inset tint.

**6. Overlaps, font swap or layout shift?** CLS is 0.0004 desktop and 0.0005 phone. The header has no overlap at any scroll step, and there is no visible font swap. Bugs: (a) on the phone the places rail has no left gutter: the `ul` sits at −16 px and the first card at x=0, flush to the glass with its title at x≈4. (b) At 320 px `body.scrollWidth` is 348 (the html element clips it). (c) Reduced motion throws a hard React hydration failure (the server renders the demo control as "Replay" and the client as "Pause"), so the red Next error badge shows. On plain loads I saw an intermittent "attributes didn't match" hydration warning on 3 of 6 loads.

**7. Which reference, and is its motion there?** It reads as an anthropic.com launch (benchmark as the hero figure, "replay of run …" method lines, 95% intervals) crossed with Apple pinned scenes (the chapter stack). Both are present. The race is the best thing on the page: two clocks counting at real speed, Hanzo in ink and Claude Code in grey, with Hanzo finishing at 2.29 s while the other runs on to 9.52 s. On the phone, though, the race sat at READY 0.00 s for the full 10 s with the Claude Code clock in view at scrollY 1218 (proof 2). It started only once both cards were scrolled into view, about 600 px later.

**8. Different from its neighbours?** Yes. The live terminal-plus-diff hero, the black race room and the chapter stack give it its own medium and signature, and nothing on it reads as the home or /chat hero.

**9. All 18 parts?** Not fully. Present: one h1, one `main`, a 2 px ink focus ring on a Tab crawl, `data-cta` on asks, copy equals display, JS-off legible (only the (i) tooltip is hidden, which is correct), reduced-motion final state (demo landed, race at DONE, no running animations after 3.5 s), and a pause/replay control on the demo and the race. Missing or broken: the sticky-ask label rule (part 4), a 44 px target ("hanzoai/dev" link is 103x21), the 320 px overflow (part 11), the error-free reduced-motion load (part 3), and no phone-specific composition for the rail (part 11).

**10. Banned tells?** The header ask fades on hover. "Built to be checked" is a row of Lucide icons with an explaining sentence under each. There are skeleton bars as a resting state. Some off-deck tints show up in states: `rgb(245,245,247)` on the Band ask, `rgb(110,110,115)` / `rgb(10,10,11)` on install tabs, `rgb(44,44,46)` on Open Dev hover. They are Apple zinc, not the deck's neutral ramp, so check them with `taste.py`. There are no gradients, gradient text, kickers above headlines or green.

**11. Copy untouched?** As far as a visitor can tell, yes. Every visible string reads as Hanzo's own, including the race method lines and FAQ. Run `parity.py` to confirm.

## Fixes (ordered by impact)

1. **Fix the hero arrival order and the diff spoiler.** Render the lede, Open Dev and Read the docs with the h1 (visible by 700 ms, no later than the h1 cut-in ends). Have the demo pane start in its "before" state: no −/+ lines, line 3 old code only, until the terminal reaches the diff step. Measure: at 1000 ms the 1470 frame shows the ask, and no `−`/`+` gutter glyph exists in the diff pane before the "Describe the change." prompt is typed.
2. **One ask label and one solid fill.** The header ask should read "Open Dev" at both widths for the whole page and stay outlined while the hero Open Dev is in view, including at 375 and JS-off. Measure: exactly one element with a solid ink or white fill per viewport at 0, 2, 5 s, and the header label never changes during load.
3. **Start the phone race when the first clock is in view.** Trigger at 40% of the first clock card visible, not the whole board. Measure: at 375x812, scrollY 1218, the Claude Code clock reads > 0.00 s within 1 s.
4. **Fix the reduced-motion hydration failure.** The server and client must render the same demo control (both "Replay", or both "Pause" until mount), and the intermittent attribute mismatch on plain loads must go. Measure: 0 console errors and no Next badge on 5 plain and 5 reduced-motion loads at both sizes.
5. **Give the phone rail its gutter.** The first card's left edge should sit at 16 px, with a 14–18% peek of the next card and `scroll-snap-type: x proximity`. Measure: `li.getBoundingClientRect().left === 16` at 375, and `body.scrollWidth <= 320` at 320.
6. **Replace the skeleton bars in the open-source card** with real content from the page (a file list or the latest-release line). Measure: no element in that card renders as a bare bar at rest.
7. **Give "Built to be checked" an object.** For example, a ledger that replays its three rows (verifier record → revision bind → replay) in place of the three icon rows. Measure: the section has a non-icon visual at least 240 px tall.
8. **Make FAQ hover answer.** A row fill to `#f5f5f5` plus a 45° turn of the + on hover and focus, 150 ms ease-out. Measure: a computed-style change on hover.
9. **Make the header ask not fade on hover.** Darken the fill (as Open Dev does) instead of opacity 0.88 (shared chrome, so request it in `docs/requests/`).
10. **Make the chapter stack hand over cleanly.** Fade or mask the outgoing card's text before the next card's edge crosses it, so no line is shown sliced in half. Measure: no text glyph intersects the incoming card edge in a 250 ms scroll capture.
11. **Fix the Band headline break** so the product name doesn't split: "Describe the change. / Dev ships it." as two balanced lines at 1470 and 375.
12. **Phone hero object above the cookie card.** With the consent card open at 375x812, at least 80 px of the running terminal must be visible above it (shorten the lede gap or move the demo up). The "hanzoai/dev" link target must be at least 44 px tall.
13. **Swap the off-deck state tints** (245,245,247 / 110,110,115 / 10,10,11 / 44,44,46) for deck values (#f5f5f5, #737373, #0a0a0a, #262626) and re-run `taste.py`.
