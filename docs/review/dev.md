# /dev — James-eye review
Verdict: 6/10
First complaint: "The terminal's the right idea, but when the diff lands nothing happens. It changes a 1 to a 0 and highlights a grey line, and I'm meant to feel it shipped? Then the race just sits on 0.00 for ten seconds. It looks broken, not alive."

Walked moving at 1470×801 and 375×812 (Playwright, 250 ms frames for the first 8 s, slow wheel scroll through the whole 10,716 px / 12,935 px page, hover and press probes, reduced motion, JS off). CLS 0.0004 desktop, 0.0004 phone. No horizontal scroll at either size. `parity.py /dev`: OK, 176/176 text and 12/12 links. Proof is in `docs/review/dev/`.

**1. Does the first screen sell in 5 s, and does something move within 1 s?** Mostly yes. The h1 cuts in by line mask at about 250 ms, and the terminal starts typing `curl -fsSL` before 750 ms. The 52 px h1, lede and "Open Dev" sit together, so the first screen reads as "coding agent, in a terminal". The payoff is weak, though. The prompt types out, two ✓ lines appear, and then the "diff landing" at about 5 s is one line, `i = 1` → `i = 0`, marked with a 2 px white bar and a grey row. There is no red/green, no hunk, and no lines-changed count. The diff pane keeps 4 lines of code above about 200 px of empty black. The terminal box also runs from y 385 to 850, past the 801 fold.

**2. One thing in, one thing out, or a document?** Partly. The 3.02× race is pinned (879 px sticky stage), and chapters 01–05 are stacked sticky sheets that cover each other, which is real "in and out". After the sheets, Install, Open source, Q&A and the agent prompt scroll past as a plain document. Between the race's last link ("Every coding task and both runs →") and the next section there is a 188 px empty black band, so the page sags there.

**3. Does every section hold a real visual object?** Yes. Terminal plus diff, the race clocks, the place carousel (terminal / web / cloud / Slack mini-UIs), command blocks, Kai's 5-step ladder, the sandbox frame, the Gen harness diagram, install tabs with code, the accordion and the closing band. "Read every line" (open source) is the thinnest: two links and a sentence, with no object of its own.

**4. One ask in the sticky header, nothing floating; scale has rhythm?** Not clean. On desktop the header says "Try Hanzo" (outline) while the hero says "Open Dev". It only switches to a solid "Open Dev" once the hero ask has scrolled off. On phone the header stays a **solid black "Try Hanzo"** for the whole page, so the first screen has two solid fills with two different labels. In the closing band, the header's solid white "Open Dev" and the band's solid white "Open Dev" are on screen together. Scale: the h1 at 52 px and 3.02× at 160 px give one big moment each. All five chapter headings, though, are 26 px in the same copy-left / object-right white sheet, so the middle third is all medium with no growth or shrink. The cookie bar covers the hero terminal on first visit at both sizes (about 210 px tall on phone).

**5. Does every hover and press answer, without jumps?** Mostly. Hero "Open Dev": background #0a0a0a → #2c2c2e, press scale 0.98. Text links: translateY(1px). The carousel arrows, install tabs and Kai rows change colour or fill and press at 0.97. Copy writes exactly `curl -fsSL https://hanzo.sh | sh` and the button says "Copied". The **header ask fails**: hover only drops opacity to 0.88, which is a banned "button that fades on hover". The race's "Replay race" hover is a faint 10% ring that you barely see.

**6. Overlaps, font swap or layout shift?** No measured layout shift (CLS < 0.001). No font swap: the h1 box is stable. On phone the hero terminal is 387 px wide on a 375 px screen (left −6, right 381) and is clipped at both edges. A hydration-mismatch console error shows the red Next "1 Issue" badge on phone and in the reduced-motion run. That is a gate failure.

**7. Which reference does it feel like, and is its motion there?** It feels like an Apple product page: a pinned race stage, a stacked-sheet chapter run and a big closing statement with a blur cut-in. The pinned-scene motion is genuinely there. The anthropic.com "benchmark as hero figure" idea is there in the 3.02×, but its proof clocks do not count up. Each clock reads **0.00 s while it is "RUNNING"** and then jumps to 2.29 s, or to 9.52 s after about 9.5 s. Most visitors will also see that 9.52 / 2.29 is 4.2×, while the headline says 3.02×, and nothing on screen reconciles the two.

**8. Different from its neighbours?** Yes. /api has a request-builder card on the right, /chat a chat window on the right, and /agents a dark stepped stack. /dev is the only one with a full-width split terminal and diff under a left h1. Its signature (a self-running demo) is its own.

**9. All 18 parts covered?** Mostly: skip link, progress bar, one h1, sticky header, sound toggle, pinned scene, carousel, tabs, copy confirmation, accordion, closing band and footer are all present. The gaps are the solid-fill rule (Part 4), the error badge (gate 3), and an objection strip that reads as a generic Q&A accordion with the first item open.

**10. Banned tells?** One: the header button fades on hover (opacity 0.88). There are no gradients, Inter, green, eyebrow above the h1, or pill bar. The small mono labels ("— 01 MODELS") sit under headings in grey, which is allowed.

**11. Copy untouched?** Yes. `parity.py /dev` reports 176/176 text and 12/12 links.

## Fixes (by impact)
1. **Make the diff land.** In the hero diff pane (`src/retry.ts`), show the change as a real hunk: the removed line with a "−" in grey strikethrough, the added line or lines with "+" in white, and a `+N −N` count in the file tab. Animate the landing over 300–400 ms: rows slide in 8 px and the highlight sweeps left to right. Fill the pane or shrink it, so there are no more than 40 px of empty black under the last line. Have the demo finish and settle by 7 s.
2. **Fit the hero object on the first screen.** Desktop terminal bottom must be ≤ 801 (now 850): cut its height to about 400 px or move it up about 50 px. On phone, the terminal must be inset ≥ 0 px (now −6 / 381); use left 16 and right 359, matching the gutter.
3. **Make the race clocks count.** Both clocks must tick up from 0.00 at real-time speed while "RUNNING". Hanzo should finish at 2.29 s while Claude Code keeps ticking to 9.52 s, with each ending in a subtle settle and the "done" border. No clock may read 0.00 s while its bar fills. Raise "Replay race" hover to at least a solid 1 px ring or a fill change.
4. **One solid fill, one label.** The header ask reads "Open Dev" from load at both sizes. It stays quiet (outline) while the hero ask is visible and goes solid only after the hero ask leaves view. On phone, replace the permanently solid "Try Hanzo" with this rule. Hide the header's solid state while the closing band's "Open Dev" is on screen.
5. **Remove the header hover fade.** On the header ask, change opacity 0.88 to a background change (outline → #f5f5f5 fill, or solid #0a0a0a → #2c2c2e), keeping the 0.97 press.
6. **Fix the hydration mismatch** so the phone and reduced-motion runs show 0 console errors and no red "1 Issue" badge.
7. **Give the chapter run scale rhythm.** Of the five 26 px copy-left / object-right sheets, flip the layout on at least 2 (object left, or full-width object). Let one object grow past its rest size while it is the front sheet (e.g. the Kai ladder or the Gen diagram at 1.15×, settling back to 1.0).
8. **Close the 188 px black gap** after "Every coding task and both runs →" to ≤ 96 px, or overlap the next section by 40 px as the brief asks.
9. **"Read every line" needs an object:** e.g. the repo line `hanzoai/dev` as a one-line terminal with the Apache-2.0 badge and the existing release tag. Use no new copy.
10. **Keep the cookie bar off the demo:** on phone, compact it to ≤ 120 px high, or dock it so it doesn't cover the terminal's prompt lines on first visit.
