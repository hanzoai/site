I walked it with Playwright (Chromium), with video recorded, at 1470x801 (wheel in 100px notches, 140 ms dwell, frame every third notch) and at 375x812 (isMobile, hasTouch, 100px steps, 160 ms dwell). I also checked:
- idle frames at 100, 400, 900, 1500 and 2600 ms, plus an idle pixel diff;
- a pointer sweep across the hero field;
- hover and press on the hero ask, Back to Zen, the Pause button, the Zen 6 node, all three rail stations and the room ask;
- pointer drift in the research room;
- header ask labels at 0, 900, 1800 and 2700px;
- a 30-stop tab crawl;
- reduced motion at both sizes (`getAnimations()` after 3 s);
- console output over 9 fresh loads (domcontentloaded, load and networkidle, at both sizes and with reduced motion);
- overflow and touch targets at 375.

## What works
- **The signature is real and the page's own.** A giant 7 with Zen 7's Sigil field showing only through the glyph. It starts at scale 1.5 low on the right and settles at scale 1 beside the 52px title by about 400px of scroll. At scroll 0 it now reads as a 7 (the whole top bar and diagonal show), not as a slab.
- **It moves within 1 s untouched.** The h1 cuts in by line mask between 100 and 800 ms. The field inside the 7 drifts without input (the idle frames differ) and bends to the pointer (the sweep changes the frame). Pause works: it holds the frame exactly (0 bytes differ), sets aria-pressed, and its label reads "Play the moving fields".
- **The lineage is now a moment, not a list.** It stays pinned for about one screen while your scroll carries a marker from Zen 6 to Zen 7. Each station inverts to ink as the marker passes and underlines its own phrase in the paragraph above. Zen 7 fills black when the marker arrives. Hovering a station also underlines its phrase. The phone does the same down a vertical line, and that line now draws all the way.
- **The hovers say something.** The Zen 6 facts part 4, 8 and 12px (strokes parting, an allowed move) and the card steps from #f5f5f5 to #ededed. The hero ask presses at scale(.98). Back to Zen nudges its arrow 4px. The room's line-art 7 drifts toward the pointer (about 20px seen).
- **The scale has rhythm.** There is one big thing per screen (the 7, then the lineage, then the 44px room head), with a 52px h1, 21px lede and 14px mono facts around it. The palette is monochrome and nothing sits above the h1.
- **The header takes the page's ask once the hero ask leaves view.** At 1800px it is a solid "Apply for research access", and inside the black room it turns quiet. The research room enters as a rounded card and opens to full width (clip inset 32px to 0). There is no horizontal scroll at 375, and no touch target under 44px.

## What fails (ranked, each with a concrete fix the owner can make in the page's own files)
1. **Reduced motion breaks the lineage, and the page throws a hydration error.** Under reduced motion every load logs "A tree hydrated but some attributes… didn't match… This won't be patched up". The diff points at the page's own markup:
   - `li className="lit"` against `""`;
   - `z7-node7 here` against `z7-node7`;
   - the room path's `strokeDasharray` "1 1" against "0 1".

   Because React does not patch these, the reduced-motion final state is wrong. No station is lit, no phrase is underlined, and Zen 7 is not filled, even though the marker already sits at the end. The red dev "1 Issue" badge also showed in my normal-motion walk at both sizes (2 of 9 loads logged the same hydration error without reduced motion). This fails DONE item 3 and gate item 5.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.jsx`:** do not branch rendered classes or `initial` on `useReducedMotion()` during the first render. Its value is null on the server and true on the client. Add a `mounted` flag (`useEffect(() => setMounted(true), [])`) and use `const calm = mounted && rm`. Better still, drive the lit and here states purely from CSS under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` (for example `.z7-rail li { background:#0a0a0a; color:#fafafa }`, `.z7-node7 { background:#0a0a0a }`, `.z7-para mark { text-decoration-color: currentColor }`). For the room path, always render `initial={{ pathLength: 0 }}` and let the global reduced-motion rule finish it, or set `style={{ pathLength: 1 }}` after mount.
2. **There is a dead white gap after the hero on desktop.** Once the 7 settles (at about 400px) the stage stays pinned to 560px. When it releases, the 7 slides up while a band of about 330px of empty white (only the Pause pill) scrolls past before "What Zen 7 brings" arrives. One thing goes out, but nothing comes in.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.css`:** cut `.z7-stage` from `170svh` to about `130svh` (the settle already ends at 0.7 of the stage). Then pull `.z7-brings` up with `margin-top: -40px` (40px overlap per MASTER part 3), and drop `.z7-brings-pin` top padding to `44px` on desktop.
3. **On the phone the signature still is not on the first screen.** At 375x812 the black of the 7 starts at about y=591, so roughly 70px of it sits inside the 660px line (the brief asks for 80px or more of the object). In practice the cookie card hides it completely. The first screen is the h1, lede, a full-width button, Back to Zen and a breadcrumb.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.css` (max-width 899px):**
   - Put Back to Zen beside the primary on one row: `.z7-acts { flex-wrap: nowrap; gap: 17px }` with the button at `padding: 0 20px`.
   - Move `.z7-crumb` below `.z7-art` with `order: 3`.
   - Cut `.z7-copy` padding-top to `44px`.
   - Lower `.z7-art`'s rise to `translateY(calc(3svh * var(--k)))`.

   That brings the top bar and diagonal of the 7 to about y≈470. The cookie card is shared chrome, so do not count it against the page, but the object has to be above it.
4. **The breadcrumb is visibly misaligned.** At both sizes "Zen" (the link) sits about 12px lower than "/ Zen 7". The link is an `inline-flex` box 44px tall with centred text, while the second `li` sets its text at the top of its line. It looks broken right under the ask.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.css`:** add `align-items: center` to `.z7-crumb ol`, and give `.z7-crumb li` `display: inline-flex; align-items: center; min-height: 44px`.
5. **The room's line-art 7 runs through the paragraph and the button.** The 22% white diagonal crosses "Apache 2.0", "research" and "feedback" at 375, and passes behind the lede at 1470. A line through body text reads as a strike or a glitch, and it is the kind of thing over content that James rejects.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.css`:** move `.z7-seven` off the text column. For example, use `left: auto; right: -8%; top: 50%; width: min(520px, 70vw)`, or on phone place it above the heading as a 120px mark (`position: relative; margin: 0 auto 27px; width: 120px`). Alternatively, drop its stroke to white 10% inside the text block with a mask (`mask-image: linear-gradient(...)` behind `.z7-access-g`).
6. **The stations flash empty as they light.** In the desktop walk one frame caught "expanded multimodal reasoning" with no box at all, because its outline vanished before the ink fill arrived. `box-shadow` is not in the transition list, and the fill goes from white to black over 150 ms.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.css`:** keep the outline through the change (`.z7-rail li.lit { box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #0a0a0a }` instead of `none`). Transition `background-color` and `color` at `300ms var(--z7-emph)` to match the scale.
7. **The hero ask hover is only a colour shift with a hint of blue.** The solid ask goes from #0a0a0a to #2c2c2e, and the room ask from #f5f5f7 to #fff. Both are kit values with chroma outside the deck ramp. This is shared (`Cta` in `kit.jsx`), so it is noted here, not counted. **Request** in `docs/requests/zen-7.md`: the kit hover should use deck greys (#262626 on ink, #ffffff on light). The page could add an allowed move of its own, such as a magnetic pull up to 22px on `.z7-acts .x-btn` for fine pointers.

Shared, not counted: on the phone the header keeps a solid "Try Hanzo" beside the page's solid ask on the first screen (two solid fills). The cookie card at 375 is 203px tall and covers the lower quarter of every phone frame. The header glass smears the black 7 into grey blobs as it passes under it on desktop.
