# /zen/7 — James-eye review

Verdict: 6/10
First complaint: "The big 7 is nice, but after that it goes dead. It's three little white boxes on a line and a black box. And on my phone I only see text and a button; the 7 is below the fold and doesn't do anything."

Walked with Playwright (Chromium) at 1470x801 (wheel in 100px steps with 120 ms dwell, video plus frame bursts) and at 375x812 (isMobile, hasTouch, 100px steps, 140 ms dwell). Tested: idle frames at 150/600/1100/2600 ms, a pointer sweep across the field, hover and press on the hero ask, Back to Zen, the Zen 6 node and the rail chips, a 40-stop tab crawl, reduced motion at both sizes (getAnimations() after 3 s = 0), and overflow at 375 (none: scrollWidth 375). Working files: /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-hanzo-site/715b2a45-dd44-5306-98d1-bc0b4d91bc34/scratchpad/critic/zen-7/

## What works
- **A real signature with its own idea.** A giant 7 with Zen 7's Sigil field showing only through the glyph. It starts large at the bottom right and settles beside the 52px title as you scroll. It's scrubbed, you can read it, and it's different from its Zen neighbours.
- **Something moves within 1 s untouched.** The h1 cuts in by line mask (about 600 ms) and the field inside the 7 drifts and bends a little toward the pointer. This is idle, pointer and arrival motion, not only scroll.
- **The scale has rhythm on the first screen.** One big thing (the 7), a 52px h1, a 21px lede and small mono details. The palette is monochrome, nothing sits above the h1, and the copy reads as Hanzo's.
- **The research room** enters as a rounded card inside the gutters and opens to full width on scroll. The desktop header swaps its ask to "Apply for research access" after the hero.
- **The hero ask presses** at scale(.98). Back to Zen nudges its arrow 4px. Reduced motion shows the settled final state with every station visible. There is no horizontal scroll at 375.

## What fails (ranked, each with a concrete fix the owner can make in the page's own files)
1. **The page goes dead after the hero (sections 2 and 3 are thin).** The lineage is two flat cards and three plain outlined chips on a hairline. The chips fade up (a Rise), have no hover, and say nothing new: they repeat words from the paragraph directly above. The dark room is a centred headline, paragraph and button over faint concentric rings.
   **Fix in `components/pages/model/ZenSeven.jsx` and `.css`:** make the lineage the moment. Pin `.z7-line` for about 100svh and scrub a marker from Zen 6 to Zen 7 along the rail. Each station grows from 14px to 17px as the marker passes and settles back, and the Zen 7 node inverts or fills only when the marker arrives. Give the chips a hover state that tells you something, such as the matching phrase in `.z7-para` underlining. In the room, let the rings answer the pointer, or reuse the 7 glyph mask small behind "Research access" so the room echoes the signature.
2. **On the phone the signature is gone.** At 375 the 7 starts at y≈667, below the 660px mark (the brief asks for 80px or more of the object inside 660px). It is fixed at `transform: none !important`, and the first screen is text, a button and a breadcrumb under 116px of empty top padding.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.css` (@media max-width 899px):** cut `.z7-copy` padding-top to about 72px, move the breadcrumb below the art or drop it out of the first screen, and size `.z7-art` to about 38svh so the top bar of the 7 sits inside the first screen. Give it a small scroll-linked settle on phone too: a smaller `--k` range (translateY 8svh to 0, scale 1.15 to 1) driven by the same `useScroll`, instead of `transform: none`.
3. **The console has an error, and a red "1 Issue" badge sits on screen at both sizes.** It is a React hydration mismatch in the footer's `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` (`FooterHTML`, shared chrome). This fails DONE item 3.
   **Fix:** this is shared, so write `docs/requests/zen-7.md` asking the footer owner to make the server and client footer HTML identical. Do not mark the page ready until the badge is gone.
4. **The phone rail line stops short.** At 375 the vertical hairline draws only about 100px and never reaches the Zen 7 card, even 2.5 s after it comes into view. The cause is `pathLength` with `vectorEffect="non-scaling-stroke"` on a stretched `viewBox="0 0 2 100"`.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.jsx`:** drop the non-scaling-stroke on `.z7-path.v`, or draw the line as a 1px div with `scaleY` from 0 to 1 (`transform-origin: top`) under `whileInView`. Do the same for the horizontal line so both match.
5. **At scroll 0 the hero object reads as a cropped black slab.** Only the top bar of the 7 and the start of its stroke show, cut off by the bottom edge. It reads as a 7 only once you scroll. A white gap of about 250px then opens as the pin releases and the 7 slides up behind the header.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.css`:** start `--k=1` at about `translate(-12vw, 18svh) scale(1.5)` so the whole glyph silhouette reads on first paint. Cut `.z7-stage` from 210svh to about 170svh, or let `.z7-brings` overlap the stage by 40px (margin-top: -40px; position: relative), so one thing comes in as the other goes out.
6. **There are two solid fills with two different labels on the first screen.** The header shows a solid "Try Hanzo" next to the solid "Apply for research access" on desktop at first paint and on the phone throughout. The phone header never takes the page's ask.
   **Fix:** add `data-ask-hide` handling or the page-ask hook the desktop header already reads, so the header carries "Apply for research access" (quiet) on /zen/7 from the start at every width. If that needs shared code, put it in `docs/requests/zen-7.md`.
7. **The hovers are thin.** The Zen 6 node lifts 4px with translateY, which is not on James's list of allowed hover moves (tilt to 2.5°, magnetic, strokes parting, art drift). The hero ask only shifts colour by one step (#0a0a0a to #2c2c2e, a hint of blue from the kit). The chips do nothing.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.css`:** give the node a 2.5° pointer tilt, or its mono facts sliding 4px apart. Give the chips a hover fill to `#f5f5f5` that links to their phrase. Ask the kit owner about the `#2c2c2e` hover.
8. **The continuous movers have no pause control.** The Sigil fields in the hero and the room animate indefinitely, and the page has no pause control (MASTER part 12 asks for one past 5 s). The breadcrumb's "Zen" link is a 21x24 target.
   **Fix:** add the page pause (the "Zen names" commit mentions one shared pause for films and demos, so wire `.z7-sig` and `.z7-sig2` to it), and give `.z7-crumb a` `min-height: 44px; padding-inline: 10px`.
