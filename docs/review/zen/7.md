## What fails (ranked, each with a concrete fix the owner can make in the page's own files)
1. **On the phone, the 7 never settles.** The top comment says it settles "(and on phone)", but at 375 `.z7-art` stays at `scale(1.1) translateY(24px)` on every one of the 45 scroll steps. On phone `.z7-stage` is `height:auto`, so it is no taller than its own content, and `useScroll({offset:['start start','end end']})` has no range to travel. `--k` stays at 1. The phone's signature is a static black 7 with a faint drift.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.jsx` (`Stage`):** use `usePhone()` and give the phone its own range, for example `offset: phone ? ['start start', 'end start'] : ['start start', 'end end']` with `useTransform(p, phone ? [0, 0.45] : [0, 0.7], [1, 0])`. Alternatively, target the art itself with `['start end', 'center center']`. Either way the phone 7 rises and settles over the first 300px or so.
2. **The desktop moment is over in a flick, then the hero leaves like a document.** The 7 goes from scale 1.5 to 1 in about 170px of wheel. The stage is only 130svh, so it is pinned for about 240px. After that the whole hero slides up, and the Pause pill rides out alone across a white band of roughly 180px before "What Zen 7 brings" arrives. One thing goes out, but nothing comes in yet.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.css` / `ZenSeven.jsx`:** make the settle the length of the pin. Keep `.z7-stage` at `130svh`, but map `k` over `[0, 1]` instead of `[0, 0.7]`, so the 7 is still moving when the release starts. Then hand over:
   - Let `.z7-copy` lift 24px and fade to 0 over the last 20% of the stage (`useTransform(p, [0.8, 1], [0, -24])`), so the copy goes out as the lineage comes in.
   - Tie the Pause pill to the 7's box (put `.z7-pause` inside `.z7-art`'s parent at the art's lower right, `bottom: 14svh`) so it leaves with the art instead of trailing in empty white.
   - Drop `.z7-brings-pin` top padding from 116px to 72px on desktop.
3. **The field inside the 7 barely reads as alive.** At rest the 7 is a near-solid black slab with 6 to 8 thin hairlines. At a 5-second glance it is a black numeral, not "Zen 7's living field". The drift is real but too faint to notice without hunting for it.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.css` / `ZenSeven.jsx`:** give the field more presence inside the glyph. Raise the `.z7-sig` line colour from `rgba(255,255,255,.8)` on sparse lines to a denser seed or a higher `data` count (for example `data={[7, 6, 2.0, 27.3]}`, or whatever Sigil takes to add rings). Or lay a second, slower Sigil at white 18% under it, so the 7 is visibly full of moving structure. Stay monochrome, and keep it to one continuous mover.
4. **There is a white gap between the released lineage and the room.** After the marker reaches Zen 7, the pin releases and about 245px of empty white passes (frame d-w-024) before the room's rounded card arrives. This is the same "one goes out, nothing comes in" beat as fix 2, in a smaller dose.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.css`:** cut `.z7-brings-pin` bottom padding from 72px to 27px, and pull `.z7-access` up 40px (`margin-top: -40px; position: relative; z-index: 1`) so the card overlaps the lineage's tail by 40px, as MASTER part 3 asks.
5. **The room's Sigil rings run behind the lede.** The large ring set sits right of centre, and its outer rings pass behind "early evaluation endpoints" and the ask at 1470. On phone they are centred straight behind the heading and lede. At white 30% they read as scratches through the words.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.css`:** mask the field out of the text column, for example `.z7-sig2 { mask-image: radial-gradient(ellipse 420px 220px at 50% 50%, transparent 60%, #000 100%) }` (on phone `ellipse 200px 260px`). Or drop `.z7-sig2`'s colour to `rgba(255,255,255,.14)`, a deck step.
6. **The stations rest as three outlined boxes in a row.** Before the marker reaches them, the three phrases sit as equal-weight outlined chips between two cards, which comes close to the banned three-boxes look in the first frame of the lineage.
   **Fix in `ZenSeven.css`:** at rest, show the stations as plain 14px grey text sitting on the line with a 6px hollow tick (no outline: `box-shadow: none; background: #fff; padding: 6.5px 10.5px`). Keep the filled ink chip only for `.lit`, so the box itself is what arrives.
