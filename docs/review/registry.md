# /registry: James-eye review (independent walk, 7 Nov, second pass)

Verdict: 7/10
First complaint: "The push is good and the layer stack finally does something. Then it falls into the same ending as every cloud page: a console full of someone else's services, a paste box, 'get started', three grey cards, then 'get started' again. It goes from a moment to a template."

Walked moving with Playwright at 1470x801 (hover-capable pointer, wheel through Lenis, video recorded) and 375x812 (touch), cookie bar rejected. Parity: `/registry: OK text 69/69 links 12/12`. 0 console errors at both sizes. Layout-shift total about 0.0004 on desktop (header tools and the people chips in step 06) and 0.0003 on phone. No horizontal scroll. No hue in any screen. Proof: `img/registry-hero.jpg` (the hero at rest after the push, with dead white in the registry pane) and `img/registry-tail.jpg` (the generic console and paste block, then "Get started").

## The 11 answers

1. **Sells in 5 s, moves in 1 s?** Yes on desktop. h1, lede and a solid View Source sit beside a terminal and registry window. By 1 s `docker push` is running: the 401 names Hanzo IAM, the token is minted, the signature checked, four layer bars fill, and the image and cosign signature land in `acme/api`. It rests at about 9 s on a blinking caret, and a Replay button appears. The registry pane still holds about 130px of empty white between `acme/api` and its rows, and more below them. On phone, h1, lede and ask fit the first screen and the window starts at y 464, already running. But the phone version cuts the registry pane to a strip of two chips above the terminal, so the result (the image landing) sits above its cause and the `acme/api` name is lost.
2. **One in, one out, or a document?** For the first two-thirds, yes. The hero, then the dark room, then the pinned "Built on Hanzo IAM" stack, where each of the six steps does one visible thing to one big object: the password is struck as the token seats, the org plate inks with `acme/* · push`, the signature and attestation dock, /v2/ grows big, a tag slides out, and four people fold into one directory with one revoked. After that it becomes a document: a console mock, Paste and ship, a "Get started with Registry" strip, Related capabilities, then the "Get Registry" close. That is five quiet blocks in a row, and three of them are asks.
3. **A real visual in every section?** Yes: the push window, the layer stack, the terminal and swap ledger, the console mock, cards with contour art, and the close art. But the console object is not this product. It lists `gateway / payments-api / checkout-web / docs-site · Ready`, and its caption cycles "A build is running from the last commit on main", which is about Builds, not images in a registry. No Hanzo media exists for Registry, so the 4-image block does not apply.
4. **One ask in the header, nothing floating; scale rhythm?** On the page, yes: View Source in the hero and the close (`data-cta` registry.hero and registry.close). The desktop header swaps to View Source once the hero ask leaves view, and is solid only when the hero ask is gone. Scale has rhythm: the h1 is 52px, section and step heads are 32px, /v2/ grows only on its step, the swap phrases are about 28px, the close is 64px, and captions are 13px mono. But "Get started with Registry" plus View on GitHub (same github.com/hanzoai destination, a different label) sits two blocks above the close's View Source, so the end asks the same thing twice.
5. **Every hover and press answers, no jumps?** Mostly. Hovering a log line or repo row scrubs the push to that moment (step 5 set `data-t=5`; the push resumed and rested at 6 on leave), and tapping does the same on phone. On the hero ask, hover moves the fill from #0a0a0a to #262626 and press scales it to .97. The swap switch outlines on hover, and pressing it strikes through the heading and wipes in IAM's phrases row by row. The ghost button fills on hover. The related cards only take a 4% grey tint on hover: nothing is previewed and nothing moves. No layout jump measured.
6. **Overlap, font swap, layout shift?** No overlap at either size, no font swap seen, and CLS is negligible. The dark-room ledger now has one hairline per row (the double rule is gone). On desktop about 170px of empty black still separates Quickstart from the ledger, so at 801px tall the switch's payoff (the rows) starts at the bottom edge of the screen. You press the switch and mostly see the heading strike.
7. **Which reference, and is its motion there?** apple.com: a pinned scene where one object changes, and that motion is now really there. A step counter and a progress bar run with the scroll. Nothing from synchronized.studio is here: the related cards are plain links with no card-to-page open.
8. **Different from its neighbours?** The top half is its own: the push-asks-IAM window, the secret switch and the layer stack. The bottom half is the shared cloud skeleton used by Node, S3, Network, Pubsub, O11y, Metrics, Mq and Realtime: a LaunchConsole with the same example services, Paste, RelatedCards, then LaunchClose with contour art. Those blocks come in the same order on every one of those pages.
9. **All 18 parts?** Largely. The page has the header comment (Persona, Goal, CTA, Metric, Signature, Beyond), one h1 cut in by LineMask, `data-cta` and `data-thumb`, a reduced-motion final state (`calm` jumps to step 6), a pause and Replay control on the push, Copy on both code blocks and JsonLd. Not shown on the page: a card Morph into its page. The sound kit was not checked in this walk.
10. **Banned tells?** None. There is no hue, no gradient text, no label above the h1 or an h2 (the step number sits under its heading), no Inter, and no fade-on-hover. "Related capabilities" is itself a 13px mono h2 over a card list, which is allowed.
11. **Copy untouched?** Yes, by parity (69/69 text, 12/12 links). The labels on the drawn objects stitch Hanzo's phrases together ("deleted · blobs reclaimed", "one directory of people · Hanzo Cloud", "401 · token from Hanzo IAM"). They are aria-hidden and make no new claims, but they sit at the edge of "never merge copy".

**Shared chrome (not counted against the page):** On phone, the header keeps a solid "Try Hanzo" on the same first screen as the solid hero "View Source", so there are two fills with two different words. Later the bottom bar says View Source while the header still says Try Hanzo. The contour art in the RelatedCards thumbnails is cut off by a hard vertical edge about 90px into a 160px panel (in the Environments and Builds thumbnails), which looks clipped.

## Fixes to reach 8

1. **Break the template tail.** Replace the generic LaunchConsole, with its unrelated services and the Builds caption, with Registry's own object: either the hero window continuing (the signed `v1.2.3` pulled by a deploy), or the console listing `acme/api` tags, cosign signatures and attestations. Use only the page's own names and phrases. Neighbours must not end in the same order.
2. **Take one of the two endings out of the way.** Fold "Get started with Registry / View on GitHub" into the close's area (as its caption-level secondary), or move it above the console. The end should not be strip, cards, then close with two links to the same GitHub URL in different words.
3. **Give the related cards a reason to hover.** A card-to-page open (Morph) on at least "Builds: Source to signed image", or a hover that previews something (its own small object moving), not a 4% tint.
4. **Bring the switch's payoff into view.** Cut the empty black between Quickstart and the ledger in the dark room (about 170px at 1470x801) so the rows that wipe in sit on the same screen as the switch.
5. **Close the hero pane's dead white.** End the registry pane where its content ends, or let the rows sit under `acme/api`, so the window has no empty middle at rest.
6. **Fix the phone hero order.** At 375, show `acme/api` and let the image rows land after the terminal, not as a strip above it, so cause comes before effect.
