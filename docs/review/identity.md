# /identity — James-eye review
7 Nov, walked at 1470x801 and 375x812 (isMobile, hasTouch) with Playwright: frames at 0, 1 and 3 s untouched, wheel-scrolled a screen at a time with 1.5 s dwell, hovered and pressed the main controls on desktop, tapped them on phone, checked overflow at 375 and 320, reduced motion, CLS and console over 4 clean loads.

Verdict: 5/10

First complaint: "The top's nice, the little sign-in plays itself. Then halfway down it turns into a wall of grey boxes with paragraphs in every one, and the 'every app' bit is nine blank grey squares. That looks unfinished, not premium."

## The 11 answers
1. **First screen sells, and moves within 1 s: yes.** "Hanzo Identity" cuts in and the device card types a password, then fills six second-factor dots on its own (t0 empty, t3 password dots and 3 of 6 code dots). The left half is very empty at 1470: an h1 and a lede float in about 480 px of white, with nothing small or medium around them.
2. **One in, one out: partly.** The pinned 01/02/03 stage (Signing in → What the token carries → One sign-in, every app) is the page's best moment: one panel changes while the copy swaps. After it the page is a document: a six-tile bento of paragraphs, a band of tabs, a form, a black block with 3 steps and a closing CTA. On phone the pinned stage unpins into three stacked copies of the same device card, so the phone gets no in/out at all.
3. **A real object in every section: no.** Some objects are real: the device demo, the live white-label preview (the chips really toggle the GitHub button in the preview) and the OTP dots. Others are placeholders: "One sign-in, every app" is 9 blank grey gradient squares; "Accounts and membership" is 5 grey gradient circles standing in for avatars; the "Running it for a large organization" tab panels are text plus tick lists; and "Add sign-in" is three numbered text pills. There are no Hanzo media and no product logos.
4. **One ask, nothing floating, scale rhythm: mixed.** The header holds "Try Hanzo" (shared). The page then repeats Try Hanzo with an equal-weight secondary three more times, and the close has 3 asks (Try Hanzo / Read the docs / View on GitHub). Scale: section h2s sit at 32 px rather than the 26 px resting size. In "Add sign-in" the three step labels are about 32 px bold in three equal stacked boxes, competing with the 44 px h2, so that whole screen is big at once. The pause button is a 44 px circle hanging over the device card's title bar and right edge.
5. **Every hover and press answers: no.** Primary button hover only darkens the fill (#0a0a0a to #2c2c2e, an off-token grey) and press scales to 0.98, which is good. Secondary gets a shadow ring. Tabs and chips get a fill and press-scale. The six bento tiles and the three steps do nothing on hover or press. Nothing gives a hover detail, and there is no tilt, magnetic ask or pointer light anywhere.
6. **Overlap, font swap or shift: overlap on phone.** At 375 the token row "organizations" runs straight into "they belong to" with no gap, and "to" wraps under it. The pause circle sits on top of the "hanzo.id Sign-in" title bar and covers its border. CLS is 0.0002, with no visible font swap and no horizontal overflow at 375 or 320 (body is 352 at 320 from the shared header only). Console is clean over 4 loads: the red "1 Issue" badge in my first walk came from a screenshot taken before hydration (a Playwright artifact), so it doesn't count. Reduced motion holds the hero on its empty first frame (blank field, faint "second factor") instead of the finished sign-in.
7. **Reference: apple.com, half there.** The pinned stage with one changing panel is Apple's. Apple's sub-nav with a persistent ask, huge type and one idea per screen are missing below the fold. There is nothing of synchronized.studio (no card opens into its page), no count-up, no scroll-tied marquee and no pointer motion. The motion mix is a self-typing demo, one pinned scroll stage, tabs, chips and arrival cut-ins.
8. **Different from neighbours: not proven.** The hero medium (a scripted device-UI demo beside a left headline) is a product-page pattern other cloud pages are likely to share. The signature, the live white-label preview, is the page's own and should be pushed further.
9. **18 parts: gaps.** There is no proof or trust part (no customers or logos), no sub-nav, no FAQ or open-detail part, and the conversion asks are duplicated rather than graded. Navigation, footer, states (tabs, chips) and responsiveness are present.
10. **Banned tells: yes.**
    - "01 / 03" and "01 / 04" mono counters sit directly above the h2s and h3s (an eyebrow above a headline).
    - The bento's second row is 3 equal boxes in a row (Single sign-on / Roles that travel / Passkeys), each a small diagram plus a heading plus a 3 to 5 line paragraph, and all six tiles carry an explaining paragraph (clutter).
    - Off-token greys: #f5f5f7 tiles and #2c2c2e hover are Apple greys, not Hanzo's ramp (#f5f5f5, #262626).
    - The black "Add sign-in" room is flat full bleed with about 280 px of dead black under the steps. It is not a rounded card that opens to full width.
11. **Copy untouched: not contradicted.** I saw nothing rewritten. The step labels "set the callback" and "install the SDK" are lower-case fragments of the lede; check they are Hanzo's own wording.

## Fixes, in order
1. Remove the "01 / 03" and "01 / 04" counters from above the headings. Move the position to a small progress mark beside or below the device card or tab panel.
2. Replace the 9 blank grey squares with real app tiles: Hanzo's own app marks (Chat, Dev, Cloud...) lighting up one after another as "one session" spreads. Replace the grey avatar circles with real monogram avatars that join one by one.
3. Break the bento's equal row of three. Use an 8/5 split with one large live tile (Passkeys or the second factor, animated) and the others smaller. Cut each tile to its heading and one line, and put the full paragraph behind an open-on-click detail.
4. Give every tile and step a hover that says something: tilt to 2.5°, the tile's diagram plays (dots fill, the role nest highlights project → workspace → organization, the audit log appends a "write"), and press translateY(1px).
5. Fix the phone overlaps. Let the token row stack key over value, or give it a fixed key column with a gap. Bring the pause button inside the title bar at 32 px, aligned to its centre and clear of "Sign-in", on both sizes.
6. On phone, keep the pinned stage as one sticky device card whose panel swaps, not three stacked copies. Make the tab list a horizontal scroll-snap rail, not a vertical stack.
7. Under reduced motion, show the finished hero state (password filled, all six code dots filled, Continue resolved), not the empty first frame.
8. Rebuild "Add sign-in" as a rounded black card inside the gutters that opens to full width on arrival. Bring step labels down to 17 to 21 px so the h2 is the one big thing, and remove the dead black band below. Make the second button there quiet (a ghost or link), not an equal white pill.
9. Snap colours to the ramp: tiles #f5f5f5 or #f2f2f2, primary hover #262626 or #171717.
10. Add one more kind of motion: a pointer-aware sheen on the device card (silver-room) or a magnetic primary ask, so the page isn't only typing and scroll.
11. Fill the empty left half of the hero at 1470: tighten the 8/5 split so the headline block and device sit as one composition, and let the device start slightly larger and settle as the first scroll begins.
12. Close with one ask: Try Hanzo, then "Read the docs" as a link and GitHub as a small tertiary link, rather than three equal-weight items.
