## Fixes, in order
1. **Phone overflow in "Add sign-in".** Let the client id row wrap or shrink: give `.id-made-id` `min-width:0; overflow:hidden`, cap the bar count to the panel width, or let the grid track use `minmax(0,1fr)`. Re-check step 1 at 375 and 320 so the steps and the card sit inside the 16 px gutters.
2. **Make the masked client id read as masked.** Use a mono string with a few visible characters and bullets, or a single shimmer bar. Don't use a row of rounded pale bars that look like tofu.
3. **Separate the hero from /iam.** Lead with Identity's own signature: let the first screen show the pinned card morphing or the white-label hostname swap, so it isn't a static white sign-in card beside a left h1. Keep the h1 and lede words as they are.
4. **Kill the dead space in the pinned card.** Size it to the tallest real state, or vertically centre each state, so the hero rest state has no empty bottom third. At 375, stop panel 01 clipping the "second factor" chip, and align the pause glyph with "hanzo.id" and "Sign-in" in the bar.
5. **Close on a different object than the white-label card.** For example, settle the steps panel, or the app grid from the pinned stage, at small scale. Don't show the same sign-in card two screens in a row.
6. **Put the copy back.**
   - Return "White-labelled by hostname — your mark on your domain, never ours" to a bullet and give that section no invented h2.
   - Drop "or" and the "OPENID CONNECT · SCIM 2.0 · WEBAUTHN · GDPR" strip, or show those only as standards inside an existing object.
   - Use "Sign-in" as in live.
   - Use Hanzo's full sentence for the client id caption.
7. **Give the page one scale moment after the hero.** Let one object grow past its resting size as it is reached, then settle. The app grid turning black or the steps room opening to full width are candidates. Right now every section sits at the same medium volume.
8. **Phone bento.** Six full-width tall tiles is 2.5 screens of grey. Shorten the tiles on phone (shrink the visual area) or pair the small ones, so one thing comes in as one goes out.
9. **Polish.**
   - Soften or remove the grey pointer-following radial on the hero; it reads as a smudge.
   - Fix the stair-stepped tilt edges on the wide tile (add `backface-visibility:hidden` or a 1 px transparent outline).
   - Under reduced motion, render the token rows at once instead of staging them.
   - Stop "SCIM 2.0" wrapping at 375 and give "sub-organizations" inner padding.
