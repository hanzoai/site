# /console — James-eye review

Verdict: 7/10
First complaint: "The Keys screen breaks. The key name is coming down one letter at a time in a skinny column. It looks broken, and it's the one screen that's meant to show the key."

Walked 7 Nov 2026 on the dev server at 1470×801 and 375×812, moving. Playwright with video, 130px wheel notches and 1.2 s dwell, hover and press on all 38 controls in main, every tile and palette opened, a Tab crawl, Esc, reduced motion, a phone pass screen by screen with taps, and 7 fresh loads to check errors. Proof: `docs/review/console/desktop-walk.jpg`, `docs/review/console/phone-walk.jpg`.

## What works
- The hero object is a real console that runs on its own. Within 1 s of load it moves from Projects to the palette, types "keys", then goes to Keys and on to Usage and balance (sampled every 0.5 s with no input). It has a 46px pause button. This is the best "alive" first screen in the cloud family.
- The pinned stage (`.cs-ch`, about 4,430px) is a real Apple-style scene. One window stays put while the sidebar item, panel and right-hand caption change through 6 steps (Projects, Models, Usage, Keys, Apps, Organization), with a `01 ── 06` scrub bar under it. As you scroll, one thing comes in and one goes out.
- "Find it by typing it" is a working palette, not a picture of one. Typing "bot" filters the list to Bot, ArrowDown and Enter take you to `/bot/`, and the `/` key hint turns into `esc`.
- The "console is a client" tiles press to `scale(0.97)` and open a sheet. /v1 opens the `app.py` client code with Copy, Esc closes the sheet and focus returns to the tile.
- Colour is clean: a scan of computed colours in `<main>` found 0 hues. Fonts are Zen, Zen Mono and Noto Kufi Arabic only. CLS during the walk was 0.0086.
- The agent prompt Copy copies exactly the text shown and changes to "Copied" within 100 ms.
- On phone, the pinned stage stacks into panels with each caption under its screen, as the brief asks. There is no horizontal scroll (scrollWidth 375), the h1 is 40px, and the first screen shows the ask and product.
- Reduced motion: no animations are left running after 3.5 s, and the final state shows.

## The 11 answers
1. **Sells in 5 s, moves in 1 s?** Yes. "Hanzo Console" at 52px (h1 top 232px), the lede "One admin for everything you run" and Open Console all sit in the first 300px. The console window starts at y=391 and is already changing screens in the first 1.5 s.
2. **One in, one out, or a document?** The hero, the 6-step pinned stage and the palette all pass. The page slows after that. "Get Console" opens with about 250px of white above it (y≈8020; screen 10 is mostly empty) and is a plain two-column link list. "Open the console" is a 44px heading with an empty right half. The last 2 screens read as a document.
3. **A real object in every section?** Hero: the live console. Stage: the console. Palette: the palette plus a console. Client: 4 type tiles, where the object is only a word in large type. Get: chips and rows. Paste: the agent prompt terminal. Close: nothing but a button and a GitHub row. Client and Close are the thin ones.
4. **One ask, nothing floating, scale rhythm?** On desktop there is one solid fill per viewport. Open Console is solid in the hero, and the header turns into a black "Open Console" once you scroll past. Nothing floats over content on desktop. Scale breaks in the client block, where 4 tiles each carry a heading of about 64px ("/v1", "OIDC", "Per-org", "OSS") at once in a 2×2 grid of 325×320 tiles. That is everything big at once, and each tile's large grey field is mostly empty.
5. **Every hover and press answers, no jumps?** Most do: sidebar rows fill `#262626`, the module buttons lighten, tiles shift fill and press to 0.97, and the hanzoai chip border darkens. Three controls are dead on hover, with no change in fill, colour, transform, border or shadow: hero "Documentation →" (x=1033, y=232), "Deploy to Cloud →" and "Self-host →" (y≈8182/8215). There is no width jump anywhere.
6. **Overlap, font swap, layout shift?** **Yes, an overlap and break inside the Keys panel.** At step 04 of the pinned stage, the key row's `.x-mono` name ("sk-...") collapses to 23×126px once the row changes to "revoked" with the wider "Issue again" button. "sk-..." and "scoped to acme" then stack one character per line ("s/k/-/./././", "sc/op/ed/to/ac/m/e"). It happens at every scroll position from 60% to 65% of the stage. The hero's Keys screen also runs this revoke loop at 1170px wide. CLS was 0.0086 and no font swap was seen.
7. **Which reference, and is its motion there?** apple.com product page: the product as hero and a pinned scene where one panel changes. That motion is really there. motion.dev's palette, typing and filter also feel right. There is no Morph and no pointer-aware moment; the page has no pointer motion at all (no tilt, magnet or light). By kind it has idle (hero demo), time (typing), state (sheets, palette) and scroll (stage). Pointer is missing.
8. **Different from neighbours?** Mostly. /bot has a light hero with a crew demo. /crawl uses the same split composition as /console (h1 low left; lede, ask and secondary at right; a wide browser frame below), but /console is a black room with a live admin UI, so they read differently. The section classes reuse the `bz-ch`/`bz-sec` scaffold. Check that the 6-step pinned stage is not also another cloud page's signature.
9. **18 parts covered?** Most of them visibly: one h1, `data-cta` on both Open Console asks (`console.hero`, `console.close`), a pause control, the palette, the copy, the sheets, a closing band. Gaps seen: the sheets are `role="dialog"`, not a native `<dialog>` (`dialog[open]` is null while one is open, though Esc works); the agent-prompt Copy is 75×30px (under 44px); the hero window's "Console — Hanzo" link is 121×13px; and `<meta name="robots">` is `index, follow`, where MASTER part 14 lists console as `noindex,follow`.
10. **Banned tells?** No hue, gradient text or pill above a headline (checked the 40px above every h1, h2 and h3: nothing). The "01 PROJECTS" Zen Mono labels sit below their h3s, which is allowed. The 4-up big-type tile grid is close to "everything big at once" (see 4). Lucide-style icons are only in the mock UI.
11. **Copy untouched?** I did not run parity. Flag for the owner: the "Per-org" sheet body ("A usage record per call and one balance per organization, so spend is attributed…") repeats the Usage chapter text word for word. Confirm that is Hanzo's line for Per-org and not filler.

## Fix list (to reach 8+), most important first
1. **Keys row collapse** (`.cs-ch` step 04, and the hero Keys screen): give the key name and its scope line `min-width: 0; flex: 1` and `white-space: nowrap; text-overflow: ellipsis`, or let the status chip and "Issue again" wrap below on narrow panels. The text must never stack one character per line.
2. **Client tiles: everything big at once.** The 4 tiles each have a 64px heading over an empty 325×320 grey field. Let one tile hold the moment (/v1, with a peek of the `app.py` code inside it) and bring the others down to medium, or cut the tile height so the words are not floating in empty grey.
3. **Dead hovers:** hero "Documentation →", "Deploy to Cloud →" and "Self-host →" change nothing on hover. Give them the house link hover (underline draw or arrow shift, not opacity).
4. **Back third reads as a document:** remove the ~250px void above "Get Console" and give the closing "Open the console" band an object in its empty right half, such as the console window settling into place or the org switcher. The page's last screen should be alive, not a heading and a button.
5. **Agent-prompt Copy** is 30px tall: make it 44px (MASTER part 8). Make the hero window's 13px-tall "Console — Hanzo" link 44px tall, or take it out of the tab order.
6. **Sheets:** move the tile sheets to native `<dialog>.showModal()` (part 8).
7. **Pointer kind is missing:** add one fine-pointer detail, such as a slight tilt or a cursor-aware sheen on the hero console window, so the motion is not idle, time and scroll alone.
8. **Robots:** `/console` serves `index, follow`, while MASTER part 14 says `noindex,follow` (this may belong to the shared metadata owner).

## Shared chrome
- **Intermittent console error with a red "1 Issue" badge:** on the first walk, every context (desktop, reduced motion) showed the Next dev "1 Issue" badge, with "A tree hydrated but some attributes of the server rendered HTML didn't match the client properties". Five later fresh loads (desktop, phone, with and without consent) showed 0 errors, so it may have come from a concurrent HMR rebuild. Re-check it before you call the page clean.
- Header ask label changes from "Try Hanzo" (outlined, over the black hero) to a solid "Open Console" after scrolling. Part 4 says the hero ask's label should be the one in the header from the start.
- Phone: the header "Try Hanzo" is a filled white pill in the same first viewport as the solid white "Open Console", so there are two solid fills with different labels. Further down, the header's black "Try Hanzo" sits beside the page's black "Open Console" on screen 12.
- Header first paint: for about 300 ms the header renders light grey (`d-early-0`) before turning black over the black hero.
- Cookie card: setting `hz-consent` to `"reject"` did not suppress it (an object value did). On phone it covers the hero console from y≈595 to 795 until you dismiss it.
- Palette "Bot" result row: a long underline bar runs the width of the row under the title while it is selected (the module list underline style). Check that this is intended.
