## What works
- **A real idea, its own.** The page is set as a research paper: Abstract, Figure 1 to Figure 8, Table 1, lettered legends. Nothing else on the site looks like it, and it fits a research repo.
- **The hero sells and moves inside 1 second.** Figure 1 (input image, embedding scatter, three loss bars) runs itself through 5 captioned steps ("image at rest", "predict how its embedding moves", ...) with a step bar and a 44px pause button. first-0ms and first-1000ms differ: the image has shifted, the embedding dot has travelled, the Huber bar has jumped to about 80%.
- **Figure 8 is a genuine moment.** Dragging the image moves the grid, the predicted point leaves z with a dashed trail, the status reads "shift the image · predict how its embedding moves"; on release the image slides back, the line returns, and the status lands on "lands where it started". It has a keyboard path (`role="img"`, tabindex 0, "arrow keys shift it too").
- **A sticky chapter bar** ("What is in the repository / I-JEPA / 01 / 06") with a progress hairline under the header, so the long middle tells you where you are.
- **Line drawings draw themselves** (pathLength, then marks fill and letters land), visible between every a/b pair on desktop.
- **Monochrome throughout.** Ink, greys, one black room (Figure 8), one quiet room (section 2). No hue.
- **Clean engineering.** CLS 0.0011 desktop and 0 phone, 0 horizontal overflow, no page console errors (the one error is the known FooterHTML hydration mismatch), parity `37/37 text, 3/3 links`, reduced motion leaves `getAnimations()` empty and freezes Figure 1 on a step, JS off is fully legible.
- **Table 1 opens as a right sheet** (bottom sheet on phone), focus moves into it, Esc closes it and returns focus to "See Table 1".

## The 11 answers
1. **Sells in 5 s, moves in 1 s?** Yes on desktop. The h1 (52px/497, top 164px), the "Jin · hanzoai/jin" line, the solid "Read the docs" and Figure 1 all fit 801px, and the demo is moving by 1000 ms. I could not catch a line-mask cut-in on the h1: it is already at rest at 0, 150 and 300 ms. On phone the h1 (40px, 3 lines), ask and Figure 1 fit, but the Abstract (the lede) falls below the figure, off the first screen.
2. **One in, one out, or a document?** Half and half. Hero to section 2 has rhythm (64px "1", then a 112px "2" with a 44px heading in a quiet room). Sections 3 to 6 then repeat one template (numeral, short heading, paragraph, line drawing, lettered legend), and the type shrinks as you go: numerals 64, 44, 44, 44px; headings 26, 21, 21, 21px. Desktop section heights are 508, 477, 477px, three in a row within 15% of each other (MASTER part 3 forbids that). On phone the whole page is one stacked column about 10,400px long, the same five-part block six times. The scroll motion is a one-shot draw-in per figure: no pinned stage, no scrub, nothing scroll-tied after a drawing has finished.
3. **A real object in every section?** Yes: 8 figures, one per section plus the hero and Figure 8. They are diagrams, not Hanzo media (the page has the one sphere render), so there is no 4-up block of real visuals. Section 6's figure (a tree and a chip) is the weakest; section 1 leaves about 250px of dead white under its paragraph at 1470.
4. **One ask, nothing floating? Scale rhythm?** Desktop: right. The header ask is a quiet "Try Hanzo" while the hero ask is in view, then turns into a solid "Read the docs" (measured: `rgba(0,0,0,0)` at scroll 0, `rgb(10,10,10)` from 900px on). Phone: wrong. The header keeps a **solid "Try Hanzo"** at every depth while the hero shows a solid "Read the docs", so there are two solid fills with two different labels on the first screen (`r2-phone-two-solids.jpg`), and the phone header never carries the page's ask. Scale: one big thing per screen holds at the top; the middle dwindles instead of breathing (see 2). Nothing floats over content except shared chrome (cookie card, dev badge).
5. **Hover and press answer, no jumps?** Partly. Primary: background ink to `#262626` on hover, `scale(0.98)` on press, no layout jump. "View on GitHub" gains an underline. Close rows fill `#f5f5f5`. That is all colour; no hover tells you anything (no number, no preview), and the 8 figures have no hover at all. On phone the walker's 9 hovers changed nothing, which is correct for touch.
6. **Overlap, font swap, layout shift?** None from the page. CLS 0.0011/0, overflow 0. The chapter counter lags one section: with Figure 7 (section 6) on screen the bar still reads "05 / 06 Ways to see what it learned". The Figure 8 status readout ("drag the image") is a light `#f5f5f5` rounded slab on the black room that looks like a text field you should type into, and it is the brightest thing in that room.
7. **Which reference, and is its motion there?** anthropic.com model launch (a technical story told in figures) and osply.co's editorial captions. The anthropic "benchmarks as hero figures" beat is not here (the page has no numbers to show), and there is no apple-style pinned scene where one panel changes while you scroll, which this diagram sequence is crying out for.
8. **Different from neighbours?** Yes. Paper-style figures, a self-running diagram hero and a drag-to-saccade plate are not used elsewhere in the apps family I have seen. Inside the page, though, Figure 1 and Figure 8 are the same three panels (input, embedding, losses) shown twice.
9. **All 18 parts?** Mostly evidenced: one h1, `data-cta="jin.hero"` and `data-thumb` on the primary, external links `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, pause control on the loop, reduced motion and JS off handled, 44px targets (View on GitHub is 44px tall on phone), parity clean. Gaps: the sticky ask on phone (part 4), the 3-equal-heights run (part 3), the Table 1 sheet is a `div role="dialog"`, not the native `<dialog>.showModal()` that part 8 asks for, and the Figure 1 loop repeats forever where part 4.1 asks a demo to run once and settle.
10. **Banned tells?** None. No hue, no gradient text, no kicker above a headline ("ABSTRACT" sits over the lede, "FIGURE 8" below its h2, "Jin" below the h1), no fading buttons, no glow.
11. **Copy untouched?** Yes. `parity.py /jin`: OK, text 37/37, links 3/3. The Saccade JEPA paragraph repeats the hero abstract word for word; that is Hanzo's copy and stays.

## Fixes (in order)
1. **Turn sections 1 to 6 into one pinned stage on desktop** (`components/pages/apps/Jin.jsx`, the `SECS` map and `.jn-sec` in `Jin.css`). Pin a single figure plate on the right while the six numbered texts scroll on the left; each chapter swaps the plate (draw out, draw in) and the chapter bar already tracks it. That turns the six repeats into one scrubbed scene, kills the 508/477/477 run, and gives the page the scroll-tied beat it lacks. Under 900px, keep the stack but alternate the figure above and below the text and cut the per-section legend to one line.
2. **Stop the dwindle.** Give sections 3 to 6 their own scale moment rather than shrinking: one of them (Three losses at once is the obvious one) should grow its figure to full container width with the numeral at 112px, the way section 2 does, and the others stay medium. Never three 44px numerals and 21px headings in a row.
3. **Make Figure 8 different from Figure 1** or move the drag into the hero. Either let the hero figure accept the drag after its first pass (the loop then settles, satisfying part 4.1), or give Figure 8 a different read-out (for example, the loss bars plotted as a trace over your drags) so the bottom is not a repeat.
4. **Fix the phone sticky ask** so the header carries "Read the docs" (quiet while the hero ask is in view, solid after), matching desktop. The page sets `data-ask-name="Jin"`; check why the phone header ignores it, or file `docs/requests/jin.md` if the phone path in the shared Ask is at fault.
5. **Let hovers say something.** Hovering a figure's lettered legend chip should light the matching part of the drawing (and the other way round); hovering a close row could slide its arrow 4px. Today every hover is a fill.
6. **Restyle the Figure 8 status line** as a mono caption on the black (white 78% text, a 1px white-10% rule above) instead of a light rounded slab that reads as an input.
7. **Fix the chapter counter lag** so it switches when a section's top crosses the bar, not one section late.
8. **Tighten section 1's empty left column** at 1470 (about 250px of dead white under the paragraph); align the figure's bottom with the text instead.
9. **Use native `<dialog>.showModal()`** for the Table 1 sheet (or the shared `Sheet` once it is), keeping the current focus return.
10. **Settle the hero loop**: run the five steps once (about 8 s), then rest on "lands where it started" with a replay on the pause button, per part 4.1. Check the h1 line-mask cut-in actually plays on first paint; I could not see it.
