Verdict: 6/10
First complaint: "The first screen is lovely, and then it's a paper. Six chapters in the same layout, a still drawing each, nothing moving, white gaps between them. And the hero's bottom half twitches every second and a half."

Walked 7 Nov 2026 on the dev server at 1470x801 and 375x812 (recorder walk at both sizes with every scroll stop and its idle frame; 9 hovers; press on the primary; a layout-shift observer over load and a slow wheel scroll; headline frames every 150 ms; Table 1 sheet opened and closed with Esc; the Figure 8 drag mid-drag and after release; a 30-stop Tab crawl; reduced motion; JS off; `parity.py` and `taste.py`).

Frames: [hero, desktop](jin/d-hero.jpg) · [chapter 3, three equal figures and the white band](jin/d-losses.jpg) · [Figure 8 mid-drag](jin/d-drag.jpg)

## What works
- **The hero is a real idea, and it is this page's own.** Figure 1 (input frame, embedding scatter, three loss bars, a five-step progress rule and a caption chip) runs by itself and is visibly moving by 1000 ms: the dashed crop shifts, the predicted point travels, the return path draws and the bars swap emphasis. It explains the abstract without a word. The h1 is 52px/497 at desktop and 40px over 3 lines on phone. h1, primary and 80px+ of the figure fit inside 660px at 375.
- **Figure 8 ("Make the saccade yourself") is the best pointer moment I have seen on a research page.** Drag the image, the predicted embedding follows with a dashed trace, release and it shifts back along a solid return line, and the chip under the copy narrates the step. It is keyboard-focusable (a 225x225 span stop on the Tab crawl).
- **Kinds of motion are mixed:** idle (Figure 1), arrival (section headlines cut in, seen in the 150 ms frames), pointer (the drag), state (the Table 1 sheet rises from the right; focus moves into the `role="dialog"` and goes back to the "See Table 1" button on Esc). Scroll-linked motion is only the rail's progress line, well under half.
- **Restraint and palette:** monochrome, one dark plate, hairline rules, Zen Mono labels in ink or grey. `taste.py` passes on `Jin.jsx`/`Jin.css`. `parity.py /jin` gives text 37/37 and links 3/3.
- **States:** reduced motion leaves a meaningful still of step 3 with 0 running animations after 3.2 s. With JS off all 9 headings render at opacity 1. No horizontal overflow at either size, 0 failed requests.
- **Press and row feedback:** the primary goes to scale .98 on press. The "Read the code" rows fill grey on hover with no shift.

## The 11 answers
1. **Sells in 5 s, moves within 1 s?** Yes on both sizes. Figure 1 has changed by the 1000 ms frame (desktop and phone), and the 3000 ms frame shows the return trace. **But the hero is not stable.** The caption chip changes from "image at rest" to "predict how its embedding moves", wraps the caption `<p>` from 24px to 48px, and that pushes `.jn-abs` (the Abstract) 4px down and back on every cycle. The observer logged layout shifts of 0.0159 at 2.5 s, 3.9 s and 4.8 s, and on and on. The recorder's desktop CLS is **0.207**, against a budget of 0.02.
2. **One in, one out, or a document?** A document. Chapters 1 to 6 (I-JEPA, Saccade JEPA, Three losses, Masked autoencoding, Ways to see, The papers) all use one template: a big thin numeral, a 26px heading, a paragraph, one static line drawing and an `a/b/c` legend, alternating left and right. Six in a row at one weight, with no loud or quiet change until the dark plate. Every desktop stop between 681 and 4767 has 0 running animations and an idle diff of 0 to 213 bytes. The recorder counts 4 of 13 desktop screens and 9 of 16 phone screens as still. Each stop also carries a 150 to 250px empty white band (screens 01, 02, 03, 04).

## Fixes (in order)
1. **Stop the hero from moving the page** (`components/pages/apps/Jin.css`). Give the Figure 1 caption a fixed height for two lines: `.jn-cap p { min-height: 3em }` at 16px/1.5, or put the step chip on its own line with `white-space: nowrap` and a reserved width. Also take `.jn-abs` off `align-self: end` in a row the figure spans: pin it with `grid-row: 2; align-self: start`, or give `.jn-hero-fig` a fixed block size. Do the same for Figure 8: `.jn-try-head { align-self: start }` and a fixed-height status chip. Target: 0 layout shifts after load over 10 s idle.
2. **Turn chapters 1 to 6 from a document into a scene.** The page already has the idea: Figure 1's five steps. On desktop, pin a stage and let the chapters change one panel at a time beside it (one thing in, one out), or at least make each chapter's drawing arrive authored rather than sitting still. The diagram strokes draw in at 800 to 1,400 ms; the I-JEPA mask cells fill; the cycle dot travels the circle once; the UMAP clusters separate. All of it runs once at 60% visible, with the final state in the HTML and under reduced motion. Under 900px keep them stacked, each with its own one-shot draw.
3. **Break the six-in-a-row rhythm.** Make one chapter loud and the rest quiet. "Saccade JEPA" ("Ours") is the obvious one: bigger drawing, full width, the moment. Shrink chapters 4 to 6 to a tighter medium scale. Cut the 150 to 250px dead white bands between chapters (`.bz-sec` padding plus `.jn-sec-in` row-gap) so the next idea peeks within the screen.
4. **Make Figure 8 a different object from Figure 1.** Keep the drag, but do not repeat the hero's exact panel. For example, let the visitor drag on the hero itself (pause the loop on pointerdown), and use the dark plate for the comparison the page sets up in chapter 4 (decoder versus no decoder), or show only the embedding space at full size.
5. **Chapter 3's three equal figures:** make Huber, cycle-consistency and VICReg one composite (one axis with three marks), or give them unequal weight. Equal thirds are banned.
6. **Differ from /ledger at the first screen.** Change the composition, not the object: for example, the h1 above a full-width Figure 1, with the Abstract set as a side note to the figure. Then the two pages stop sharing the "copy left, panel with pause right, abstract under a rule" layout.
7. **Rail on phone:** make `.jn-run` opaque (`#fff`), or use the header's glass, so headings do not ghost behind it.
8. **Close:** add the one proof line the page has (licence Apache-2.0, `hanzoai/jin`) as a quiet strip above "Read the code", so the page ends on proof, then the Band, then the footer.
9. Shared chrome, request only: the primary hover `rgb(44,44,46)` is off the deck ramp; on phone the header shows a second solid fill ("Try Hanzo") beside the hero ask; the header glass leaves grey smudges at desktop scroll stops.
