Make the hero ask the solid fill (48px), or confirm with the chrome owner that the header's solid stays solid. Right now nothing on screen one is filled.
- **Put an object in the dead sections.** "Powered by Community" needs a real thing: the GitHub repo card, contributors or the license chip as a live object, not four link rows. The Security block needs its one quiet object (a data-residency or retention control drawn as UI, or the "Report on request" audit row as a document card). "From a visit to a number" can lead straight into the chapter stage instead of floating alone.
- **Close the white gaps.** Cut the roughly 300px of empty space under the hero (chip alone at y≈773), before the "From a visit" heading and before "If you sell something", so the next section peeks about 56px and objects overlap as one goes and the next comes (MASTER part 3).

Lift the card picture (translateY of 2 to 4px or a 2.5° tilt on fine pointers), start its little scene (the Journeys path drawing, the funnel bars filling), and have the link asks ("Request Demo", "Read how reports work", "Read the docs") move their arrow or underline visibly.
- **Fix the hydration error and the clipped label.** Find the hydration mismatch that showed "1 Issue" in the walk (time- or random-based markup in the realtime list or the chart, most likely). Also widen or wrap the Attribution card's third "referrer" block so the word is not cut at the card edge (desktop, about x 1210 to 1280, y≈290).
