# /press: James-eye review (independent critic, 7 Nov)

Verdict: 7/10
First complaint: "On the phone, the press contact jumps. The email types itself in, then everything under it leaps up a thumb's width. That's not premium, that's the page breaking in front of me."

How I walked it: Playwright Chromium at 1470x801 (desktop hover and pointer flags) and 375x812 (touch). I took screenshots 0.2, 1, 2 and 3 s after load and two frames 1 s apart on every half screen, scrolled desktop with the wheel, logged layout shifts with their sources, tested hover and press on every control type, scanned computed colours for hue, and dismissed the cookie bar with Reject all. Link clicks were blocked during the press tests. Proof: `img/press-phone-jump.jpg` (phone contact while typing, left, and after, right) and `img/press-kit-spread.jpg` (the pinned kit spread at rest). `python3 scripts/parity.py /press`: OK, text 83/83, links 20/20.

## The 11 answers

1. **Sells in 5 s; moves within 1 s?** Yes. "Press Resources." sits left with the lede, one solid "Contact Press Team" and an outlined "Download Press Kit". On the right is a tilted stack of the three kit plates, and a sheen keeps crossing the dark plate while the page sits idle. The h1 line-mask starts at about 0.7 s and settles by 1.5 s on a warm dev load. The lede rises by 2 s. On phone the h1 (44px, 2 lines), lede, primary and the dark plate all fit inside the first screen.
2. **One in, one out, or a document?** The top half is genuinely staged. The pinned stage fans the stack out into the kit spread (stage 1842px; the titles fade in at the end) and the headline leaves as the spread arrives. Then the boilerplate fills word by word, the news index runs with a still dark card that follows the row at mid-screen, and the black room types the email. From Brand Assets down it reads more like a stack of lists: partner rows, a nine-row social list and two portrait plates. Each has a one-time beat (hairlines, the index walking 01 to 09, plates opening), so it is not dead, but the rhythm flattens.
3. **A real visual object in every section?** Mostly. Kit plates, the word-fill boilerplate with figures, the news card, the 84px email, the Brand Guidelines mark tile, partner logo rows and portrait plates. In this sandbox the partner logos and portraits 404, and their fallbacks hold up as fallbacks: letter discs, and Sigil plates with initials (AW, ZK). Social is a hairline list with no object.
4. **One ask in the sticky header; scale rhythm?** Desktop works. The header shows a quiet "Try Hanzo" while the hero ask is on screen, then a solid "Contact Press Team" once it leaves. There is nothing floating on desktop. At the close, though, the header's solid "Contact Press Team" and the page's solid "Request Executive Bios" share the viewport: 2 solid fills. On phone the header keeps a solid "Try Hanzo" beside the hero's solid "Contact Press Team", and the ask bar later shows "Contact Press Team" under a header that still says "Try Hanzo" (shared chrome, see below). Scale: h1 64px, section heads 32px, h3 26px and the email as the one big thing in its room is good rhythm. The About facts are four 44px figures in an equal 2x2, which is the one place where everything is big at once.
5. **Every hover and press answers, no jumps?** Yes on desktop. Primary: fill ink to #262626 and press scale .97. Outline asks: quiet fill plus a darker border. Kit plates: the plate scales 1.04 and the format pills invert. News rows: underline, the arrow slides 6px and the side card swaps to that story (a real preview). Guidelines tile, partner download chips (invert to ink), social rows (name slides 8px) and the copy icon (inverts) all answer. Every press is a scale of .96 to .98. None of the hovers move layout.
6. **Overlap, font swap or layout shift?** Desktop CLS is 0.002, with no overlap and no horizontal scroll. **Phone fails.** While the email types, the caret plus the invisible copy button (opacity 0, but still in the flex row) wrap the row to 104px. When typing ends it collapses to 48px, and Media Inquiries, the phone line, the address and the next section all jump up 56px (CLS 0.032, sources `xpe-contact-a`, `xpe-tel`, `xpe-brand`). There is no font swap; the h1 box holds at top 208.
7. **Which reference, and is its motion there?** Mostly osply or hamol editorial. You get the asymmetric 8/5 heads, an editorial news index with a pointer-aware preview card and the masked heads. The Apple pinned stage is honestly there in the kit spread. The chatgpt-enterprise calm proof shows in the facts. Story-type motion is present: the word reveal and the figures settling once.
8. **Different from its neighbours?** Yes. The stack dealing out into the kit spread is its own move. The hero medium is the product's own brand plates, not a photo or a film.
9. **18 parts covered?** As seen on the page: one h1, one `<main>`, `data-cta` and `data-thumb` on the primary, copy buttons with `role=status`, JS-off styles that show everything, the reduced-motion end state coded in, and fallbacks for missing media. Gaps on the page: the h1 is followed by h3 kit titles with no h2 (a skipped level). On phone the news card repeats the title and summary of a story already in the list above it (aria-hidden, but you see it twice).
10. **Banned tells?** No hue (0 chromatic computed colours in `main`), no gradient text, no pill above any headline ("Press & Media" and "Leadership" sit below their heads as captions), no fade-on-hover, no cursor glow. The only tell is the phone layout shift in answer 6.
11. **Copy untouched?** Parity is OK (83/83 text, 20/20 links). The 10-54% token is only kept from breaking, not reworded.

**Shared chrome, noted and not counted against the page:** the header ask on phone stays "Try Hanzo" (solid) and never picks up the page's `data-thumb`, so phone shows two solid fills on the first screen and two different asks once the bar rises. The header does not go quiet when a second `data-thumb` ask (Bios) is on screen. In two of my first walks a dev "1 Issue" badge showed a hydration-attribute mismatch whose trace starts above the page (`<Head>` / root boundaries). It did not reproduce in four later fresh loads, so I cannot attribute it to /press. The partner and portrait 404s are a sandbox mirror gap.

## Fixes to reach 8

1. **Stop the phone contact jump.** Reserve the copy button's space from the start (`visibility:hidden` or keep it in the row at a fixed width) and give `.xpe-mail-row` `flex-wrap: nowrap` with the caret overlaid absolutely, not inline, so the row is 48px from the first frame. Then check that CLS while scrolling through the black room at 375 reads 0.
2. **One solid fill at the close.** With the header showing a solid "Contact Press Team", make "Request Executive Bios" the only solid in that viewport by quieting the header, the same way the hero does. If that needs a header change, request it, and until then render Bios as the outline and "View Team" as a text link so only one ink pill is on screen.
3. **Give the About facts a scale rhythm.** Keep one figure as the moment (for example "2016" settling large) and set the other three at the medium step, so the 2x2 of equal 44px blocks is no longer big all at once.
4. **Fix the heading skip:** give the kit spread an h2 using text already on the page ("Press kit"/the zip line) or use `as` to make the plate titles follow an h2. On phone, show only the summary and date in the walking news card (not the repeated headline) so each story reads once.
5. **Lift the lower half out of list mode.** Give Connect With Us one object (for example the index walking with the highlighted channel's handle set large beside it, from existing handles) so Brand Assets, Social and Team aren't three quiet lists in a row.
