## Fixes to reach 8

1. **Clear the phone overlap.** Under 899px, take the format pills out of the plate's corner: set them in flow under the plate, or reserve top padding equal to the pill row so the mark centres below them. At 375 no pill may touch the mark on any of the three plates. Also clip or hide the rear plates' pills in the desktop hero stack so no fragment shows past the front plate.
2. **Give the middle something alive at rest.** At least one ambient, non-scroll mover in About → Brand Assets, using a single continuous mover per viewport and existing content only. For example, the news card could keep walking the six stories slowly on desktop (pausing on pointer and focus, with a pause control) instead of holding 01. Or the black room could keep a slow sheen or Sigil drift behind the email. Right now four screens in a row sit dead after their one-time beat.
3. **Remove the dead white bands.** The roughly 350px empty gaps before Recent News and before Connect With Us read as an unfinished document. Tighten those section paddings, or let the next section's object peek 56px into them. Do the same under the desktop hero, so the next thing peeks (part 3).
4. **Let the boilerplate finish.** End the word fill (`offset` end) early enough that the whole paragraph, "everyone." included, is fully ink once the section is at rest in the viewport.
5. **Break up the list rhythm.** News, partners and channels are three hairline lists in a row in the lower half. Without adding copy, give one of them a different form (for example, partners as one still logo row, as the page's own header comment promises) so the lower half stops reading as three lists one after another.
