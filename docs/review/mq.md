## What works
- **The hero object is the product doing the thing.** A live job conveyor (waiting, two workers with progress bars, completed/failed, a limiter strip of 6 cells, a QueueEvents ticker) is moving at 1000 ms with no input. Tickets glide lane to lane by layoutId, a priority-1 job cuts to the top of waiting, and the limiter cells fill and reset. It is a real, readable idea of what MQ does, built from the page's own words.
- **The hover tells you something.** Hovering a ticket prints `export priority 2 · attempt 1 / 3 · waiting` in the conveyor's own toolbar. That is the kind of hover detail JAMES.md asks for.
- **The pinned chapters are a stage where one panel changes.** Six chapters with `01 / 06` captions and a progress rail, over one job inspector whose tab underline glides (layoutId) and whose panels run by themselves: the schedule survives "process · restarting", the limiter window fills 8 and spills into "waits for the next", welcome (#5, priority 1) jumps to the top, attempt dots fill along a backoff timeline while the lock hands from worker a to worker b, events stream in, and the failed set has a Retry that flips to "back in waiting / Undo". Clicking a tab scrolls to its chapter (clicked "retries" and landed on retries).
- **The motion mix is right.** Idle (conveyor, inspector panels, contour drift on the close card). Pointer (ticket peek, tab hover, magnetic close ask). State (gliding tab underline, Retry/Undo). Arrival (the timed wipe into the black room, the 10K cut-in). Scroll (the stage settling, the pin). Scroll is clearly under half.
- **Scale has rhythm.** h1 52px/497, chapter heads medium, mono 12px captions, one 64px/220 figure (10K), and "Get MQ" grows for its moment at the close. Never everything big at once.
- **One ask.** One solid fill per desktop viewport (measured: only "GitHub"). The header ask swaps to a solid "GitHub" once the hero ask leaves (desktop). Hover goes #0a0a0a to #262626, press is `scale(0.97)`; no opacity fade, no jump.
- **Calm and resilient.** CLS 0.0010 desktop, 0.0008 phone; 0 overflow; 0 failed requests. Reduced motion: 0 running animations after 3.5 s, h1 opacity 1, the conveyor holds a full warm frame. JS off: 0 of 40 text nodes hidden, and the full page reads top to bottom.
- **The black room enters as a rounded card inside the gutters and opens to full width** (phone screen 09 a/b), as MASTER section 3 asks.

## Fixes (in order)
1. **Kill the blank panel and the double headline at chapter changes.** In `components/pages/cloud/Mq.jsx` (Inspector, line ~306), `AnimatePresence mode="wait"` empties the panel for the whole 150 ms exit before the 400 ms enter. Use `mode="popLayout"` (or a sync crossfade with the outgoing panel absolutely positioned) so the next panel rides in as the old one leaves, and keep the body's min-height. The ghosted heading/lede overlap comes from the shared `components/v2/PinnedChapters.jsx` text swap. File `docs/requests/mq.md` asking for the outgoing copy to clip out (translateY −100% under an overflow mask) before the incoming line-masks in, not an opacity crossfade of two stacked blocks.
2. **Make the pin earn its length.** The intro holds for about 700px of desktop scroll with nothing new. Lower `per` or give the intro a smaller share in `<PinnedChapters … per={80}>` (Mq.jsx line ~362), so the first scroll lands on Scheduling within about one screen.
3. **Break the cloud-family running order.** MQ should not scroll in the same shape as PubSub, S3 and Node. Cheapest MQ-specific move: let the hero conveyor be the thing that pins. Scroll drives the same conveyor through the six behaviours (the chapters' text docks beside it), so the first object becomes the stage instead of being replaced by a second box. Or drop the separate walk section and put the 10K figure in the black room beside worker.ts. Either way, the page's signature (the conveyor) should own more than the first screen.
4. **Show the h1 arriving and seat it lower.** Confirm `LineMask` actually runs on first load (no cut-in was visible at 0 to 150 ms). If it is skipped for LCP, start the mask from the SSR state with a CSS animation so the line still rises in 700 ms. Move `.mq-top` down so the h1 baseline sits near 38% (it is at 30.6%), and let the stage keep its 56px peek.
5. **Fill the empty bottoms of the inspector panels.** On desktop the priority, events and failed-set panels leave 100 to 200px empty inside the outlined box. On phone the priority panel is more than half empty (p-pinned-limiter, screen 05). Size `.mq-ins-body` to its content per breakpoint in `Mq.css`, or vertically centre `.mq-p`. On phone also give "ONE WINDOW" its top padding (it sits flush under the tab bar).
6. **Give the chapter steps a hover answer.** The six `lx-pin-step` articles are focusable and clickable but change nothing on hover. Add an ink rail fill or the step's number darkening (page-local CSS in `Mq.css` on `.mq .lx-pin-step:hover`).
7. **The empty "Cron" cell.** The live copy has no label under Cron. Keep the words, but stop the empty `<dt>` from reading as a missing line: let that column centre its value, or collapse the label row for that cell (`.mq-spec .lx-spec-l:empty { display:none }`).
