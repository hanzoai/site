## Fixes to reach 8

1. **Recompose the desktop hero.** Lift the h1 so its baseline sits near 38% of 100svh, not 68%. Close the dead gutter by pulling the gauge in toward the copy, or by setting the gauge and reasons as one tighter unit on the 8/5 split. Put "ACTION" directly under the verdict word, with no 60px gap. Let the next section peek about 56px at 1470x801. Keep the dial and its behaviour exactly as they are.
2. **Fix the phone overlap.** Move the chapter pause button so it never sits on the case card's label row: put it inside the card's top row, or below the card, at 44px. Check the same spot on the Track and Lanes cards. Move the Abuse card's "+" clear of its tallest bar at 375.
3. **Chapter cards: raise or outline, not both.** Drop either the outline or the floating shadow on the `.rk-case` / `.rk-track` / `.rk-lanes` cards. A flat `#f5f5f5` tile would match the rule and declare cards further down.
4. **Thin the idle loops.** Keep the dial, the chapter visuals and the event stream as the self-runners. Let the declare stack, the tier ladder and the gaps-list ticker run once when they come into view and then rest. That gives at most one continuous mover per viewport and a calmer, more premium lower half.
5. Right-edge alignment: the hero's reasons column and pause run to x≈1453, past the header's 1446 edge. Align them to the container.
6. (Shared chrome, request, not this page's fault) The header ask should carry the page's "Talk to us" from load, at both widths. On phone it should not stay a solid "Try Hanzo" beside the hero's solid "Talk to us". The open sheet's scrim also leaves the header bright and solid above it.
