## Fixes (ordered, most important first)
- **Stop the journey clipping its own panes.** The mask at about x 555 (the left edge of the pane track, 1470x801) cuts headings and paths mid-word during the scrub ("apture", "d}/capture"). At rest, pane 05 has its left border and radius cut off. Either fade the outgoing pane out (opacity plus a 16px slide) before it reaches the mask, or move the mask edge to the column gutter so it falls in the gap between panes. The last pane must settle fully inside the track, with all four corners visible.
- **Don't tell the five stops twice back to back.** The hero rail already walks Product and variant → Cart → Authorize → Capture → Refund under "An order, start to end". The pinned journey then repeats the same heading and the same five stops. Make the journey read as the zoom-in of the rail rather than a second telling. For example, carry the rail's active stop into the journey's first pane (same card position, then grow), and drop the duplicate small "01" rail state once the journey pins. Keep the copy, change the presentation.
- **Give the tail one thing per screen and an object.** At 1470x801 the ways rows (y 140–490), "Get Commerce" (y 320–480) and the close heading plus solid ask (y 575–760) share one screen. Separate the ways section from the close by a full section pad (about 116–188px) so the close stands alone. Give the integrations rows something to look at: for example, the selected processor from "Who takes the money" stays lit as the rows pass, or a hover on a name shows its group count.
- **One solid fill at the close.** The header's solid "Read the docs" and the close's solid "Read the docs" (1146,710 at 165x48) are both on screen. Hand the fill back to the header outline while the close ask is in view, as already happens in the hero.
- **Quiet the zeros in the finder.** With "refund", three of five columns show a 32px "0" plus an em dash. MASTER says a zero never gets hero size. Drop the zero counts to the 13–14px mono caption size, or dim the whole empty column to 30%. On the phone the stacked zeros take a whole screen (see phone-walk.jpg), so collapse empty groups to one line there.
- **Smaller:** Put the desktop eyebrow under the lede's line or align it to the h1's baseline instead of above the h1's level. Make the order card larger in the hero (it is 340x172 on a 1470 band), or let the band's height grow with it, so the product reads as the hero. Fix the coverage card's "panels in place" wording, since it is a modal, and its "ends in proof strip" claim.
