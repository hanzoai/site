7 Nov, round 1 of this critic. Walked it moving at 1470x801 and 375x812 (phone: isMobile, hasTouch) with Playwright on :4182. At each size I took frames at 0, 1 and 3 s with no input. Then I scrolled one screen at a time with a 1.5 s dwell (wheel on desktop, and a second pass stepping exactly one viewport, because the smooth wheel skipped sections). On desktop I also hovered and pressed, clicked a tab and dragged the divider. I measured CLS with its sources, horizontal overflow and console output, and probed the phone layout in the DOM.

Verdict: 6/10

First complaint: "The top is lovely: the music app is actually playing. Then on my phone 'One brief' is chopped off the left edge, and the whole capabilities section looks greyed out, like it's disabled. And the bottom half is just a grid of boxes that each say 'Your brief'."

## The 11 answers
1. **Does it sell in 5 s and move within 1 s? Yes.** Within 1 s the workspace window clips in on the left. The Neon Horizon player's 40-band spectrum dances at 124 BPM by itself, and the h1 cuts in with "interactive apps" on an ink block. The upload chip and prompt bubble tell the story without a word added. There is a pause control. On phone the text comes first and the window follows below the fold. That is fine, but the first phone screen is all type.
2. **Does one thing come in as one goes out? Only the first half does.** After the hero comes the before/after divider. It sweeps once by itself and is chosen by the three steps, so it is a real stage. The black models room opens next with real Iro, Koma, Oto, Koe, Uta and Mori media. After that the page turns into a document. "One brief" is a pill set beside six cards titled "Your brief". "Everything a creative team ships" is a 2x3 grid of equal cards, and "Paste and ship" sits beside "Get Design" over about 200px of empty white. Then the Band.
3. **Does every section hold a real visual object? Yes, but the same ones repeat.** The waveform appears in the hero, in the divider's "after" side, in a capability card and in a brief card. The Neon Horizon poster appears twice and the deck fan twice. Each new section re-shows the hero's artifacts instead of bringing something new.
4. **One ask, nothing floating, and does scale have rhythm? The ask is right; the rhythm is flat after the hero.** "Open Design" is the hero's solid, and the header takes over the label once the hero ask leaves view on desktop. Nothing sits fixed over content. On phone the header keeps "Try Hanzo" beside the hero's "Open Design", which makes two solid fills (a shared issue, not held). Scale: the 52px h1 and the dark room's 44px heads have rhythm. From "One brief" on, everything is 26px heads over equal tiles, and nothing grows or shrinks.
5. **Does every hover and press answer? Yes, on desktop.** Tabs glide on a layoutId pill and the frame morphs to each artifact's shape (Presentation: a 16:9 slide slides in). Synth pads press to scale .94, play a note and kick the spectrum. The divider drags with a pointer and has arrow keys and `role="slider"`. Copy confirms.
6. **Do overlaps, a font swap or layout shift appear? Yes, on phone.**
   - **Clipped section.** On phone "One brief" sits 80px off the left edge. `.dz-brief-l` carries `translateX(-96px)` while it is in view (the desktop drift runs at 375 too). The heading reads "rief", the eyebrow reads "RSATION, EVERY ARTIFACT", the input is cut, and the pill set and the next cards are cropped (frames `design_m2_s08`–`s09`).
   - **Stuck fade.** On phone the six capability sheets stay at `opacity: .35`. The `.dz-e-roll` section never leaves `data-enter='pre'` (measured on "Slide decks" with the sheet centred, 3 s after arrival). The whole section reads as disabled.
   - **Layout shift.** Phone CLS is 0.063–0.119 (sources `dz-frame k-deck`, `dz-spec`, `dz-modes`). The hero frame changes height as the tabs cycle by themselves and pushes everything below it.
   - **Desktop is clean:** CLS 0.0004, no font swap, no horizontal overflow at either size.
7. **Which reference does it feel like? Apple, in the hero.** It has the product as the hero, a live demo and one idea per screen down to the dark room. The black room is the synchronized-style "card opens into its page" set-up. After that, no reference's motion is present: the grids are plain osply-less tiles.
8. **Is it different from its neighbours? Yes.** No other page I know has a playing music app with synth pads as the hero, or a before/after divider.
9. **Are all 18 parts covered? The card is filled, but one claim is false.** `docs/build-notes/coverage/design.md` has 18/18 parts, and its part 15 claims "check.mjs at 375×812 … CLS 0.000" and "375×812 … pass with no overlap". I measured CLS 0.06–0.12 at 375 and a clipped section, so the false claim caps this page at 7 (_shared.md §2).

## Fixes, in order
1. **Phone: kill the brief drift.** Under 900px, set no `translateX` on `.dz-brief-l` (or clamp the drift to 0). Nothing may sit outside the 16px gutter.
2. **Phone: the capability sheets must arrive.** `.dz-e-roll` never flips from `pre` at 375. Use a smaller `amount` (or per-sheet in-view) so each sheet reaches opacity 1 when it is on screen, and never leave a sheet at .35 at rest.
3. **Phone: hold the hero frame's height.** Give `.dz-frame` a fixed aspect box on phone (the tallest artifact's), so the auto-cycling tabs morph inside it and stop pushing the page (CLS to under 0.01). Then correct the coverage card's part 15.
4. **Give the second half its own moment.** "Everything a creative team ships" should not be six equal boxes. Make it one stage where the six capabilities step through one large frame (the hero's frame morph, reused at section scale), or a 1-big/2-small rhythm. Cut the "Your brief" placeholder titles until the visitor types, or show the artifact name instead.
5. **Stop re-showing the hero's artifacts.** Use each capability's own media (Koma film, Iro stills, the deck pages) rather than the same waveform and Neon Horizon poster again.
6. **Close the white gap** under Paste and ship / Get Design (about 200px at 1470), and let the Band's heading be the one big type moment (64px) on its way in.
