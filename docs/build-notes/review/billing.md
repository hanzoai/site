## Fixes (ordered, most important first)
- **Close the dead air between rooms.** Desktop, after the hero pin releases, ~350px of blank white sits between the machine's bottom and "The objects you bill with". Either let the chapter head rise into the machine's exit (40px overlap, one in as one goes out), or shorten the pin's tail. Also trim the ~190px empty top of the black history room and the ~160px empty top of Get started.
- **Don't replay the hero machine as three dark boxes at the close.** `bl2-fin` repeats events → meter → invoice a third time, as three same-height dark tiles in a row (a banned tell). Make the close one object: just the Issued invoice landing once, or the single meter line. It should not be a third copy of the hero.
- **Make the chapter numbers agree.** In chapter 02 the left meter read 5 while the invoice read "× 10" in the same frame. In chapter 03 the stepper showed "Issued" while the invoice chip still read "Preview". Drive both from one value so the logic reads true ("logic applied").
- **Phone: give the hero machine its gutter, and don't stack three full invoices.** `.l2-stage-pin` / `.bl2-mach` sit at left 0, width 375, so the outer frame's rounded border is clipped at both edges. Put them inside the 16px gutter. In stack mode, show the full invoice once and let chapters 02 and 03 show only the line that changes (the × 18 row, then the stamp), instead of three ~600px invoices.
- **Phone code pane wraps mid-token.** In the typescript pane at 375, `'default_incomplete',` and `}],` fall onto their own lines. Use horizontal scroll (a labelled `tabindex=0` region) with no wrapping inside string literals.
- **Hover on the 01/02/03 chapter rail** (a 48x44 button, currently no hover change): darken the segment or lift the number. Also let the history ledger rows answer hover themselves, lighting their matching capability on the left.
- **Dark rooms enter as rounded cards that open to full width** (MASTER 3.1). Both `bl2-hist` and `bl2-fin` currently cut in as hard rectangles.
