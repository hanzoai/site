Rank

Rank reads as opacity.

The palette is a ladder, not a wheel. White on black, in fixed steps. Higher is more important. No in-between step is ever invented.

Motion

Restrained, never still.

Hanzo's rule: an entry is a fade and a small rise, hover is colour only, nothing bounces. The redesign keeps every one of those and adds arrivals that suit each object.

duration-press90 ms
duration-fast150 ms
duration-base300 ms
duration-slow400 ms
duration-slower500 ms

Also in the tokens: stagger 60 ms, entry-rise 16 px (24 px large), an ambient glow that breathes over 9 s. The redesign runs all of it through Motion (motion.dev) using these exact curves and times. It uses no springs and no transform on hover, as Hanzo's rules ask.

Materials

Five rooms, not three.

Silver and graphite sit between paper and black, so a page can change material eight times without repeating one. Black rooms open from a card and carry a live halftone field.

Paper#ffffff · reading, forms, rows
Quiet#f5f5f5 · questions, tables
Silvergradient #e9e9e9 to #f6f6f6 · a sheen follows the pointer
Graphite#171717 · ledgers, code, help
Black#000 · art, races, the field
  • FieldThe room's own halftone on the GPU: it breathes, parts around the pointer and ripples where it is pressed.
  • BloomA dark room enters as a rounded card inside the gutters and opens to the full width as it is reached.
  • DepthArt sits a layer behind the words and drifts a little slower than the page.
  • WordsSection headings arrive one word after another, from under a line.
  • SceneA stage sticks while its steps pass; on Models one mark becomes the next family.
  • TickerOne line of the page's own words, very large and very thin, moving.
  • AnswerButtons lean toward the pointer, cards tilt a few degrees, strokes part and brighten under it.
  • Between pagesThe page crossfades and settles; the header and dock stay put. The scroll keeps a little weight.

Art

Drawn in white line, on true black.

The model cards on the home page set the style: fine white strokes of uneven weight on black. Hanzo's imagery rule is cool-toned, low-key, no warm gradients, no illustration style.

One set of 320 strokes that redraws itself as four shapes. A new product gets a new shape as data, with no new code and no image file.

Model marks. The model's ID is the seed: the lab picks the family of shape, the model picks its proportions. The same ID always draws the same mark, so all 543 model pages gain their own image at no cost. The IDs above are examples.

Pages

One system. No two pages alike.

Every page shares the tokens above. Each one then gets its own idea, its own object and its own moment, so the site never reads as one template repeated.

  • Home

    The race. Two bars run for as long as each product really took.

    Product cards hold a live fragment of each product instead of an icon.

  • Models

    The wall of marks. Every model is a row with its own mark.

    Point at a row and its mark is drawn large beside the table.

  • Model page

    The plotter. The model's mark is drawn stroke by stroke.

    Its figures settle beside it; the code arrives line by line.

  • Product page

    The route. One request line splits to the models behind it.

    Feature rows alternate sides, each with one real object.

  • Enterprise

    The seal. A waveform closes into a sealed ring as you scroll.

    Four places to run, one pinned scene, deep detail one click down.

  • Cloud service

    The boot. A terminal starts a cluster in the time it really takes.

    A timeline under it marks 0.30 s and 7 s as they pass.

  • Open source

    The ledger. Counts and repositories set like a quiet ledger.

    Small numbers stay small. Search filters the rows as you type.

  • Benchmark

    The paper. A reading column with notes in the margin.

    Bars drawn to true scale; what is not measured gets equal weight.

  • Integration guide

    The rail. A line down the side fills as each step is passed.

    Numbered steps, code with tabs, a contents list that follows you.

  • Pricing

    The columns. Three plans as columns of one sheet, not three cards.

    Point at a plan and its column lifts out of the others.

  • Customers

    The figures. Results as very large thin numerals.

    One story in a black room; the rest as a hairline grid.

  • Company

    The letter. A calm reading page, set like a letter.

    Wide margins, one statement, a hairline timeline.

  • Contact sales

    The exchange. What you send on the right, what comes back on the left.

    Fields answer as you type; the form is replaced by what happens next.

  • Not found

    The field. The halftone dots swell under the pointer.

    A quiet numeral, a search field, four useful links.
