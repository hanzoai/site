# /dataroom coverage (PLAN-COMPONENTS areas 2.1–2.18)

Page file: `components/pages/apps/Dataroom.jsx` (+ `Dataroom.css`) · gallery: `docs/gallery/dataroom/`

1. **Strategy and conversion.** Persona: a founder raising, or a deal team running diligence, sending documents outside. Goal: see what a link tells you and controls after it has gone, then start sharing. One ask, Hanzo's "Try Hanzo" (/login): solid under the title, in the sticky AskBar after the hero, on the phone thumb bar and as the one solid button of the close. "Documentation", the plans' "Contact Sales" / "Get on GitHub" and every other door are text links.
2. **Information architecture and navigation.** Hanzo's header and footer are unchanged. Order: the live room, the five steps ("A link, shared") with the room pinned, the features as the room's folder tree, the dark room to share it ten ways, the plans ladder with the open-source note, the close.
3. **First impression and page narrative.** Nothing above the h1: title, the "Secure Document Sharing" label under it, one line, the ask; the room sits beside it at 1470×801 (views figure, feed and the page strip all visible) and under it at 375×812 (bar, views and feed inside the first 660px). The room moves within a second with no input.
4. **Conversion system.** `data-thumb="Try Hanzo"` and `data-cta="primary"` on the hero ask and on the close ask; `AskBar` (title "Dataroom") is the last child of `<main>`, steps aside while the close is on screen, and the site's own bar is hidden on desktops so only one sticky ask shows. One solid button per screen (the header's "Try Hanzo" turns outline).
5. **Trust and proof.** Every sentence, price and plan line is Hanzo's (parity 102/102). The room's views count, feed and reading bars are demo state, labelled with Hanzo's own phrases (time spent on each page, how far they got, came back); no invented claims.
6. **Content presentation (copy untouched).** One idea per screen; the ten feature descriptions open file by file (BizOpen) and are truncated to one line in the tree, full in the panel. No kicker above any headline; labels sit under them.
7. **Visual design system.** Biz grammar (Display 96/80, Headline, Lede, Body, Zen Mono labels), ink, greys and white only: no hues. Highlights are ink against grey (the current page, the page read twice, the active bar), inverted chips ("came back", "2×", "Most Popular") and weight. One dark room: "A link you still control".
8. **UI component library.** Kit: `A`, `More`, `BizLines`, `BizChapters`, `BizOpen`, `AskBar`. Custom controls: pause, switches (`role=switch`), gate buttons, file rows, close rows; each has hover (lift, detail or veil), press .98 and focus. No browser-default controls.
9. **Motion and interaction.** Idle: the room reads Deck.pdf page by page, the camera follows, visits arrive, views tick (44px Pause/Play). Scroll: five pinned chapters redraw the room. State: files open into their page (BizOpen morph), switches spring, gates fly off as you clear them. Arrival: headlines cut into their line, the room slides in by CSS. Motion tokens from `lib/motion`.
10. **Media.** No Hanzo film or photo applies; the hero object is product UI (the room, Deck.pdf's ten pages drawn as slides).
11. **Mobile and responsive.** At 375px: title, label, line, ask, then the room bleeding off the right edge; chapters pin a compact room on top; the switches go to one column; the ladder becomes indented rungs; close rows stack. No text under 14px, every target 44px or more, no sideways scroll.
12. **Accessibility.** One h1; real buttons and links; `role=switch` with `aria-checked`; `aria-pressed` on Pause; BizOpen panels are dialogs with focus in and back; the email field is labelled. Reduced motion: the room shows its finished visit, chapters and switches without transitions.
13. **Performance and engineering.** Transform and opacity only (the reading bars are scaleX, the strip a translateX); the room's timer runs only while on screen and not paused. No blur, canvas or video. JS off: all hero text is visible in the server HTML (0 opacity:0 runs).
14. **SEO and machine files.** Metadata and JSON-LD unchanged (`app/dataroom/page.jsx`). All copy, including every panel's text, is in the server HTML.
15. **Catalogue and product pages.** The features read as a catalogue of ten files that open into their page (synchronized.studio).
16. **Developer experience.** Docs, the repo (hanzoai/dataroom, 3 stars), upstream Papermark and Deploy to Cloud are kept as rows and links.
17. **States and resilience.** The room has playing, paused and reduced-motion states; the viewer has gated, cleared ("Open it again") and "Opens straight away" states, downloads on/off and expiry on/off. No network data, so no loading or error state.
18. **Measurement and handover.** `data-cta="primary"` marks both asks; no analytics wired yet (pending Hanzo's event log). Filmed at 1470 and 375 (`desktop.mp4`, `desktop-hero.jpg`, `phone-1..3.jpg`); interactions checked by script (a file opens and closes, gates clear, the typed email is the watermark).
