# /careers/* coverage (PLAN-COMPONENTS areas 2.1–2.18)

Template: `components/pages/CareerRole.jsx` + `CareerRole.css`, data `components/pages/careersData.js` (`role`, `peers`, `deckNo`), route `app/careers/[slug]/page.jsx`. Eight members. Checked: `/careers/compiler-engineer`, `/careers/open` (also academic-research-intern). Gallery: `docs/gallery/careers/compiler-engineer-*`, `open-*` (the other files there belong to the /careers index).

1. **Strategy and conversion.** Persona: an engineer, designer or researcher deciding to apply. Goal: apply. Metric: one contact click (the application email). One solid ask in Hanzo's words, "Apply for this position" (`data-thumb`, `data-cta="careers.hero"`, magnetic); "Email resume directly" is outlined.
2. **Information architecture and navigation.** Hanzo's header and footer unchanged. "Back to all careers" leads the page; the sheet's contents open every chapter, Why Join Hanzo and the application; the other roles close the page. Real `<a href>` throughout.
3. **First impression and page narrative.** The hero is the same white sheet as the role's card on /careers (docs/review/careers.md fix 5): same title type (600, −.045em, line height 1), the same giant numeral from the deck (01–07; the open application has none), the same tags and line, and it is the Morph target, so the card you clicked lands as this page's hero. At 1470×801 the whole sheet (title, tags, line, both asks, numeral, contents) is on the first screen; at 375×812 the sheet holds numeral, title, tags, line and asks. The title cuts in on load and the role's contour drifts in the sheet within a second. No eyebrow anywhere: the team is a tag under the title, and the chapter counters are gone.
4. **Conversion system.** The site ask bar carries "Apply for this position" after the hero (named after the role via `data-ask-name`); on phones the thumb bar does the same. One filled button per screen; in the dark room the form's own submit is the one fill.
5. **Trust and proof.** Hanzo's own "Why Join Hanzo" as an editorial list; the application writes the same email Hanzo's form writes, to careers@hanzo.ai.
6. **Content presentation (copy untouched).** Parity: compiler-engineer 47/47 text, 2/2 links; open 42/42, 2/2; academic-research-intern 46/46, 2/2; the /careers index still 76/76, 13/13. One chapter per screen; long lists are tickable rows, not a wall.
7. **Visual design system.** Ink, greys and white only (no orange: numerals, meters, ticks, progress and focus rings are ink). One dark room (the application). The sheet keeps the deck's 28px radius and shadow so the open is continuous; cards 16px; tags are mono pills.
8. **UI component library.** Kit `A`, `Magnetic`; v2 `FamLines`, `FamChapters`, `FamNext`. The step controls are round arrow buttons named from the register (`previous`, `next`); share answers with a drawn tick and a `copied` status; the file picker and the opt-in checkbox are styled (no browser defaults).
9. **Motion and interaction.**
   - Arrival: the title rises out of its masks.
   - Idle: the role's contour (Sigil seeded `/careers/<slug>`) drifts behind the numeral.
   - State: each requirement you mark draws its tick and fills its chapter's meter and the total by the form; the form slides step to step; share swaps its link icon for a tick.
   - Pointer: the apply button leans to the pointer; contents rows nudge on hover; the sibling rail drags with inertia; press is .98 or 1px everywhere.
   - Scroll: the chapter book (words pin, the page wipes in). Under half of the page's motion.
10. **Media.** Hanzo has no photographs for roles; the role's own contour, the giant numeral and the tickable lists are the visuals.
11. **Mobile and responsive.** 375px: the sheet stacks (numeral, title, tags, line, asks), chapters read straight down, the form takes one column with 48px fields and arrows. Sideways swipe turns to the previous or next role (site Phone layer; tested compiler-engineer → cryptography-engineer-zkp and open → mcp-protocol-engineer). Targets ≥44px, no sideways scroll.
12. **Accessibility.** One h1. Requirements are `role=checkbox` buttons with `aria-checked`; the form's steps are fieldsets with legends; inactive steps are inert only once scripts run; status text for share and the ready email comes from the register. Reduced motion: no drift, no slides, meters and ticks set at once.
13. **Performance and engineering.** One Sigil drifting in the hero (others still); the chapter scene is CSS transitions on opacity, transform and clip; no timers.
14. **SEO and machine files.** Metadata and JSON-LD unchanged. With JS off every chapter, every requirement and every step of the form are in the HTML and visible (steps stack).
15. **Catalogue and product pages.** The other roles close the page as up to six cards (next roles first), each with its own contour, Hanzo's line on hover and its tags; titles are Morph sources into the next role's sheet.
16. **Developer experience.** Not a developer surface.
17. **States and resilience.** The form checks each step before the next (native validity, focus on the first problem); the ready state shows a tick and the address. Nothing is sent from the page.
18. **Measurement and handover.** `data-cta="careers.hero"` on the ask; no analytics wired yet. Ledger (deleted): the orange accent, the kicker over the h1 and the counters over every headline, the separate contents plate (folded into the hero sheet), the red required mark, the browser checkbox, and the invented "Back", "Next", "· Copied" and "Your email is ready" strings.
