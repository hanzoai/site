1. **Strategy and conversion.** Persona: Developer. Goal: try Hanzo MQ (read the source, then deploy it). One ask, Hanzo's "GitHub" (https://github.com/hanzoai): solid in the hero (`data-cta="mq.hero"`), carried by the site's sticky ask after the hero, and again as the one solid at the close (`data-cta="mq.close"`). "View on GitHub" is a ghost button, "Deploy to Cloud" a text link. Metric: `api_key_created`.
2. **Information architecture and navigation.** Hanzo's header and footer are unchanged. Order: the queue at work (hero), the statement and six facts on one inspector, the code in the dark room, the free tier and spec, the close. The inspector's tabs take you to their chapter, as the rail does.
3. **First impression and page narrative.** At 1470×801 the first screen holds the cut-in "Hanzo MQ", the line "Background work, done later" with its ask beside it, and the whole live conveyor beneath (toolbar, three lanes, QueueEvents). Tickets move within about a second, with no input. No label above the headline: "Job queue" now sits in the conveyor's own toolbar.
4. **Conversion system.** `data-thumb="GitHub"` on the hero ask drives the site's ask bar at every width; `data-ask-name="Hanzo MQ"` names the page in it. The header's "Try Hanzo" is held as an outline on this page, so one solid shows per screen.
5. **Trust and proof.** Every figure is Hanzo's (6379, 10, 100, 60_000, 5000, 3, 1000, 10K jobs/month). Job names, ids and the stack trace are examples drawn by the page, not measurements.
6. **Content presentation (copy untouched).** Parity 81/81 text and 2/2 links. The statement opens the pinned scene beside its object instead of standing alone; the six facts come one at a time; the free tier's "10K" is set as a giant figure (decorative, aria-hidden) above Hanzo's own sentence.
7. **Visual design system.** LaunchType scale (display 128 → 56, headline, lede, body; Zen Mono labels). 0 UI hues: ink, greys, silvers, white. Job states read by fill (running = ink, done = grey fill), weight (priority 1 = 1.5px ink ring, bold), dash (backing off, failed set, a run that will fail) and a shape (✓, ×; events: filled, crossed, half, dashed dot). One dark room.
8. **UI component library.** Kit: `LineMask`, `SettleStage`, `PinnedChapters` (with `intro`), `ClipWipe`, `SpecDrawer` (rows only), `LaunchClose` (`ask`, `cta`), `LivePause`; Frame's `Paste` and `Code`; ui's `Count`. Tabs, Retry and pause are real buttons with hover (ink, underline or fill, never opacity) inside `(hover:hover)` and press (translateY 1px or .98). No `title=` tooltips, no browser-default controls.
9. **Motion and interaction.**
   - Idle: the conveyor (tickets glide lane to lane on one layoutId FLIP, progress lines run, the limiter fills, QueueEvents prints) and the inspector's panels; each has a pause. Timeouts only, stopped off screen.
   - Pointer: a job under the cursor shows its priority, attempt and lane in the conveyor's toolbar; tickets answer hover with an ink edge; the close's ask leans to the pointer.
   - State: the inspector's tab underline morphs (layoutId); Retry puts the failed job back and Undo returns it.
   - Arrival: the line-masked title, the timed wipe into the dark room, "10K" cutting in once (the real figure; it never counts through numbers that are not Hanzo's).
   - Scroll (under half): the stage settle and the pinned chapters.
10. **Media.** The page has no Hanzo images or film. Its objects are product UI: the conveyor, the job inspector, worker.ts, the close's Sigil.
11. **Mobile and responsive.** At 390×664 the lanes stack (three waiting, two done shown), the inspector fits a 37svh window so every chapter's words clear the thumb bar (pinchk: 7/7 ok), panels drop a row or a line on phones. Text 14px or more, targets 44px or more, no sideways scroll at 320.
12. **Accessibility.** One h1. The conveyor is a labelled image with a real pause button (`aria-pressed`); the inspector has its own pause. Tabs use `aria-current`. Muted text is #6e6e73 or darker on white; dark-room text is #a1a1a6 or lighter. Reduced motion: still conveyor, progress at rest, no cut-in, chapters swap without movement.
13. **Performance and engineering.** No setInterval in the page (timeouts while live and not paused); motion is transform, opacity and clip-path; no video, no canvas, no blur. One continuous mover per screen.
14. **SEO and machine files.** Metadata and JSON-LD unchanged (`app/mq/page.jsx`). With JS off every chapter, the paste block and the code are visible (checked: 0 hidden text runs).
15. **Catalogue and product pages.** Product page: the close carries license (Apache-2.0) and source (hanzoai) as quiet facts.
16. **Developer experience.** The agent prompt and worker.ts have copy buttons; the hover read-out mirrors `queue.add` options (priority, attempts, backoff).
17. **States and resilience.** The demo shows the queue's own states: waiting, backoff, running, completed, failed set with its error, retry and undo, a worker dying mid-job and the job handed over, a process restarting while the schedule is held. Figures are static, so there is no loading or error state.
18. **Measurement and handover.** `data-cta` ids `mq.hero` and `mq.close`; the site's ask bar fires `cta_click` (where: ask-bar). Filmed with `film.cjs`: `d.mp4`, `d.jpg`, `p.jpg`.
