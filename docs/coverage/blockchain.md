# /blockchain (the overview) coverage (PLAN-COMPONENTS areas 2.1–2.18)

Page file: `components/pages/blockchain/BlockchainIndex.jsx` (+ `BlockchainIndex.css`) · gallery: `docs/gallery/blockchain/` (desktop.mp4, desktop-hero.jpg, phone-1..3.jpg). The /blockchain/* family template (`BlockPage.jsx`, `Directory.jsx`, `blockchain.css`, `bcData.js`) belongs to another group and is untouched; this page only reads `bcData.js` through `app/blockchain/page.jsx`.

1. **Strategy and conversion.** Persona: a developer building on one chain who will need a second. Goal: see that the chain is an argument, find the piece they need, then start. One ask, Hanzo's "Try Hanzo" (/signup): solid beside the title, in the site's sticky ask bar after the hero (every width) and as the one solid button of the close. "View Pricing" is a text link (hero) and a full-width row (close).
2. **Information architecture and navigation.** Hanzo's header and footer are unchanged. Order: the chain argument, the pieces (catalogue index), what sits behind the key (nine groups), the agent (dark room), the chains with the coverage matrix, the close. Each piece links to its own /blockchain/* page.
3. **First impression and page narrative.** Nothing above the h1: title (two lines, the second soft), the ask with "Now Available" and the free-tier line beside it, then one giant line, `chain:` and a reel of every network Hanzo lists, with the product pages that name it lighting below. At 1470×801 the line, its count and the product chips are on screen; at 375×812 the line and the first chips are inside 660px. It moves within a second. The long lede follows the object.
4. **Conversion system.** `data-thumb="Try Hanzo"` and `data-cta="primary"` on the hero and close asks; `data-ask-name="Hanzo Blockchain"` on `<main>` names the page in the site's sticky ask bar, which carries the same label at every width and steps aside while either data-thumb button is on screen; the header's "Try Hanzo" turns outline while the page's ask is on screen.
5. **Trust and proof.** Every word is Hanzo's (parity 153/153 text, 16/16 links). The numbers shown (k / 15 product pages name a chain, "36 chains on its page", the matrix totals) are counted from Hanzo's own product pages, not claimed.
6. **Content presentation (copy untouched).** Catalogue of 19 pieces collapsed to 8 with a true count ("Show all 19 pieces"; every row stays in the HTML). Nine groups open one at a time. No kicker above any headline.
7. **Visual design system.** Biz grammar (Display 128/96, Headline, Body, Zen Mono labels), ink, greys and white only: no hues (the family's violet is not used). Highlights are ink against grey: lit product chips, the lit row and column, inverted chips. One dark room: the agent.
8. **UI component library.** Kit: `A`, `More`, `Copy`, `BizLines` (and its CSS cut-in for the h1). Custom controls (pause, show-all, group toggles, matrix cells, index rows, close rows) answer hover (lift, detail, veil), press .98 and focus.
9. **Motion and interaction.** Idle: the reel rolls through 32 networks and the product chips light (44px Pause/Play, stops off screen). State: a piece's name flies into its page's hero (Morph, `data-morph` → the family's `data-morph-target` h1); groups and the show-all toggle open. Pointer: the matrix lights a row and column and reads out the pair with both counts; rows nudge. Arrival: headlines cut into their line.
10. **Media.** No Hanzo imagery applies; the hero object is the argument itself; the agent room shows Hanzo's own code sample, in greys.
11. **Mobile and responsive.** At 375px: title, ask, the line stacked (`chain:` over the value), chips; index rows stack with count and "Get started" on one line; the matrix scrolls sideways inside its own region with the product column pinned (the page does not scroll sideways). Text ≥ 14px, targets ≥ 44px.
12. **Accessibility.** One h1; the reel is `aria-hidden` with an sr-only "chain: <value>"; `aria-pressed` on Pause, `aria-expanded` on the toggles; the matrix is a real table with scope headers, a focusable region and an `aria-live` readout; every dot has an aria-label ("Hanzo Pay names Base"). Reduced motion: the reel stands still on Ethereum.
13. **Performance and engineering.** The reel is one translateY on a column of names (transform only), one timer while seen and not paused. No blur, canvas or video. Server HTML shows all hero text (0 opacity:0 runs).
14. **SEO and machine files.** Metadata and JSON-LD unchanged (`app/blockchain/page.jsx`). Every piece, group sentence, chain and matrix cell is in the server HTML.
15. **Catalogue and product pages.** The pieces are the catalogue: an index with a true count, each row opening into its product page by morph; the matrix answers "which products run on my chain".
16. **Developer experience.** The agent's TypeScript sample with Copy (44px); "the call you already wrote takes a different argument" is the page's idea.
17. **States and resilience.** Reel: playing, paused, reduced motion. Matrix: idle prompt ("Point at a cell, or tap one") and a readout for every pair, named or not. Data is read at build time from Hanzo's pages; a product page without chains simply drops out of the matrix.
18. **Measurement and handover.** `data-cta="primary"` on both asks; no analytics wired yet (pending Hanzo's event log). Interactions checked by script: pause holds, show-all 8 → 19, a group opens, the matrix lights 16 cells in a column with a readout, a piece opens /blockchain/chains, one sticky bar at a time.
