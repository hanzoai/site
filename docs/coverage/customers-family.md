# Coverage: /customers/* (one template, components/pages/story/Story.jsx; members /customers/damon-motorcycles, /customers/kanoa)

- **2.1 Strategy and conversion:** Persona, Goal and CTA lines are in the file header. There is one solid ask, Hanzo's "The original case study (PDF)": in the stage and at the close. "Every launch →" stays a text link. Gap: the metric (`contact_sales_submit`) is journey level only; nothing is wired on this page.
- **2.2 Information architecture and navigation:** "← Customers" in the stage and "Every launch →" at the close both go back to the index. A next-launch card at the end links to the sibling story and opens into it (Morph `data-morph` on its picture, `data-morph-target` on this hero). No links were removed (link parity 2/2 on both).
- **2.3 First impression and page narrative:** the first screen is the launch's picture full-bleed with the name cut into its line, then year and kind under it and the one ask. Below that: one statement (the lede), the figures on a rule, the platform report (Damon only), the chapters as 100svh scenes, the media wall and the way out.
- **2.4 Conversion system:** one filled button per screen. `data-thumb` is on the closing PDF button, so the site's phone thumb bar carries it after the hero. There are no forms. Gaps: no `data-cta` attribute, and no `lib/cta.js` (the file does not exist yet).
- **2.5 Trust and proof:** the figures are Hanzo's own. Pressing one travels to the sentence that states it and marks it; a figure the page never states opens Hanzo's case-study PDF. Year and kind ("2015 · Crowdfunding and preorders", "2020 · CES launch and preorders") label these as past commerce results. Nothing was added.
- **2.6 Content presentation (copy untouched):** parity is 0 missing (Damon 37/37 text, KANOA 26/26). Body text runs at 62ch and 19px (17px on phone). Headings use `text-wrap: balance`, text uses `pretty`, and the lede is set at statement size.
- **2.7 Visual design system:** no UI hues: ink, greys, silver and white. Colour is meant to come only from the launch's own pictures. Hero objects use 28px radii and tiles 16px, with 1px hairlines and one dark room (the stage). Display line height is 0.92. Gap: the values are local to Story.css because there is no shared tokens.css or CANON file yet.
- **2.8 UI component library:** uses the kit's `A`, `Flap`, `Sigil` and `useArrival` (doorway), plus the global `.x-btn`. The parts local to this template are `Pic` (a picture over its caption plate), `Figures`, `Scene` and `Wall`. Gap: a kit `Pic`, `Cta` or `PageHero` does not exist yet, so a raw `<img>` and `.x-btn` are used.
- **2.9 Motion and interaction:** the signature is chapters as sticky scenes, each wiping up over the last with a scroll-scrubbed clip-path. Motion that is not scroll-linked: the name cuts in, the Sigil drifts and leans to the pointer, the figures spin to their value (split-flap), pressing a figure travels and marks its words, tiles wipe open on arrival, and hovers give real detail (captions, arrows). `variety.py` OK: 4 techniques, 0 fade-ups. Easing is [0.16,1,0.3,1]; only transform, opacity and clip-path animate.
- **2.10 Media:** every picture is Hanzo's own file under `/customers/<slug>/*.webp`. The cover uses fetchPriority high and the rest are lazy and async-decoded. Gap: these files are not in the repository, and hanzo.ai was blocked in the cloud sandbox, so every frame here (and in the gallery films) shows Hanzo's caption on a silver plate instead. There is no video.
- **2.11 Mobile and responsive:** checked at 375×812 and 1470×801, with no sideways scroll. On phone the scenes stand in one column, and each picture wipes open on arrival. Figures become a list and the gallery a single column with captions always shown. Tap targets are 44px or more.
- **2.12 Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA):** one h1 with h2 and h3 in order. Figures are real buttons with `aria-pressed`, and PDF figures are links with an aria-label. Pictures keep Hanzo's alt text, and the plates are `aria-hidden`. Reduced motion stacks the scenes and shows final states. Gap: `--faint` (#86868b) is used for small labels and is below 4.5:1, and no axe run was done.
- **2.13 Performance and engineering:** the scenes run on motion values (no React state per frame; only the reel index changes). There is no permanent will-change, and the Sigil pauses off screen. Gap: no Lighthouse, LCP or CLS measurement, and there is no budgets.json.
- **2.14 SEO and machine files:** metadata comes from `content/pages.json`, plus `<JsonLd route>` and `generateStaticParams` over the members. Nothing new was added.
- **2.15 Catalogue and product pages:** does not apply (story page). The only catalogue touch is the next-launch card.
- **2.16 Developer experience:** does not apply (no code on the page).
- **2.17 States and resilience:** a picture that fails to load (including ones that failed before hydration) hides and leaves Hanzo's caption plate. Reduced motion shows final states. Gap: with JS off, the h1 is server-rendered at its pre-cut-in offset (translateY(108%)) and the stacked desktop scenes after the first are clipped, so the page is not legible without JS.
- **2.18 Measurement and handover:** the `V2:` line is in the header. Proof is in `docs/gallery/customers/` (desktop mp4 and phone jpg per member). Gap: no analytics events wired.
