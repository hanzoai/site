# /blockchain/* coverage (PLAN-COMPONENTS areas 2.1–2.18)

Template: `components/pages/blockchain/BlockPage.jsx` (16 products) + `Directory.jsx` (the chain directory) + `BlockPage.css` + `bcData.js` · gallery: `docs/gallery/blockchain/` (defi film and phone shots, directory and exchange phone shots).

1. **Strategy and conversion.** Persona: a developer or product team building on chains. Goal: see what this product does on which chains, then get a key. One solid ask in Hanzo's words: "Try Hanzo" on the key card (`data-thumb`, `data-cta="blockchain.key"`, magnetic on a mouse; metric `api_key_created` / `signup_start`). "View Documentation" is outlined. The exchange's own hero pair ("Read the docs" solid, "Contact Sales" outlined) works the same way. The directory has no hero button, so the site ask bar carries the header's "Try Hanzo".
2. **Information architecture and navigation.** Header and footer untouched. Order: hero with its object, the features Hanzo lists once, the exploded deck of capabilities, code, Built For, Supported Chains with the chain finder, sibling products, the closing room, the Get strip. "Back to Web3 Overview" stays as a link, now in the meta row under the lede.
3. **First impression and page narrative.** At 1470×801 the name, claim and lede sit on the left and the hero object on the right, whole on the first screen: the black key card (chains streaming across its top, "Get Your API Key", its figures huge) with this product's own contour drifting behind it. The exchange shows its product console there instead, walking its four products by itself. At 375×812 the object starts about 480px down with its title and ask visible. No eyebrow over any headline: "Available now" / "Exchange infrastructure" / "Chain Resource Directory" now sit in the meta row under the lede.
4. **Conversion system.** The site ask bar (components/Ask.jsx) carries the hero's ask after the hero at every width (`data-thumb`), and steps aside over the closing room (`data-ask-hide`). One solid per screen: the Get strip's "Deploy to Cloud" is outlined now so it never sits beside the closing room's white button.
5. **Trust and proof.** The key card's figures (HA, <50ms, 100+) and "Trusted by developers building" chips, Hanzo's own. Supported Chains lists what the page names; the finder shows which other products name a chain, from their own lists.
6. **Content presentation (copy untouched).** Parity: defi 149/149 text, 3/3 links; exchange 109/109, 6/6; wallets 170/170, 4/4; directory 62/62; the /blockchain index 153/153, 16/16. All 17 members return 200. The finder's round-2 sentences ("Named on this page", "N other products name…", "Your chain") are gone from view: a tick or dash, the chain's name, a count and the product links (Hanzo's names); the sentences live in `content/ui-strings.json` as accessible names until James approves them.
7. **Visual design system.** Ink, greys and white only. Coloured accents, dots, numerals and the purple/green/yellow code colours of the shared stylesheet are overridden inside `.xb2` with grey steps (keywords white and bold, strings light grey, comments mid grey italic). Radii 32 for the key card, console and code, 16 for sheets and cards. Giant numerals in silver (Built For, console).
8. **UI component library.** Kit `A`, `Copy`, `Magnetic`; Families A kit `FamLines`, `FamStage`, `FamNext`; `ui()` for every functional string. Pickers and tabs are real buttons with `aria-pressed` / `role=tab`; the directory's open card is a dialog with focus return. The native `title=` tooltips on the directory marks are gone (each mark has its product name as hidden text).
9. **Motion and interaction.**
   - Idle and time: the chain stream on the key card, the product's contour drifting behind it, the exchange console advancing every 3.6s with a filling line, the directory's contour.
   - Pointer: magnetic ask; the stream holds under a pointer; the console holds while pointed at; Built For numerals turn ink on hover; sibling rail drags with inertia; cards lift, press is .98.
   - State: the chain finder, console tabs, the directory's kind pill (layoutId), the grid re-flowing and a card opening into its sheet.
   - Scroll (under half): the stage settles from 1.05; the capabilities deck is dealt by scroll (the signature). The directory has no scroll-linked motion.
10. **Media.** Hanzo has no photographs or film for these pages; the key card, code, the console and each product's contour are the visuals.
11. **Mobile and responsive.** 375px: one column, the key card or console under the meta row, the deck as a plain list, the directory as list rows (name, ID, kind, marks), not a grid. No sideways scroll (scrollWidth 375). Targets 44px or more. A sideways swipe turns to the neighbouring product (tested: defi → directory, directory → defi).
12. **Accessibility.** One h1. The stream is labelled with the key card's title and the chains; the finder's tick/dash is `role=img` with its name from the register; the directory's count is `aria-live`. Reduced motion: no running animations after 3.5s (stream, console and contour hold still).
13. **Performance and engineering.** CSS marquee paused off screen (`[data-off]`); the console timer runs only while on screen and not held. Canvases: one member contour plus at most 6 still sibling contours. Transform and opacity only.
14. **SEO and machine files.** Metadata and JSON-LD unchanged (`app/blockchain/[slug]/page.jsx`). With JS off, 0 text blocks are hidden on defi and on the directory.
15. **Catalogue and product pages.** The directory: real buttons per chain that open in place, filter by kind, search, filter by product, a live count, an empty state (a silver 0 with its accessible name). Products: up to 6 sibling cards that open into their page (title is the Morph source), each with its own contour and its first chains.
16. **Developer experience.** The code block keeps its Copy (copies exactly the displayed text) and file name; language chips are Hanzo's.
17. **States and resilience.** Static data; the directory's empty state is designed; members without a key card get the console; without code or chains those sections are absent.
18. **Measurement and handover.** `data-cta` on every primary (`blockchain.hero`, `blockchain.key`, `blockchain.band`, `blockchain.deploy` and the second actions); no analytics wired yet. Ledger: this round deleted the eyebrows over the h1, the per-member accent hue (buttons, dots, numerals, marks, progress lines), the purple/green/yellow code colours, the invented finder sentences, the native tooltips, and the phone card grid (now list rows).
