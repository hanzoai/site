How a bounty is taken

  1. Say on the issue that you are taking it.
  2. Open a pull request that says Fixes and the issue’s number.
  3. A maintainer reviews it, merges it, and credits your account with points and AI credits.

Verified bug reports and merged bounties earn Hanzo AI credits and leaderboard points, with email notifications sent as you climb ranks.

Propose a bounty

Found something worth fixing? Open an issue in its repository saying what is wrong, where, and how a fix is checked, and ask for the bounty label. A maintainer decides.
