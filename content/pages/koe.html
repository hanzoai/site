Capabilities

What Koe does

Director-grade emotional modulation

Direct nuances in tone: breath control, hesitation, whispers, intense delivery, irony, and pitch contour through natural language instructions.

Cross-lingual voice cloning

Preserves vocal identity, formant fingerprint and emotional timbre across languages and regional dialects.

Viseme-synchronized dubbing

Phoneme timing alignment matched to video mouth shapes and facial cadence for localization.

Actor likeness rights

Every synthesized voice carries an imperceptible cryptographic watermark, and its provenance is logged.
