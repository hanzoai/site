Capabilities

What Uta does

Multi-movement song and score generation

Composes full multi-movement compositions with cohesive motifs, verse-chorus-bridge progressions, and orchestral codas.

Dynamic video cue scoring

Automated tempo ramps, harmonic tension builds, and dynamic hits synchronized to scene cuts and dramatic beats.

Multitrack stem separation

Generates separate mixable stems: lead vocal, backing vocal, drums, bass, rhythm guitars, synthesizers, and orchestral strings.

Precise music theory controls

Explicit direction over key signatures, modes (Dorian, Mixolydian, etc.), time signatures, BPM tempos, and arrangement density.
