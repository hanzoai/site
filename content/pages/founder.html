Questions

Who grants the Pro time, and when?
A Hanzo admin, by hand, once the pull request has merged and you have sent it with the form on this page. Nothing grants it automatically.
Which pull requests count?
One a maintainer merges into a public repository of hanzoai, hanzo-apps, hanzo-go, hanzo-js. Level 2 is three merged pull requests, or 1,000 lines merged.
Does a bounty pay?
No. A bounty is an issue scoped for an outside fix, and the points it names are reputation, not money. Its merged fix counts toward a level like any other pull request.
