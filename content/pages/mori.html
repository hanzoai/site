Capabilities

What Mori does

Production quad and tri topologies

Generates clean, water-tight, manifold meshes with optimized edge flow ready for rigging, animation, and real-time engine shaders.

Complete PBR material synthesis

Delivers full Physically Based Rendering material stacks: albedo, normal, roughness, metallic, ambient occlusion, and emissive maps.

Spatially coherent environments

Construct full 3D interior and exterior scene geometry aligned to absolute real-world metric scale and coordinate grids.

Universal engine export

Exports USDZ, glTF and GLB, FBX and OBJ, and 3D Gaussian splats.
