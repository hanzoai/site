What is measured

hanzo-engine and llama.cpp, built against the same weights, run on the same box, alternating. Prefill is tokens per second at a given prompt length, and the ratio is ours over theirs, so above 1.000 is a win. The models are Zen-1.7B at Q4_K_M and Zen-4B at Q4_K_M; 7 repetitions per cell, as its run records, with the first discarded as warmup, a Student-t 95% interval on each engine, and the ratio's interval formed by adding the two relative intervals in quadrature.

The verdict is the harness's, not a reading of ours: WIN where the whole interval lies above 1.000 and neither engine's coefficient of variation exceeded 5%, which is how the harness says the box was quiet. Every cell below is one.
