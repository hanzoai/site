Conditions

NVIDIA Grace-Blackwell: 121 GB unified, NVFP4 + Latent Sparse Attention (D-LSA) (141.2 tok/s with DFlash2 · 46.29× prefill & 78.57× decode at 1M context (99.8% KV pruned))AMD Strix Halo: 128 GB unified, 4-bit build (0.1 ms warm snapshot TTFT · 262K context in two slots)Apple silicon: unified memory, MLX buildD-LSA (Distilled Latent Sparse Attention): 512-token blocks, 4-key pooling, mutual-cheapest Ward clustering into 16 semantic buckets (bucket_size = 8, bucket_cap = 16), top_p = 128 buckets, top_k = 512 pooled keys, window_size = 512, sink_size = 4N-LSA (Native Latent Sparse Attention): trained from scratch on next-token cross-entropy loss alone; 1.6× faster per-layer training at 128K tokens while matching full-attention accuracydecode is one request unless a row says otherwisea range is what the runs spread over; the low end is the figure ordered and drawn
