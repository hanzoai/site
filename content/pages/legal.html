The canonical home for Hanzo's legal agreements, policies, and notices. Every Hanzo product links here.

This is the canonical home for the agreements, policies, and notices that govern the Hanzo Services. Every Hanzo product — the Console, cloud, API documentation, and signup — links to these documents at hanzo.ai/legal/<name> rather than restating them, so there is one authoritative version of each.

Status: draft, pending counsel. The documents below are complete drafts written to be truthful about what the platform does today. They are not yet effective. Each carries a publication gate listing what must be verified — entity address, mailboxes, retention jobs, subprocessor rows, consent pipeline — before it is published as binding. See the internal implementation checklist for the engineering gates behind these commitments.

Agreements

  • Terms of Service — the master agreement for all Services, including Enso routing, benchmarks, research, agents, and APIs.
  • Data Processing Addendum — processor terms, SCCs, subprocessor governance, and security measures for business customers.

Policies

  • Privacy Policy — what personal data we process, why, and the four data-use states that govern research and training.
  • Refund Policy — when a payment is refunded, how cancelling works, and how to ask about a charge.
  • Acceptable Use Policy — the conduct rules for every user, application, and agent.
  • AI Research and Data Contribution Terms — the opt-in terms for using your data in Hanzo research and training, and the Verified Commons.
  • Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy — our posture under Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws.
  • Cybersecurity Policy — how we protect your data: who can reach it, how it is encrypted, and what happens if something goes wrong.

Notices and registers

  • Research API Supplemental Terms — terms for the /v1/research registry, evidence integrity, and durability states.
  • Subprocessor Register — the providers that process data on your behalf, including the Enso routing chain.
  • Cookie Notice — cookies and similar technologies, and how to control them.
  • Biometric, Voice, and Likeness Notice — how features that process faces, voices, or likeness are governed.
  • Text message opt-in — how to consent to, and stop, two-factor codes and account alerts by SMS.

How these fit together

The Terms of Service is the spine; it incorporates the Acceptable Use Policy, Privacy Policy, DPA, and — only on an affirmative election — the AI Research and Data Contribution Terms. The Privacy Policy defines four data-use states (service-only by default, then private improvement, research and training, and public commons); no data is used for training without an affirmative election, and none is published without a separate per-artifact contribution. The Subprocessor Register names every provider a request can reach, including model providers behind an inference gateway. The remaining notices cover cookies, biometric/voice/likeness, consumer health, and the research registry.
