Why We Build

Moving the frontier without trading away safety, privacy, or ownership.

Most AI platforms ask you to pick two: raw capability, low latency, affordable cost, or control over your data. Chasing the Pareto frontier means advancing every axis at once through better systems engineering and open research.

Deterministic Safety Bounds

Calibrated decisions, not guesswork

Unconstrained token generators drift in high-stakes workflows. Hanzo puts calibrated decisions (Kai), policy checks and isolated sandboxes in front of any autonomous agent that touches production data or billing.

Policy Governance: Policy checks stop a tool call or a change your policy refuses before it is sent.

Sovereign & Air-Gapped: Run inside your own VPC or offline cluster with zero external telemetry or third-party relays.

Calibrated Confidence: Kai returns calibrated probability distributions and defers cleanly when evidence is insufficient.
