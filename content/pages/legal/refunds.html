This policy explains how refunds work for purchases from Hanzo AI, Inc. It restates section 9 of the Terms of Service ("Plans, fees and payment"), which governs if the two ever differ.

1. Plans

  • Renewal. A paid plan renews automatically each month or year, as you chose, until you cancel. We tell you the renewal price and date before you buy.
  • Cancelling. Cancel any time from Billing in your settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the plan keeps working until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • No partial refunds. A plan payment is not refunded, in whole or in part, for the rest of a period after you cancel, downgrade or stop using it, except where the law requires it or we agree in writing.

2. Prepaid balance and credits

  • Money you add to your balance pays for usage. It is not refunded except where the law requires it or we agree in writing.
  • Prepaid credits cannot be transferred, have no cash value and may expire as disclosed when you buy them.
  • Credits we grant you (for example a starter credit or a promotion) have no cash value and are never refunded.

3. Your right to withdraw

Where your local law gives you a right to withdraw from a purchase, you keep it. If you ask for the Services to start during that period, we may charge for what you used before you withdrew.

4. Charges made in error

If you were charged twice for the same purchase, charged after you cancelled, or charged an amount you did not agree to, tell us at support@hanzo.ai with the date and amount. We will look into it and refund a charge made in error to the payment method it came from.

5. Crypto deposits

A deposit in a cryptocurrency or stablecoin is credited to your balance once the network confirms it. A transaction on a blockchain cannot be reversed, so send only the asset and network shown for your deposit address. Funds sent to the wrong network or as an unsupported asset may not be recoverable.

6. App store purchases

If you buy through an app store, that store's terms govern the purchase, and refunds are requested from the store under its rules.

7. Disputes

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank or card issuer, so we can resolve it directly. While a payment is disputed, we may pause the plan or usage it paid for.

8. Contact

Questions about a charge or this policy: support@hanzo.ai.
