Reading this honestly

A row marked we ran this is a number produced by our own harness, and the link goes to the run. A row marked they published this is each vendor’s own claim, taken from the page linked and dated the day it was read — we relay those, we did not verify them, and they are their numbers and not ours. Where a vendor has not published something the cell says not published rather than being left blank, because a blank reads as zero.

This page is not arranged to make Hanzo look best. Where OpenRouter, Portkey, LiteLLM are ahead, that is on the page. The bet is that a comparison you can check is worth more than one that only says good things about itself.
