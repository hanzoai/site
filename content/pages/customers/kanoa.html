How it went

  1. 01

    Prelaunch · July 1 to August 30, 2015

    Hanzo began the prelaunch with $39K of ad spend across Facebook and Google. It reached 4M+ early tech adopters and acquired 60,000+ users at an average cost of $1.50.

  2. 02

    Preorders · from August 30, 2015

    Daily ad spend grew 66 times from launch as the campaign scaled to 25M+ people. Hanzo's programmatic advertising acquired 45,000 customers at an average of $30 each, on a $149 product, and brought in over $1M a month in preorders: $7M+ raised.

  3. 03

    What Hanzo ran

    Website design, paid advertising, email campaigns, campaign management and product photography, on Hanzo's analytics, commerce and advertising platform. Public relations came from LMGPR and product design from Surfaceink.
