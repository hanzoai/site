What is Hanzo Design

One conversation, every creative artifact in a tab

Describe what you need or drop in your raw files. Hanzo Design creates and iterates on each artifact side-by-side in live tabs you can inspect, play, and export.

01

Upload any content

Drop in audio (.mp3, .wav, .flac), images, video clips, PDFs, Office docs, or CSV/JSON data. Every uploaded file is wired directly into the visuals, decks, and apps you create.

02

Presentations, documents & ads

Generate 16:9 keynote slide decks, typeset brand & technical documents with vector PDF export, and multi-format social and display ad campaigns.

03

Interactive music & visual apps

Upload a song or audio stem and build a full-blown interactive WebAudio music app with a 60fps FFT spectrum visualizer, waveform scrubber, 3-band EQ, and playable synth pads.
