Capabilities

What Oto does

Timeline-synchronized Foley

Aligns transient sound impacts, footsteps, collisions, and cloth rustle to video frames down to millisecond precision.

Acoustic room impulse modeling

Automatically matches early reflections, reverberation decay, and high-frequency absorption to physical scene geometry.

Layered multi-stem ambience

Generates isolated multichannel bed tracks: wind, urban traffic, foliage movement, room tone, and distant atmospheric cues.

Dynamic mechanical and impact sound

Produces complex physical audio: vehicle engine load, firearm transients, glass stress fractures, and metallic resonance.

In production

Where Oto fits

Post-production

Automated Foley track generation

Game audio

Procedural audio asset banks

Generate endless non-repeating variations of impact sounds, footsteps on varying surfaces, and weapon transients.

Virtual worlds

Spatial 3D audio environments

Bake realistic acoustic audio stems into virtual venues, architectural walkthroughs, and simulations.
