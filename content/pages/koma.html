Capabilities

What Koma does

Temporal character continuity

Maintains identity, costume attributes, facial geometry, and lighting coherence across multi-cut cinematic sequences.

Shot-level director control

Parametric camera choreography: focal length, pan/tilt rate, crane elevation, and dynamic optical depth-of-field.

High-fidelity motion synthesis

Continuous flow-matching spatial-temporal diffusion eliminating morphing, temporal flicker, and anatomy degeneration.

Keyframe-guided scene trajectories

Interpolates smoothly between user-defined storyboard keyframes with physics-aware object kinematics.
