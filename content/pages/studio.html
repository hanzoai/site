What is Hanzo Studio

Production-grade AI film direction and node-graph VFX

Where Hanzo Design (/design) builds everyday visuals, decks, docs, and interactive apps inside your workspace, Hanzo Studio (studio.hanzo.ai) is a full cinema production suite built for directors, filmmakers, and VFX teams.

01

Cinema Camera & Lens Rig

Control physical 6-DoF camera trajectories (Dolly In/Out, Crane Rise, Vertigo Zolly, Crash Zoom, Whip Pan, 360° Orbit, Bullet-Time), 14mm–135mm prime & anamorphic 2.39:1 lenses, T-Stop aperture bokeh, and volumetric lighting.

02

Character & World Consistency Lock

Lock Actor ID identity embeddings, wardrobe, reference stills, and Koe voice profiles so your cast and environment stay frame-accurate across every angle and cut.

03

Multi-Shot Timeline & Node Graph

Chain shots directly with last-frame conditioning (ConcatVideo), layer Oto foley and Uta musical scores on a multi-track timeline, or drop into the full Comfy-compatible Node Graph pipeline.

Capabilities

Built for cinema, VFX, and multi-shot storytelling

Koma 2.0 Camera Motion Control

Direct exact camera paths, focal lengths (14mm to 135mm Anamorphic), shutter angles, and depth-of-field rack focus before rendering.

Persistent Actor & Set Vault

Upload character sheets or lock generated seeds once; reference @KAI-01 or @SORA-02 in any shot to preserve face, costume, and voice.

Frame-Chained Storyboard Sequencer

Cut multi-shot scenes on a real timeline with automatic boundary frame matching, lip-sync dialogue, and OpenTimelineIO / MP4 master export.

Visual Node-Graph Pipeline

Switch to the node-graph canvas at any time to wire custom checkpoints, ControlNets, relighting passes, and Python nodes.

Synchronized Foley, Voice & Score

Every shot automatically pairs Koma video with Oto spatial foley, Koe multi-speaker lip-sync dialogue, and Uta stems.

Dedicated Cloud GPU Render Farm

Queue 4K anamorphic multi-shot renders across distributed Hanzo Cloud GPUs with organization-wide asset libraries and IAM access control.
